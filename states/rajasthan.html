41 Top Tourist Places & Temples in Rajasthan

Bharat Unfolded · Rajasthan

Discover Rajasthan

Explore royal forts, desert landscapes, sacred temples, lakes and living heritage across the Land of Kings.

Rajasthan brings together the hill forts of the Aravallis, the golden architecture of the Thar Desert, the lakeside palaces of Mewar and some of India's most visited pilgrimage sites. This guide covers 41 destinations across Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur, Jaisalmer, Pushkar, Ajmer, Mount Abu, Chittorgarh, Rajsamand, Sawai Madhopur, Sikar, Churu, Bikaner and Kota. Timings and ticket rates are indicative: government revisions, private management, seasonal schedules and special events can change what visitors pay or when a site admits visitors.
