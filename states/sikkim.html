25 Top Tourist Places in Sikkim

Explore sacred glacial lakes, high mountain passes, Buddhist monasteries, flower-filled valleys, historic villages and Himalayan viewpoints across Sikkim.

Bharat Unfolded · Sikkim

Sikkim

From the high-altitude lakes of the north to the tea gardens and monasteries of the south, discover the Himalayan beauty and living heritage of Sikkim.

Places to Explore

25 destinations · Visitor timings · Entry and permit information

DESTINATION 01 · SIKKIM

Tsomgo Lake / Changu Lake

Gangtok · East Sikkim

A sacred glacial lake at about 12,313 ft (3,753 m), around 40 km east of Gangtok. Its colour changes with the seasons and it often freezes in winter. Visitors come for snowy mountain views, yak rides and the small lakeside Shiva temple.

Opening time8:00 AM–3:00 PM; permit and weather dependentEntry / permitLake viewing is free; Protected Area Permit is mandatory through a registered operator. Yak rides cost extra.
DESTINATION 02 · SIKKIM

Nathula Pass

Gangtok · East Sikkim

At about 14,140 ft (4,310 m) on the historic Old Silk Route, Nathula offers dramatic Himalayan views and a glimpse of the India–China border posts. Access can close at short notice because of weather or security conditions.

Opening timeUsually Wed–Sun, daytime only; Army clearance appliesEntry / permitSpecial permit through an authorised agency; Indian citizens only. Permit and vehicle charges vary.
DESTINATION 03 · SIKKIM

Baba Harbhajan Singh Memorial Temple

Between Tsomgo Lake and Nathula · East Sikkim

This Army-maintained shrine honours Captain Harbhajan Singh, remembered by soldiers and travellers as the guardian of the Nathula border region. It is commonly visited on the Tsomgo–Nathula route.

Opening timeUsually 8:00 AM–4:00 PM; route clearance appliesEntry / permitNo separate entry fee is generally charged; access is covered by the route permit where applicable.
DESTINATION 04 · SIKKIM

Rumtek Monastery

Near Gangtok · East Sikkim

Also known as Dharma Chakra Centre, Rumtek is a major seat of the Karma Kagyu school of Tibetan Buddhism. Its richly painted halls, golden details and monastic campus sit on a green hillside overlooking the valley.

Opening timeUsually 9:00 AM–5:00 PM; check local accessEntry / permitSmall entry charge may apply; carry photo ID and follow monastery rules.
DESTINATION 05 · SIKKIM

MG Marg

Gangtok · East Sikkim

Mahatma Gandhi Marg is Gangtok’s pedestrian heart: a clean, vehicle-free boulevard lined with cafes, restaurants, shops, benches and flowerbeds. It is especially lively in the evening and during city festivals.

Opening timeStreet open all day; most shops around 9:00 AM–9:30 PMEntry / permitPedestrian access is free; shopping and dining are paid.
DESTINATION 06 · SIKKIM

Namgyal Institute of Tibetology & Do Drul Chorten

Deorali · Gangtok, East Sikkim

The institute preserves Tibetan, Lepcha and Sanskrit manuscripts, thangkas and ritual objects. Nearby Do Drul Chorten is a white stupa surrounded by 108 prayer wheels, making this a rewarding cultural stop.

Opening timeInstitute: Mon–Sat, 10:00 AM–4:00 PM; Chorten: daytimeEntry / permitInstitute museum ticket applies; Do Drul Chorten is generally free. Confirm current rates locally.
DESTINATION 07 · SIKKIM

Tashi View Point, Ganesh Tok & Hanuman Tok

Gangtok ridge · East Sikkim

These hilltop viewpoints look across Gangtok and, on clear mornings, towards Kanchenjunga and other Himalayan peaks. Ganesh Tok and Hanuman Tok also have small temples and peaceful viewing areas.

Opening timeGenerally 5:00 AM–7:00 PM; timings varyEntry / permitViewpoints and temple access are generally free; parking or local facilities may charge.
DESTINATION 08 · SIKKIM

Banjhakri Falls & Energy Park

Near Gangtok · East Sikkim

A forested park built around a waterfall, with landscaped paths, bridges, gazebos and sculptures inspired by the Jhakri shamanic tradition. It is a relaxed half-day stop close to Gangtok.

Opening time8:00 AM–6:00 PM; confirm locallyEntry / permitIndicative entry: about ₹50 for adults and ₹20 for children; rates may change.
DESTINATION 09 · SIKKIM

Gurudongmar Lake

Near Lachen · North Sikkim

At roughly 17,800 ft (5,430 m), Gurudongmar is one of the world’s highest lakes and is sacred to Buddhists, Sikhs and Hindus. Its turquoise water, stark mountain bowl and prayer flags are unforgettable, but the altitude is severe.

Opening timeEarly morning to around noon; permit, weather and road dependentEntry / permitNo regular lake ticket; North Sikkim Protected Area Permit and authorised transport are required.
DESTINATION 10 · SIKKIM

Yumthang Valley & Zero Point / Yumesamdong

Near Lachung · North Sikkim

Yumthang is an alpine valley at around 11,800 ft, known for rhododendrons and primulas in spring and snow in winter. Zero Point, higher at about 15,300 ft, is a dramatic snowfield area beyond the regular road network.

Opening timeUsually 6:00 AM–3:00 PM; permit and weather dependentEntry / permitNorth Sikkim permit required; Zero Point usually involves an additional local vehicle charge.
DESTINATION 11 · SIKKIM

Lachen & Lachung Villages

Mangan district · North Sikkim

These mountain villages sit in separate river valleys and serve as overnight bases for excursions to Gurudongmar Lake and Yumthang Valley. Wooden homes, orchards, waterfalls and high Himalayan scenery shape the experience.

Opening timeVillage access all day; travel in daylight recommendedEntry / permitNo general village entry ticket; protected-area permits and registered travel arrangements apply.
DESTINATION 12 · SIKKIM

Pelling Skywalk & Chenrezig Statue

Sangachoeling · Pelling, West Sikkim

The glass skywalk faces a 137-foot golden statue of Chenrezig (Avalokiteshvara). The transparent deck gives a dramatic view into the valley, with the Himalayan range visible in clear weather.

Opening time8:00 AM–5:00 PM; weather dependentEntry / permitIndicative entry around ₹50–₹100 per person; confirm current ticket and shoe-cover rules.
DESTINATION 13 · SIKKIM

Pemayangtse Monastery & Rabdentse Ruins

Pelling · West Sikkim

Pemayangtse, founded in 1705, is one of Sikkim’s oldest important monasteries, known for its intricate wooden model of Guru Rinpoche’s celestial palace. A forest trail leads to the remains of Rabdentse, the kingdom’s former capital.

Opening timeMonastery: usually 9:00 AM–5:00 PM; ruins: daylightEntry / permitMonastery entry charge may apply; Rabdentse follows ASI access rules. Verify locally.
DESTINATION 14 · SIKKIM

Khecheopalri Lake

Near Pelling · West Sikkim

A sacred lake surrounded by dense forest, revered by both Buddhists and Hindus. Local belief says birds remove leaves that fall onto the water, keeping its surface clear. A wooden walkway and prayer flags lead visitors towards the shore.

Opening time6:00 AM–6:00 PM; local access may varyEntry / permitSmall local entry or maintenance fee may apply; confirm at the entrance.
DESTINATION 15 · SIKKIM

Yuksom & Dubdi Monastery / Coronation Throne

Yuksom · West Sikkim

Yuksom was Sikkim’s first capital, where the first Chogyal was consecrated in 1642 at the Norbugang Coronation Throne. A forest walk climbs to Dubdi Monastery, founded in 1701 and regarded as Sikkim’s oldest surviving monastery.

Opening timeNorbugang: generally 7:00 AM–5:00 PM; Dubdi: daylightEntry / permitGenerally free; trekking and national-park permits may be needed for longer routes.
DESTINATION 16 · SIKKIM

Kanchenjunga Falls & Rimbi Waterfalls

Pelling–Yuksom road · West Sikkim

Kanchenjunga Falls is a powerful perennial cascade dropping over rocky tiers, while Rimbi Waterfalls is a smaller roadside stop beside the Rimbi River. Both are popular photo breaks on the Pelling–Yuksom drive.

Opening timeUsually 7:00 AM–5:30 PM; local conditions applyEntry / permitSmall local entry fees may apply; zipline or other activities cost extra where available.
DESTINATION 17 · SIKKIM

Singshore Bridge & Dentam Valley

Near Uttarey · West Sikkim

Singshore is a high suspension bridge spanning a deep green gorge, with views of forested slopes and waterfalls. Nearby Dentam Valley is known for quiet rural scenery, terraced fields and the Singalila range.

Opening timeDaylight visits recommended, roughly 7:00 AM–5:30 PMEntry / permitBridge and valley sightseeing are generally free; local transport costs extra.
DESTINATION 18 · SIKKIM

Buddha Park / Tathagata Tsal

Ravangla · South Sikkim

This landscaped spiritual complex is centred on a 130-foot copper-gilded statue of the Buddha, set against the Himalayan foothills. Gardens, prayer wheels and walking paths make it a peaceful stop in Ravangla.

Opening time9:00 AM–5:00 PM; confirm seasonal timingsEntry / permitIndicative entry around ₹50 per person; battery-car rides may cost extra.
DESTINATION 19 · SIKKIM

Char Dham / Siddheshwar Dham

Solophok Hill · Namchi, South Sikkim

A large pilgrimage complex on Solophok Hill, dominated by an 87-foot Shiva statue and replicas of Badrinath, Jagannath, Dwarka and Rameswaram, alongside representations of the twelve Jyotirlingas.

Opening time8:00 AM–6:00 PM; check local noticesEntry / permitIndicative entry around ₹50 per person; parking charges may apply.
DESTINATION 20 · SIKKIM

Samdruptse Hill & Guru Padmasambhava Statue

Near Namchi · South Sikkim

Samdruptse, meaning Wish-Fulfilling Hill, is crowned by a 135-foot statue of Guru Padmasambhava. Pine forests, prayer flags and broad views towards Kanchenjunga make it a striking hilltop visit.

Opening time8:00 AM–5:30 PM; approximateEntry / permitIndicative entry around ₹30–₹50 per person; rates can change.
DESTINATION 21 · SIKKIM

Temi Tea Garden

Near Ravangla / Namchi · South Sikkim

Established in 1969, Temi is Sikkim’s only tea estate. Its terraced green slopes are especially scenic, with cherry blossoms often appearing around November. Visitors can enjoy viewpoints and, when arranged, factory visits and tastings.

Opening timeViewpoints: daylight; factory tours usually on working daysEntry / permitGarden viewpoints generally free; guided factory visits and tastings may be chargeable.
DESTINATION 22 · SIKKIM

Ralong Monastery

Near Ravangla · South Sikkim

Ralong Monastery, also known as Palchen Choeling, is a major Kagyu Buddhist monastery with colourful murals, a large prayer hall and mountain surroundings. It hosts masked-dance festivals including Pang Lhabsol and Kagyed.

Opening timeUsually 8:00 AM–5:00 PM; respect prayer timesEntry / permitEntry is generally free; donations are welcome.
DESTINATION 23 · SIKKIM

Zuluk & Silk Route Loops

Rongli area · East Sikkim

Zuluk is a small high-altitude settlement on the historic Silk Route, famous for a dramatic series of 32 hairpin bends. Thambi View Point, Lungthung, Tukla and Kupup (Elephant Lake) are popular stops when road and permit conditions allow.

Opening timeDaylight travel only; road access can close during snow or bad weatherEntry / permitNo regular sightseeing ticket; Protected Area Permit required for the route.
DESTINATION 24 · SIKKIM

Aritar / Lampokhari Lake

Aritar · East Sikkim

Lampokhari is a calm natural lake set among green hills and often described as boot-shaped. Visitors enjoy the lakeside path, boating, gardens and nearby Aritar Monastery and Dak Bungalow.

Opening time7:00 AM–5:30 PM; approximateEntry / permitSmall lakeside fee may apply; boating is charged separately.
DESTINATION 25 · SIKKIM

Khangchendzonga National Park

West & North Sikkim

This vast protected Himalayan landscape includes glaciers, alpine meadows, forests and sacred mountain scenery around Mount Khangchendzonga. It is known for rare wildlife such as the red panda, snow leopard, Himalayan tahr and musk deer, and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Opening timeTrekking is seasonal; spring and autumn are common windows, subject to permitsEntry / permitPark and trekking permits, guide/porter and camping charges apply; check current rules before travel.
