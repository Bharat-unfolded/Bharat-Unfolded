31 Top Tourist Places & Temples in Uttar Pradesh

Bharat Unfolded · Uttar Pradesh

Discover Uttar Pradesh

Explore the Taj Mahal, sacred cities, royal architecture, Buddhist heritage, river ghats, wildlife and the living traditions of India's most populous state.

Uttar Pradesh brings together the Mughal monuments of Agra, the sacred riverfront of Varanasi and Prayagraj, the Ramayana landscape of Ayodhya and Chitrakoot, the Krishna pilgrimage towns of Mathura and Vrindavan, and the Nawabi heritage of Lucknow. It is also home to Buddhist sites such as Sarnath and Kushinagar, historic forts, protected forests and rich craft, food and festival traditions. This guide features 31 destinations across the state. Timings, access rules and ticket prices are indicative and can change with season, festivals, security arrangements and local administration.
