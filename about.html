About Us

Bharat Unfolded is an independent discovery project about India's states, Union Territories, destinations, culture, food, heritage and stories.

Our story

Why Bharat Unfolded?

India has 28 states and 8 Union Territories, each with its own places, languages, traditions, food and history. This website aims to bring useful introductory information about these regions together in one easy-to-explore place.

What you can explore

  • State and Union Territory guides
  • Famous places, monuments and destinations
  • Culture, festivals, food and local traditions
  • Travel ideas and published news or updates
