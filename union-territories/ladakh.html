Ladakh

From the turquoise lakes of Changthang and the cold deserts of Nubra to ancient monasteries, high mountain passes and the remote valleys of Zanskar — discover Ladakh, the Land of High Passes.

EXPLORE LADAKH
30 destinations across Leh, Nubra, Changthang, Kargil and Zanskar, with destination-specific photographs and visitor notes. Ladakh is a high-altitude region: spend time acclimatising, stay hydrated, and check current road, weather, permit and security advisories before travel. Opening access and local rules can change by season.
