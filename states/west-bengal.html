25 Top Tourist Places in West Bengal

From Himalayan hill towns and Dooars forests to Kolkata’s heritage, terracotta temples, mangroves and the Bay of Bengal coast, explore the many landscapes of West Bengal.

Bharat Unfolded · West Bengal

West Bengal

Where the Himalayas meet the mangroves, and centuries of art, faith, food and history shape every journey.

Places to Explore

25 destinations · Visitor timings · Entry information

DESTINATION 01 · WEST BENGAL

Darjeeling Himalayan Town & Mall Road

Darjeeling · North Bengal

Darjeeling sits in the eastern Himalaya and is known for mountain views, colonial-era streets, tea culture and its busy Mall Road. On clear mornings, viewpoints around the town can offer views towards Kanchenjunga. Allow time for local markets, cafés and heritage walks.

Opening timeOpen public town areas daily; shops and attractions keep their own hours. Early morning is best for mountain views.Entry / ticketNo town-entry fee; individual attractions, transport and guided walks charge separately.
DESTINATION 02 · WEST BENGAL

Darjeeling Himalayan Railway / Toy Train

Darjeeling – Ghum route

The UNESCO-listed Darjeeling Himalayan Railway is a narrow-gauge mountain railway famous for its loops, steep gradients and historic steam locomotives. Selected joyride services connect Darjeeling with Ghum and pass the Batasia Loop; book the train and date in advance.

Opening timeTrain timings vary by season and service. Check the current IRCTC or railway timetable before travel.Entry / ticketTicket prices depend on the route, class and locomotive; reserve through authorised railway channels.
DESTINATION 03 · WEST BENGAL

Tiger Hill Sunrise Viewpoint

Ghoom · Darjeeling district

Tiger Hill is a high viewpoint above Darjeeling, popular for sunrise views of the Himalayan range. On exceptionally clear mornings, the distant peaks can include Kanchenjunga and, under favourable conditions, Everest. Arrive well before sunrise and expect cold weather.

Opening timeAccess is usually arranged before dawn; timings depend on sunrise and local road conditions.Entry / ticketA local vehicle or parking charge may apply; confirm current rates with the operator.
DESTINATION 04 · WEST BENGAL

Batasia Loop & War Memorial

Ghoom · Darjeeling district

Batasia Loop is a spiral railway track built to help the toy train negotiate the steep descent from Ghum. A landscaped garden surrounds the loop, with a memorial honouring Gorkha soldiers. The site offers a memorable view of Darjeeling and the hills on clear days.

Opening timeGenerally visited during daylight; railway operations and garden access may affect the experience.Entry / ticketA small garden or entry fee may be charged; verify locally. Toy-train tickets are separate.
DESTINATION 05 · WEST BENGAL

Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park

Darjeeling town

This high-altitude zoo is known for conservation work involving Himalayan species such as the red panda and snow leopard. It forms part of the Himalayan Zoological Park complex near the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute and is a popular family visit.

Opening timeUsually open during daytime and closed one day each week; confirm the current weekly closure and hours before visiting.Entry / ticketPaid entry; camera and other charges may apply. Check the current rate at the official ticket counter.
DESTINATION 06 · WEST BENGAL

Kalimpong & Deolo Hill

Kalimpong district · North Bengal

Kalimpong is a hill town with monasteries, flower nurseries, heritage homes and views over the Teesta valley. Deolo Hill, one of the town’s highest viewpoints, has landscaped grounds and broad views of the surrounding hills when the weather is clear.

Opening timePublic viewpoints are best visited in daylight; individual gardens and attractions may have separate hours.Entry / ticketTown access is free; parks, activities and transport may have separate charges.
DESTINATION 07 · WEST BENGAL

Lava & Neora Valley Gateway

Kalimpong district

Lava is a quiet mountain settlement surrounded by forests and misty ridges. It is a key gateway for visitors heading towards Neora Valley National Park, an important eastern Himalayan forest landscape with rich birdlife and biodiversity. Forest treks require planning and local guidance.

Opening timeVillage visits are possible in daylight. National park entry and trekking are subject to forest permissions, weather and current access rules.Entry / ticketForest permits, guide and vehicle fees may apply; confirm with the forest department before entering protected areas.
DESTINATION 08 · WEST BENGAL

Samsing & Suntalekhola

Dooars · Kalimpong district

Samsing is a scenic foothill village surrounded by tea gardens, forested slopes and streams. Nearby Suntalekhola is known for its suspension bridge, forest setting and access to nature trails. The area is a relaxing base for exploring the eastern Dooars.

Opening timeDay visits are best planned in daylight; forest trail access may be restricted by the forest department.Entry / ticketGenerally no town-entry fee; forest permits, stays, parking and guided activities may cost extra.
DESTINATION 09 · WEST BENGAL

Gorumara National Park

Jalpaiguri district · Dooars

Gorumara is a protected forest landscape in the Dooars, known for Indian one-horned rhinoceros, elephants, gaur and varied birdlife. Officially managed safari routes and watchtowers provide regulated ways to experience the forest; sightings are never guaranteed.

Opening timeSafari slots and park access vary by season, weather and forest-department rules. Confirm the current schedule before travelling.Entry / ticketEntry, safari, vehicle and guide charges apply and can change; book only through authorised channels.
DESTINATION 10 · WEST BENGAL

Jaldapara National Park

Alipurduar district · Dooars

Jaldapara protects grassland and riverine forest habitat and is especially associated with the Indian one-horned rhinoceros. Elephant grass, wetlands and river corridors also support elephants, deer and many birds. Safari access is regulated and depends on forest conditions.

Opening timeSafari timings and seasonal opening dates are set by the forest department; check current notices before visiting.Entry / ticketPaid entry and safari fees apply; rates, availability and permitted safari types may change.
DESTINATION 11 · WEST BENGAL

Buxa Tiger Reserve & Buxa Fort

Alipurduar district · Dooars

Buxa Tiger Reserve protects forests along the Bhutan border and forms part of a larger eastern Himalayan landscape. The historic Buxa Fort, reached by a forest trek from the Santalabari side, is associated with the freedom movement and once served as a detention site during British rule.

Opening timeTrek access is daylight-only and can be affected by monsoon, forest permissions and local conditions.Entry / ticketForest entry or guide permissions may be required; confirm current access and charges locally.
DESTINATION 12 · WEST BENGAL

Sundarbans National Park & Tiger Reserve

South 24 Parganas · Sundarbans

The Sundarbans is a UNESCO World Heritage mangrove landscape formed by the Ganges-Brahmaputra delta. Its tidal creeks and islands are habitat for the Royal Bengal tiger, estuarine crocodile, spotted deer and many birds. Visitors explore through regulated boat routes and forest watchtowers; wildlife sightings are not guaranteed.

Opening timeBoat trips are generally arranged as authorised day or multi-day tours. Access, routes and timings depend on tides and forest-department rules.Entry / ticketForest permits, boat, guide and package fees apply. Use registered operators and verify current rules before booking.
DESTINATION 13 · WEST BENGAL

Sajnekhali Watch Tower & Interpretation Centre

Sundarbans · South 24 Parganas

Sajnekhali is an important visitor point in the Sundarbans, with a watchtower, interpretation facilities and mangrove surroundings. It is often included in authorised boat itineraries and offers opportunities to learn about the protected ecosystem and its wildlife.

Opening timeAccess is by permitted boat itinerary; opening and visiting hours follow forest-department schedules and tide conditions.Entry / ticketIncluded or charged as part of a forest permit or tour package; confirm what the operator's quoted price covers.
DESTINATION 14 · WEST BENGAL

Victoria Memorial

Kolkata

Built in memory of Queen Victoria and opened in 1921, the white-marble Victoria Memorial is one of Kolkata’s best-known landmarks. The museum galleries cover colonial-era history, while the landscaped grounds provide a striking setting for the monument. Check gallery schedules separately from garden access.

Opening timeMuseum galleries and gardens have separate schedules and may close on specific days; check the official notice before visiting.Entry / ticketMuseum and garden tickets may be separate, with different rates for Indian and overseas visitors.
DESTINATION 15 · WEST BENGAL

Howrah Bridge & Hooghly Riverfront

Kolkata – Howrah

The cantilever Howrah Bridge, officially Rabindra Setu, is a major engineering landmark connecting Kolkata and Howrah across the Hooghly River. It is best appreciated from the riverfront and nearby ghats; the bridge itself is a working road bridge rather than a pedestrian sightseeing deck.

Opening timeThe bridge is in daily use; river cruises and nearby attractions keep their own schedules.Entry / ticketViewing from public riverfront areas is generally free; cruises and organised tours are paid.
DESTINATION 16 · WEST BENGAL

Dakshineswar Kali Temple

Dakshineswar · North Kolkata

Set beside the Hooghly River, Dakshineswar Kali Temple was established in the 19th century by Rani Rashmoni and is closely associated with Sri Ramakrishna. Its main shrine and surrounding Shiva temples form an important pilgrimage complex.

Opening timeDarshan hours change between morning and afternoon sessions and may vary on festivals; check the temple’s current notice.Entry / ticketEntry is generally free; special rituals, offerings and services have separate costs.
DESTINATION 17 · WEST BENGAL

Indian Museum

Kolkata · Chowringhee

Founded in 1814, the Indian Museum is one of the oldest and largest museums in Asia. Its collections span archaeology, anthropology, art, geology, zoology and natural history, including sculpture, fossils and the famous Egyptian mummy.

Opening timeUsually open during daytime, with a weekly closure and possible holiday changes; verify the current gallery schedule.Entry / ticketPaid entry; rates for Indian and foreign visitors and camera permissions may differ.
DESTINATION 18 · WEST BENGAL

Belur Math

Howrah · on the Hooghly

Belur Math is the headquarters of the Ramakrishna Math and Ramakrishna Mission, founded by Swami Vivekananda. The temple complex combines architectural influences and stands on a calm stretch of the Hooghly riverbank. Visitors are asked to maintain silence and follow photography rules.

Opening timeTemple and campus timings have separate morning and evening sessions and can change seasonally; check the official visitor schedule.Entry / ticketEntry is free; donations are voluntary.
DESTINATION 19 · WEST BENGAL

Bishnupur Terracotta Temples

Bankura district

Bishnupur is celebrated for its Malla-era brick and terracotta temples, including Rasmancha, Jor Bangla, Shyam Rai and Madan Mohan temples. Their carved panels depict scenes from epics, daily life and devotional traditions, making the town a major heritage destination.

Opening timeIndividual monuments have separate visiting hours and may be closed on certain days; plan a daylight heritage walk.Entry / ticketSome protected monuments charge entry; rates and combined tickets may vary. Check ASI or local ticket counters.
DESTINATION 20 · WEST BENGAL

Hazarduari Palace & Museum

Murshidabad

Hazarduari Palace, built in the 19th century for Nawab Nazim Humayun Jah, is a landmark of Murshidabad. Its museum displays paintings, armour, furniture, manuscripts and objects associated with the Nawabi period. Nearby landmarks include Nizamat Imambara and the Katra Mosque.

Opening timeMuseum opening days and hours can change for maintenance and holidays; check the current Archaeological Survey of India notice.Entry / ticketPaid museum entry; Indian and foreign visitor rates and photography rules may differ.
DESTINATION 21 · WEST BENGAL

Cooch Behar Rajbari

Cooch Behar town · North Bengal

Cooch Behar Palace, also known as Rajbari, is a grand heritage building associated with the Koch royal family. Its European-influenced design, central dome and long arcaded corridors make it one of North Bengal’s most distinctive historic monuments.

Opening timeMuseum access is during notified daytime hours; confirm weekly closure and current timings before visiting.Entry / ticketMuseum entry may be ticketed; verify current rates and photography rules at the site.
DESTINATION 22 · WEST BENGAL

Santiniketan & Visva-Bharati

Birbhum district · Bolpur

Santiniketan grew around the educational and cultural vision of Rabindranath Tagore. Visva-Bharati’s campus, prayer spaces, art traditions and nearby institutions make it an important centre of learning and Bengali culture. Some campus areas are open to visitors while academic spaces may have restricted access.

Opening timeCampus access and museum schedules vary by institution, term dates and holidays; check current visitor arrangements before travelling.Entry / ticketPublic campus areas may be accessible without charge; museums and special guided visits may require tickets.
DESTINATION 23 · WEST BENGAL

Tarapith Temple

Tarapith · Birbhum district

Tarapith is a major Shakti pilgrimage centre associated with the goddess Tara. The temple and nearby cremation-ground traditions draw devotees throughout the year, especially during religious occasions. Visitors should follow temple customs and be mindful of crowds.

Opening timeDarshan runs in daily sessions; exact timings and queue arrangements can change on festivals and special days.Entry / ticketGeneral darshan is usually free; offerings, rituals and special services have separate charges.
DESTINATION 24 · WEST BENGAL

Digha Sea Beach

Purba Medinipur district · Bay of Bengal

Digha is one of West Bengal’s best-known coastal getaways, with a long shoreline, seafood stalls and a busy promenade. New Digha is popular for family stays, while nearby areas offer quieter stretches of coast. Follow local safety flags and avoid swimming during rough sea conditions.

Opening timeBeach access is open throughout the day; sunrise and sunset are popular times. Follow lifeguard and local authority instructions.Entry / ticketPublic beach access is generally free; parking, rides and water activities may have separate charges.
DESTINATION 25 · WEST BENGAL

Mandarmani Beach

Purba Medinipur district · Bay of Bengal

Mandarmani is a coastal destination known for its wide sandy shoreline and resort stays. Visitors come for a quiet beach break, seafood and views across the Bay of Bengal. Vehicle access on the beach may be restricted for safety and environmental reasons; follow local instructions.

Opening timeOpen beach access is generally available in daylight; check tide and local safety guidance before walking far along the shore.Entry / ticketNo general beach ticket; accommodation, parking and activities are charged separately.
DESTINATION 26 · WEST BENGAL

Gangasagar & Kapil Muni Ashram

Sagar Island · South 24 Parganas

Gangasagar is a major pilgrimage destination where the Ganges meets the Bay of Bengal. Kapil Muni Ashram and the nearby beach attract pilgrims throughout the year, with particularly large gatherings during the annual Makar Sankranti fair. Ferry and local transport form part of the journey to the island.

Opening timeOpen year-round; ferry and launch schedules depend on tides, weather and seasonal crowd management. Festival arrangements are notified separately.Entry / ticketNo general site-entry ticket; ferry, bus, local transfers and accommodation are paid separately.
DESTINATION 27 · WEST BENGAL

Ayodhya Hills & Purulia

Purulia district · Chota Nagpur Plateau

The Ayodhya Hills are a scenic plateau landscape of forest, rocky outcrops, dams and waterfalls in Purulia. Visitors use the area for nature walks, photography and seasonal trips to sites such as Bamni Falls and Marble Lake. Water levels and road conditions vary sharply with the season.

Opening timeDaylight visits are recommended; waterfall access and local roads can be affected by monsoon conditions.Entry / ticketMost viewpoints have no general ticket; parking, boating, guides and selected facilities may charge separately.
