8 Top Tourist Places in Andaman and Nicobar Islands

INDIA · ANDAMAN AND NICOBAR ISLANDS · 8 DESTINATIONS

Discover the islands beyond the blue.

From white-sand beaches and coral reefs to historic landmarks, mangrove trails and quiet island escapes.

THE EMERALD ISLANDS OF INDIA
The Andaman and Nicobar Islands bring together tropical beaches, marine life, colonial-era history and forest landscapes. This guide introduces eight well-known places across the archipelago. Ferry operations, permits, activity schedules and entry charges can change with weather and local rules; confirm details with the relevant authority or operator before travelling.

01 · SWARAJ DWEEP

Radhanagar Beach

Radhanagar Beach on Swaraj Dweep (Havelock Island) is celebrated for its long curve of white sand, clear turquoise surf and lush tropical forest backdrop. Visitors value its calm swimming zones, sunset views and clean beachfront layout.

LocationSwaraj Dweep (Havelock Island), South AndamanKnown forWhite sand, swimming and sunset views
Opening timeCommonly visited during daytime and sunset hours. Confirm local beach and lifeguard timings on arrival.
Ticket pricePublic beach entry is generally free. Water sports, rentals and local transfers, if available, cost extra.

Sea conditions and safety flags can change. Follow lifeguard instructions and posted swimming-zone notices.

02 · SRI VIJAYA PURAM

Cellular Jail

Located at Atlanta Point in Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair), Cellular Jail is a historic former colonial prison preserved as a national memorial and museum. Its radiating cell wings and exhibitions tell the story of India's freedom struggle.

LocationAtlanta Point, Sri Vijaya PuramKnown forNational memorial, museum and freedom struggle history
Opening timeCommonly listed Tue–Sun: 9:00 AM–12:00 PM and 2:00 PM–5:00 PM; closed Monday. Verify current hours. Light-and-sound show timings are separate.
Ticket pricePaid monument/museum admission applies; Indian visitor entry has commonly been around ₹30. Light-and-sound show tickets cost extra. Confirm current rates.

Show schedules and ticket prices may change; check the official counter before visiting.

03 · NEAR SRI VIJAYA PURAM

Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose Island (Ross Island)

Set just off the coast of Sri Vijaya Puram and reached by short boat transfers, this island features atmospheric historic ruins overtaken by banyan roots, coastal views and island wildlife. It makes an easy half-day excursion.

LocationNear Sri Vijaya Puram, South AndamanKnown forColonial ruins, banyan roots and coastal walks
Opening timeDaytime visitor access follows ferry schedules. Confirm daily boat departures and return timings at the jetty.
Ticket pricePaid boat transfers and nominal island entry/conservation fees may apply. Verify current tariffs at the ticket counter.

Boat services depend on weather, sea conditions and operator schedules.

04 · NORTH BAY ISLAND

North Bay Underwater Activity

Situated near Sri Vijaya Puram and known for its coastal scenery and lighthouse backdrop, North Bay is popular for guided marine activities and boat outings. Options can include glass-bottom boat rides, diving and snorkelling.

LocationNorth Bay Island, near Sri Vijaya PuramKnown forGlass-bottom boats, scuba diving and snorkelling
Opening timeOperators commonly run daytime trips, often around 8:00 AM–4:30 PM. Weather and sea conditions govern departures and water activities.
Ticket priceBoat transfers and each marine activity are paid separately according to operator and package.

Use licensed operators and check sea conditions before booking activities.

05 · SWARAJ DWEEP

Elephant Beach

Located on Swaraj Dweep (Havelock Island), Elephant Beach is known for coral reef snorkelling, swimming and water-sports rides. Visitors enjoy its shallow reef areas and access by boat or forest trail.

LocationSwaraj Dweep (Havelock Island), South AndamanKnown forCoral reefs, snorkelling and water sports
Opening timeBoat and beach visits commonly operate in the morning and early afternoon, often around 7:30 AM–3:30 PM. Confirm current local timings.
Ticket priceBoat rides, snorkelling gear and water sports such as speedboat or banana-boat rides are charged separately.

Access may close or change due to weather, forest rules or sea conditions.

06 · SHAHEED DWEEP

Shaheed Dweep (Neil Island)

A quieter counterpart to the larger islands, Shaheed Dweep offers relaxed coastal scenery, coral coves and a natural rock formation. It is appreciated for its slower pace, sunset spots and island-hopping ferry connections.

LocationShaheed Dweep (Neil Island), South AndamanKnown forQuiet beaches, natural rock bridge and sunsets
Opening timeThe island is an open destination; individual beaches, ferries and local sights follow their own daytime schedules.
Ticket priceInter-island ferry tickets, local transport and activities are paid separately. Check ferry classes and seasonal schedules.

Plan ferry connections in advance, especially during rough-sea periods.

07 · BARATANG ISLAND

Limestone Cave Baratang

Located in Baratang along the Andaman Trunk Road corridor, the limestone caves feature natural formations reached after creek crossings and a walk through mangrove-lined terrain. The guided journey is part of the experience.

LocationBaratang Island, Middle AndamanKnown forNatural limestone formations and mangrove route
Opening timeDay excursions commonly run during daylight, often around 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Convoy and boat timing rules apply; verify locally.
Ticket pricePermit or entry fees, boat transfers and local vehicle/guide charges may apply. Confirm current rates at the entry and boat points.

Follow the forest department's route and convoy instructions; do not leave designated paths.

08 · WANDOOR

Mahatma Gandhi Marine National Park

Near Wandoor in South Andaman, this marine national park protects island groups, coral habitats and coastal marine life. Island excursions and visitor access are subject to forest department rules and weather.

LocationWandoor, South AndamanKnown forCoral habitats, marine biodiversity and island excursions
Opening timeVisitor facilities are commonly listed Tue–Sun, around 9:00 AM–4:00 PM; closed Monday. Confirm current access and island schedules.
Ticket pricePark permits/entry fees may apply, with separate charges for boats, cameras and guided island visits. Verify current tariffs.

Some islands may be closed to visitors to protect wildlife and coral ecosystems.
