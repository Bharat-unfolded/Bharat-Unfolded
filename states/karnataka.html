01 · MYSURU, KARNATAKA

Mysuru Palace

Mysuru Palace, also called Amba Vilas Palace, is one of South India’s best-known royal residences. Its Indo-Saracenic design blends Hindu, Muslim, Rajput and Gothic details, with ornate halls, carved doors and stained-glass ceilings. The palace is especially popular during Dasara, when its façade is illuminated for the festival. Plan ahead.

02 · VIJAYANAGARA, KARNATAKA

Hampi Group of Monuments

Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire and is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Its landscape contains temple streets, royal enclosures, market remains, giant boulders and stone monuments. Explore the Virupaksha Temple, Vittala complex and riverside ruins respectfully; distances between monuments can be considerable. Plan your visit ahead.

03 · BAGALKOT, KARNATAKA

Badami Cave Temples

Carved into red sandstone cliffs above Agastya Lake, Badami’s four principal cave temples date mainly from the 6th and 7th centuries. Their sculpted panels portray Hindu and Jain themes, including Vishnu, Shiva and the cosmic dance. The climb also rewards visitors with views over the lake and town. Plan ahead.

04 · BAGALKOT, KARNATAKA

Pattadakal Group of Monuments

Pattadakal preserves early Chalukya temples where northern Nagara and southern Dravida architectural styles meet. The UNESCO-listed complex includes Virupaksha, Mallikarjuna and Papanatha temples, with detailed carvings depicting stories from Hindu epics. Visit in the morning for softer light and fewer crowds. The village setting makes it easy to combine Aihole with nearby Badami and Pattadakal on a heritage circuit. Plan ahead.

06 · HASSAN, KARNATAKA

Chennakeshava Temple, Belur

Commissioned by Hoysala king Vishnuvardhana in the 12th century, Chennakeshava Temple is celebrated for its star-shaped platform, polished pillars and intricate soapstone sculptures. Carved friezes show dancers, musicians, animals and epic scenes. The active temple remains a place of worship, so modest clothing and quiet conduct are appropriate. Plan ahead.

13 · MANDYA, KARNATAKA

Shivanasamudra Falls

The Kaveri River divides around a rocky island at Shivanasamudra to form Gaganachukki and Bharachukki waterfalls. During and after the monsoon, the cascades become powerful, creating dramatic viewpoints across the gorge. Follow marked paths, avoid slippery edges and never enter restricted water zones when the river is in spate. Plan.
