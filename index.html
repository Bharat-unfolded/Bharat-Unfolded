About the project

A little curiosity.
A lot of Bharat.

Anjaan Bharat is an independent discovery project created to bring lesser-known places, regional culture and meaningful stories of India into one welcoming space. Our aim is to encourage curious travel, celebrate diversity and help readers appreciate the many voices and experiences that shape our country.

We believe every region has something worth sharing — from a small-town food tradition to an ancient monument, from a local artist to a landscape far from the usual tourist trail.
