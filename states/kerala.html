40 Top Tourist Places & Temples in Kerala

Explore Kerala’s ancient temples, beaches, backwaters, wildlife reserves, hill stations, waterfalls and historic towns.

Places to Explore

40 destinations · Visitor timings · Entry information

DESTINATION 01 · KERALA

Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple

Thiruvananthapuram

Located in East Fort, Thiruvananthapuram, Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple is an ancient Dravidian- and Kerala-style Vaishnava shrine dedicated to Lord Vishnu reclining on the serpent Anantha. Renowned for its towering seven-tiered gopuram and golden-plated pillars, it enforces strict traditional dress codes for entry.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 3:30 AM–12:00 PM and 5:00 PM–8:30 PMEntry / ticketGeneral darshan free; special quick-darshan passes around ₹250–₹500.
DESTINATION 02 · KERALA

Kovalam Beach & Vizhinjam Lighthouse

Thiruvananthapuram

Stretching along the Arabian Sea about 16 km south of Thiruvananthapuram, Kovalam comprises three crescent beaches—Lighthouse, Hawah and Samudra—famed for calm surf and seaside cafés. The red-and-white Vizhinjam Lighthouse offers panoramic coastal views.

Opening timeKovalam Beach open 24 hours; lighthouse Tue–Sun 10:00 AM–12:45 PM and 2:00 PM–5:45 PM; closed MondayEntry / ticketBeach free; lighthouse ₹20 Indian adults, ₹10 children, ₹50 foreign visitors.
DESTINATION 03 · KERALA

Varkala Cliff & Papanasam Beach

Thiruvananthapuram

About 50 km north of Thiruvananthapuram, Varkala is known for red laterite cliffs rising beside the Arabian Sea. Below the cliffside promenade of cafés and yoga centres lies Papanasam Beach, revered for its sacred mineral springs.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; daytime and sunset are bestEntry / ticketFree.
DESTINATION 04 · KERALA

Ponmudi Hill Station

Thiruvananthapuram

Nestled at 1,100 metres in the Western Ghats, about 55 km from Thiruvananthapuram, Ponmudi is a misty hill retreat approached by 22 scenic hairpin bends. Tea estates, cardamom plantations, shola forests and Golden Valley viewpoints shape the landscape.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM–5:00 PMEntry / ticketForest checkpost around ₹40–₹50 per adult, plus vehicle parking.
DESTINATION 05 · KERALA

Poovar Island & Backwaters

Thiruvananthapuram

Near Kerala’s southern tip, Poovar lies where the Neyyar River, estuary backwaters and Arabian Sea meet at a golden sandbar. Boat trips pass through mangrove tunnels and coconut-lined waterways, with chances to see coastal birds and sunset views.

Opening timeBoating Mon–Sun: 7:00 AM–6:00 PMEntry / ticketSandbar access free; motorboat cruises around ₹1,000–₹2,500 per boat for 1–2 hours.
DESTINATION 06 · KERALA

Napier Museum & Attukal Bhagavathy Temple

Thiruvananthapuram

Napier Museum is a 19th-century Indo-Saracenic landmark housing Chola bronzes, temple chariots and ivory carvings. Nearby, Attukal Bhagavathy Temple is known as the “Sabarimala of Women” and for its large Attukal Pongala festival.

Opening timeNapier Museum Tue–Sun 10:00 AM–4:45 PM (Wed 1:00 PM–4:45 PM), closed Monday; Attukal Temple 4:30 AM–12:30 PM and 5:00 PM–8:30 PMEntry / ticketMuseum around ₹20–₹30 adults; temple free.
DESTINATION 07 · KERALA

Sabarimala Sree Dharma Sastha Temple

Pathanamthitta

Set on a forested hill inside the Periyar Tiger Reserve belt, Sabarimala is a major annual pilgrimage centre dedicated to Lord Ayyappa. Pilgrims reach the shrine by a steep forest trek from Pamba, traditionally after a 41-day vratham.

Opening timeSeasonal: Mandala-Makaravilakku (mid-November to mid-January), Vishu and the first five days of Malayalam months; usual darshan windows around 3:00 AM–11:00 PMEntry / ticketGeneral darshan free; mandatory online Virtual-Q registration.
DESTINATION 08 · KERALA

Gavi Eco-Tourism

Pathanamthitta

Gavi is an evergreen forest and cardamom-plantation eco-tourism destination in the Periyar Tiger Reserve belt, managed by the Kerala Forest Development Corporation. Visitors can enjoy jeep safaris, boating on Gavi Lake, canopy walks and wildlife sightings.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM–4:00 PM; day-visit entry closes around 11:00 AM at Angamoozhy checkpostEntry / ticketForest entry around ₹25–₹50 per person plus vehicle fee; KFDC packages around ₹1,500–₹2,000 per person.
DESTINATION 09 · KERALA

Jatayu Earth’s Center / Jatayu Nature Park

Chadayamangalam, Kollam

Jatayu Earth’s Center crowns a granite hill at Chadayamangalam with a monumental bird sculpture inspired by the Ramayana. Visitors can take a cable car to the summit for hilltop views, walkways and an audiovisual museum.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 10:00 AM–5:30 PMEntry / ticketCable car and hilltop entry around ₹450–₹550 per person; adventure packages extra.
DESTINATION 10 · KERALA

Ashtamudi Lake & Munroe Island

Kollam

Ashtamudi Lake is known for its eight palm-fringed channels, houseboat cruises and Chinese fishing nets. At the meeting of the lake and Kallada River, Munroe Island’s quiet islets are explored by narrow hand-rowed country canoes.

Opening timeLake and island accessible 24 hours; canoe and houseboat tours around 6:30 AM–6:00 PMEntry / ticketIsland access free; two-hour canoe tours around ₹500–₹800 per person.
DESTINATION 11 · KERALA

Alappuzha / Alleppey Backwaters & Houseboats

Alappuzha

Alappuzha is the heart of Kerala’s lagoons, canals and Vembanad Lake. Visitors cruise past paddy fields, coconut groves and village life aboard traditional thatched kettuvallam houseboats or government passenger ferries.

Opening timeDaytime shikkara/ferry cruises 6:00 AM–6:00 PM; overnight houseboats check in 12:00 PM and check out 9:00 AMEntry / ticketSWTD ferry ₹10–₹30; shikkara ₹800–₹1,500/hour; overnight houseboats from around ₹7,500–₹15,000+.
DESTINATION 12 · KERALA

Marari Beach & Alappuzha Beach

Alappuzha

Alappuzha Beach has a historic sea pier, lighthouse and sunset promenade. About 11 km north, Marari Beach is a quieter fishing-village shoreline fringed by palms and eco-resorts.

Opening timeBoth beaches open 24 hours; Alappuzha Lighthouse Tue–Sun 9:00 AM–12:45 PM and 2:00 PM–5:45 PM, closed MondayEntry / ticketBeaches free; lighthouse around ₹20 for Indian adults.
DESTINATION 13 · KERALA

Mannarasala Sree Nagaraja Temple

Haripad, Alappuzha

Mannarasala is a serpent-worship centre set within a sacred forest grove near Haripad. Its shaded pathways are lined with thousands of stone snake images, and the temple is traditionally officiated by a senior female priestess, the Valiya Amma.

Opening timeMon–Sat 5:00 AM–12:00 PM and 5:30 PM–7:30 PM; Sun 5:00 AM–1:00 PM and 5:30 PM–7:30 PMEntry / ticketGeneral darshan free.
DESTINATION 14 · KERALA

Ambalappuzha Sree Krishna Swamy Temple

Alappuzha

Built in traditional Kerala temple architecture, Ambalappuzha Sree Krishna Temple houses Krishna as Parthasarathi holding a whip and conch. It is renowned for its milk-and-rice pudding offering, Ambalappuzha Palpayasam, and its connection with Ottamthullal.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 3:00 AM–12:00 PM and 5:00 PM–8:00 PMEntry / ticketGeneral darshan free; Palpayasam tokens purchased separately.
DESTINATION 15 · KERALA

Kumarakom Backwaters & Bird Sanctuary

Kottayam

Kumarakom sits along the eastern shore of Vembanad Lake and is known for mangrove-lined islands and backwater cruises. Its bird sanctuary is a haven for migratory waterbirds, egrets, darters and kingfishers.

Opening timeBird Sanctuary Mon–Sun: 6:00 AM–5:00 PM; early morning 6:30 AM–9:00 AM is best for birdwatchingEntry / ticketAround ₹100 Indian adults and ₹250 foreign visitors; boating extra.
DESTINATION 16 · KERALA

Ettumanoor Mahadeva Temple & Vaikom Mahadeva Temple

Kottayam

Ettumanoor Mahadeva Temple is celebrated for its murals, Ezharaponnana golden elephants and Koothambalam. Vaikom Mahadeva Temple, near Vembanad Lake, is one of Kerala’s old Shiva shrines and is associated with the historic Vaikom Satyagraha.

Opening timeBoth temples: Mon–Sun 4:00 AM–12:00 PM and 5:00 PM–8:00 PMEntry / ticketGeneral darshan at both temples free.
DESTINATION 17 · KERALA

Ilaveezhapoonchira & Illikkal Kallu

Kottayam / Idukki

Ilaveezhapoonchira is a high valley ringed by three hillocks and known for sunrise views. Nearby Illikkal Kallu is a striking monolithic rock peak reached by a steep jeep track and cliffside walk above cloud-filled valleys.

Opening timeBoth sites Mon–Sun: 6:00 AM–6:00 PM; daylight visits recommendedEntry / ticketEco-tourism entry/parking around ₹20–₹40 per person; jeep transfers around ₹100–₹150 per person.
DESTINATION 18 · KERALA

Munnar Tea Gardens & Eravikulam National Park

Idukki

Munnar sits amid rolling tea plantations in the Western Ghats. Nearby Eravikulam National Park protects the endangered Nilgiri tahr and offers views towards Anamudi, South India’s highest peak.

Opening timeEravikulam National Park Mon–Sun: 7:30 AM–4:00 PM; closed during February–March for Nilgiri Tahr calvingEntry / ticketMunnar viewpoints free; park entry with forest minibus around ₹200 Indian adults, ₹150 children and ₹500 foreign visitors.
DESTINATION 19 · KERALA

Mattupetty Dam, Top Station & Echo Point

Munnar, Idukki

Along the Munnar–Top Station road, Mattupetty Dam offers boating amid forested hills, while Echo Point is known for its lakeside setting. Top Station near the Kerala–Tamil Nadu border gives wide views over the Western Ghats and Theni Valley.

Opening timeMattupetty Dam and Echo Point 9:00 AM–5:00 PM; Top Station 6:00 AM–6:00 PMEntry / ticketDam and Echo Point around ₹10–₹20; boating ₹500–₹800 per boat; Top Station around ₹25–₹30 per person.
DESTINATION 20 · KERALA

Thekkady & Periyar National Park / Wildlife Sanctuary

Idukki

Periyar Tiger Reserve in Thekkady centres on Periyar Lake, where boat safaris pass forested shores and may reveal elephants, gaur, sambar and birds. The surrounding area also offers spice-plantation visits, bamboo rafting and cultural shows.

Opening timePark entry 6:00 AM–5:00 PM; boat cruises at 7:30 AM, 9:30 AM, 11:15 AM, 1:45 PM and 3:30 PMEntry / ticketPark entry around ₹45 Indian / ₹500 foreign; boat safari around ₹255 per Indian adult.
DESTINATION 21 · KERALA

Vagamon Meadows & Pine Forest

Idukki / Kottayam

Vagamon is a highland retreat known for rolling green meadows, pine forest, Kurisumala hill and Thangalpara. It is popular for quiet walks, paragliding and its glass bridge.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM–6:30 PMEntry / ticketMeadows and Pine Forest around ₹10–₹20 per person; Glass Bridge around ₹250–₹500 extra.
DESTINATION 22 · KERALA

Idukki Arch Dam & Ramakkalmedu

Idukki

Idukki Arch Dam spans a granite gorge between Kuravan and Kurathi hills. Ramakkalmedu, near the Tamil Nadu border, is a wind-swept hilltop known for the Kuravan-Kurathi statues and views over the plains.

Opening timeDam select holiday/weekend windows 9:30 AM–5:00 PM (verify status); Ramakkalmedu 6:00 AM–6:00 PMEntry / ticketDam around ₹25–₹50 when open; Ramakkalmedu around ₹20–₹30 per person.
DESTINATION 23 · KERALA

Fort Kochi, Chinese Fishing Nets & Mattancherry Palace

Kochi, Ernakulam

Fort Kochi’s waterfront is lined with iconic Chinese fishing nets operated at high tide. Nearby, 16th-century Mattancherry Palace houses Kerala mural paintings depicting the Ramayana and portraits of the Kochi royal family.

Opening timeFort Kochi and nets open 24 hours; Mattancherry Palace Sat–Thu 9:30 AM–1:00 PM and 2:00 PM–4:45 PM, closed FridayEntry / ticketFort Kochi free; palace ₹5 Indian citizens and ₹100 foreign visitors.
DESTINATION 24 · KERALA

Paradesi Synagogue & St. Francis Church

Fort Kochi, Ernakulam

The 1568 Paradesi Synagogue in Jew Town is known for hand-painted porcelain floor tiles and glass chandeliers. St. Francis Church in Fort Kochi dates to 1503 and is associated with the burial of Vasco da Gama.

Opening timeSynagogue Sun–Thu 10:00 AM–1:00 PM and 3:00 PM–5:00 PM; Fri 10:00 AM–1:00 PM; closed Saturday. St. Francis Church Mon–Sat 9:00 AM–5:00 PM; Sunday afternoon only.Entry / ticketSynagogue ₹10 per person; St. Francis Church free.
DESTINATION 25 · KERALA

Cherai Beach & Marine Drive

Kochi, Ernakulam

Cherai Beach on Vypin Island is a long sandy shoreline where backwaters meet the Arabian Sea, with occasional dolphin sightings. Marine Drive in central Ernakulam is a waterfront promenade overlooking Kochi harbour, with boat rides and sunset views.

Opening timeBoth locations open 24 hoursEntry / ticketFree; Marine Drive harbour cruises around ₹150–₹300.
DESTINATION 26 · KERALA

Chottanikkara Bhagavathy Temple

Ernakulam

Chottanikkara Devi Temple is a major Shakti shrine where Bhagavathy is worshipped in three forms through the day: Saraswati in the morning, Lakshmi at noon and Durga in the evening. Devotees also visit the lower Keezhukavu Temple.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 4:00 AM–12:00 PM and 4:00 PM–8:30 PMEntry / ticketGeneral darshan free; special quick-darshan passes around ₹50–₹100.
DESTINATION 27 · KERALA

Athirappilly Waterfalls & Vazhachal Waterfalls

Thrissur

Athirappilly is Kerala’s largest waterfall, dropping around 80 feet from the Chalakudy River amid the Sholayar forest range. A short distance upstream, Vazhachal Waterfalls runs over rocky slopes framed by rainforest.

Opening timeBoth waterfalls Mon–Sun: 8:00 AM–5:00 PM; entry closes around 4:30 PMEntry / ticketCombined entry around ₹50 Indian adults, ₹10 children and ₹200 foreign visitors.
DESTINATION 28 · KERALA

Guruvayur Sree Krishna Temple

Thrissur

Guruvayur Temple is one of South India’s major Krishna pilgrimage centres, revered as Bhooloka Vaikuntha. Its traditional Kerala architecture, rituals, temple arts and elephant processions draw pilgrims throughout the year; Hindu-only entry and traditional dress rules apply.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 3:00 AM–12:30 PM and 4:30 PM–9:15 PMEntry / ticketGeneral darshan free; special Nei Vilakku quick darshan around ₹1,000 per person.
DESTINATION 29 · KERALA

Vadakkunnathan Temple

Thrissur

Set within a large walled compound in central Thrissur, Vadakkunnathan Temple is a classic Kerala-style Shiva shrine with timber-roofed gateways, ancient murals and a Koothambalam theatre. The surrounding Thekkinkadu Maidan hosts Thrissur Pooram.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 4:00 AM–10:30 AM and 5:00 PM–8:30 PMEntry / ticketGeneral darshan free; traditional attire required.
DESTINATION 30 · KERALA

Silent Valley National Park

Palakkad

Silent Valley National Park protects a major tract of tropical evergreen rainforest in the Nilgiri Hills. Its wildlife includes the endangered lion-tailed macaque, Nilgiri langur and many other species. Visitors explore through regulated forest safaris from Mukkali to Sairandhri.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM–1:00 PM; day-visit jeep departures from MukkaliEntry / ticketAround ₹50 per Indian visitor plus mandatory 4x4 safari jeep rental around ₹1,800–₹2,200 per jeep and guide fee.
DESTINATION 31 · KERALA

Malampuzha Dam & Gardens

Palakkad

At the foothills of the Western Ghats, Malampuzha Dam is Kerala’s largest reservoir. The gardens include a hanging bridge, aquarium and the giant Yakshi sculpture; an aerial passenger ropeway passes above the gardens and reservoir.

Opening timeGardens Mon–Sun 9:00 AM–8:00 PM; ropeway 10:00 AM–1:00 PM and 2:30 PM–6:00 PMEntry / ticketGarden entry around ₹30 adults and ₹10 children; ropeway around ₹120 round-trip.
DESTINATION 32 · KERALA

Nelliyampathy Hills

Palakkad

Nelliyampathy rises into the Sahyadri ranges and is reached by a scenic ghat road overlooking Pothundi Dam. Tea, coffee, cardamom and orange estates cover its slopes, with Seethargundu Viewpoint, Kesavanpara and forest trails among its highlights.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; daytime travel 6:00 AM–6:00 PM recommendedEntry / ticketHill station and viewpoints free; around ₹20 at some eco-spots.
DESTINATION 33 · KERALA

Kappad Beach & Kozhikode Beach

Kozhikode

Kappad Beach is the historic shore associated with Vasco da Gama’s landing in 1498 and is marked by a stone monument. Kozhikode Beach has sea piers, a lighthouse and lively evening food stalls serving Malabar snacks.

Opening timeKozhikode Beach open 24 hours; Kappad Beach 6:00 AM–7:00 PMEntry / ticketKozhikode Beach free; Kappad maintenance fee around ₹20–₹25 per adult.
DESTINATION 34 · KERALA

Beypore Beach & Thusharagiri Waterfalls

Kozhikode

Beypore Beach lies at the mouth of the Chaliyar River and is known for its long stone Pulimuttu breakwater and traditional Uru wooden dhow shipbuilding. Inland, Thusharagiri Waterfalls features forest cascades and short nature trails.

Opening timeBeypore Beach open 24 hours; Thusharagiri Waterfalls 8:00 AM–5:00 PMEntry / ticketBeypore Beach free; Thusharagiri entry around ₹30–₹50 per person.
DESTINATION 35 · KERALA

Edakkal Caves

Wayanad

Edakkal Caves are natural rock shelters on Ambukuthi Mala, reached by a steep uphill walk. Their walls preserve rare Neolithic petroglyphs depicting human figures, animals and ancient symbols, making the site an important prehistoric location.

Opening timeTue–Sun: 8:30 AM–4:00 PM; closed MondayEntry / ticketAround ₹50 Indian adults, ₹30 children and ₹100 foreign visitors; camera fees extra.
DESTINATION 36 · KERALA

Banasura Sagar Dam & Pookode Lake

Wayanad

Banasura Sagar Dam creates a broad reservoir dotted with forested islands and offers boating and other activities. Nearby Pookode Lake is a natural freshwater lake surrounded by evergreen woods, with pedal-boating trails.

Opening timeBoth sites Mon–Sun: 9:00 AM–5:00 PMEntry / ticketEntry around ₹40 per adult at each site; Banasura speedboat around ₹900/boat; Pookode pedal boats around ₹150–₹250.
DESTINATION 37 · KERALA

Chembra Peak & Soochipara Waterfalls

Wayanad

Chembra Peak is Wayanad’s highest summit and is known for the heart-shaped Hridhayathadakam lake along its guided trekking route. Nearby Soochipara Falls is a three-tiered cascade dropping into a rocky pool surrounded by forest.

Opening timeChembra trek 7:00 AM–2:00 PM with limited slots; Soochipara Falls 8:00 AM–4:00 PMEntry / ticketChembra trek package around ₹750–₹900 for a group of up to 10 Indians; Soochipara entry around ₹80 per person.
DESTINATION 38 · KERALA

Thirunelli Temple & Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary / Muthanga

Wayanad

Thirunelli Temple is a granite-pillared Vishnu shrine in a forested Brahmagiri valley, known for ancestral rituals by the Papanasini stream. Muthanga Wildlife Sanctuary, part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve, offers jeep safaris through elephant and gaur habitat.

Opening timeThirunelli Temple 5:30 AM–12:00 PM and 5:30 PM–8:00 PM; Muthanga safaris 7:00 AM–10:00 AM and 3:00 PM–5:00 PMEntry / ticketTemple free; Muthanga around ₹110 Indian / ₹300 foreign plus jeep rental around ₹1,500–₹2,000 per vehicle.
DESTINATION 39 · KERALA

Muzhappilangad Drive-in Beach & St. Angelo Fort

Kannur

Muzhappilangad is a long drive-in beach where firm sand allows vehicles to travel beside the waves. In Kannur, St. Angelo Fort is a laterite sea fortress built in 1505 and overlooking Mapilla Bay.

Opening timeMuzhappilangad Beach open 24 hours; St. Angelo Fort 8:00 AM–6:00 PMEntry / ticketBeach pedestrian access free; vehicle toll around ₹50–₹100; fort entry free.
DESTINATION 40 · KERALA

Bekal Fort & Bekal Beach

Kasaragod

Bekal Fort spreads across a rocky headland above the Arabian Sea and is Kerala’s largest well-preserved fort. Its laterite ramparts, observation tower and sea-facing walkways overlook Bekal Beach, known for sunset views beside the fort walls.

Opening timeBekal Fort 8:00 AM–5:30 PM; Bekal Beach Park 8:00 AM–8:00 PMEntry / ticketFort around ₹25 Indian citizens and ₹300 foreign visitors; beach park around ₹10–₹20.
