DESTINATION 01 · Srinagar

Dal Lake & Shikara Rides

Dal Lake is Kashmir’s iconic lake, known for cedar-wood houseboats, floating markets, Char Chinar and shikara rides beneath the Zabarwan mountains.

OPENING24 hours; shikaras roughly 6 AM–8 PM; floating market around 5–7 AM.
ENTRY / COSTLakefront free; shikara rides roughly ₹700–₹1,200+ per hour.

Persian-Mughal terraced gardens with fountains, water channels, lawns and ancient Chinar trees along Dal Lake.

OPENINGUsually 9 AM–7 PM (Apr–Oct); 10 AM–5 PM (Nov–Mar).
ENTRY / COSTAbout ₹24–₹50 Indian adults per garden; rates vary.
