Travel & Guide: Know Before You Go

Simple, practical tips for visitors travelling within India. Local rules and conditions can vary, so check details for your destination.

💳 Keep your money safe

  • Carry a little cash in small notes for local transport, markets and places where digital payments are unavailable.
  • Use UPI through a trusted banking app where accepted; never share your UPI PIN or OTP. You do not need to enter a UPI PIN to receive money.
  • Keep cards and cash in separate places. Use bank ATMs in well-lit, secure locations and shield your PIN.
  • Decline help from strangers at ATMs. Check the amount and merchant name before approving a payment.

🛡️ Avoid common scams

  • Agree on taxi, auto-rickshaw or guide prices before the journey; use trusted app-based services or official prepaid counters where available.
  • Be cautious of unsolicited “closed attraction”, “special permit” or “cheap tour” claims. Confirm with the official venue or tourism desk.
  • Do not hand your phone, passport, card or luggage to an unknown person. Avoid deals that pressure you to pay immediately.
  • Book hotels, trains and buses through recognised providers and check cancellation terms before paying.

🎒 Before leaving

  • Keep your original ID/passport secure and carry a protected copy; share your itinerary with someone you trust.
  • Check weather, local advisories, entry rules, opening hours and transport options for the exact destination.
  • Pack medicines you normally need, a basic first-aid kit, water and weather-appropriate clothing.
  • For remote areas, plan mobile connectivity, transport back and a backup payment method.

🚦 Stay safe on the move

  • Use marked crossings, wear a helmet on two-wheelers and always use seat belts where provided.
  • Choose licensed operators for adventure activities; check safety equipment and weather before starting.
  • Respect queues, photography restrictions, religious customs and local dress guidance at places of worship.
  • India's pan-India emergency response number is 112. Use it for urgent police, fire or medical assistance.

Travel note: This is general guidance, not a substitute for destination-specific government advisories. Never share OTPs, passwords, card PINs or UPI PINs with anyone.
