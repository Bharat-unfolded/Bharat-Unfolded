The destination

Discover India

India brings together a remarkable range of landscapes, languages, food, architecture and living traditions. Travel north for mountain scenery, head west for desert forts and coastal towns, explore the forests and backwaters of the south, or follow the rivers and historic cities of the east. Every region has its own pace and stories. This guide is a starting point for planning a trip around the places, experiences and seasons that interest you.

A balanced travel note

What to keep in mind

Why explore India?

  • Different landscapes and climates across regions
  • Deep and varied cultural and architectural heritage
  • Regional food traditions and local markets
  • Travel options ranging from short breaks to long journeys

Plan with care

  • Popular sites can be crowded during holidays
  • Summer heat and monsoon rain affect some routes
  • Travel times between regions can be long
  • Check official advisories, opening hours and local transport
