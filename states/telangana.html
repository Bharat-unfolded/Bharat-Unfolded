Telangana

Explore Hyderabad’s Deccan landmarks, Kakatiya-era stone temples, pilgrimage centres, film-city attractions and forest waterfalls across Telangana.

Places to Explore

Heritage, architecture, spirituality, entertainment and nature.

8 DESTINATIONS
01
Deccan landmark · Hyderabad

Charminar

Built in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, Charminar is Hyderabad’s best-known monument. Its four grand arches and minarets overlook the lively lanes of Laad Bazaar and the old city.

Opening time
Generally 9:30 AM–5:30 PM daily; confirm last entry locally.
Entry / ticket
Indicative ASI ticket: around ₹25 for Indian visitors and ₹300 for foreign visitors; check the current counter rate.
02
Fort & history · Hyderabad

Golconda Fort

First developed as a Kakatiya fort and later expanded by the Qutb Shahi rulers, Golconda is famous for its granite ramparts, gateways, royal remains and remarkable acoustic design.

Opening time
Generally 9:00 AM–5:30 PM; sound-and-light shows run separately in the evening.
Entry / ticket
Indicative ASI ticket: around ₹25 Indian and ₹300 foreign visitors; show tickets extra. Confirm current rates.
03
Film & entertainment · Ranga Reddy

Ramoji Film City

A large film-studio and entertainment complex with guided studio tours, film sets, themed gardens, live shows and family attractions on the outskirts of Hyderabad.

Opening time
9:00 AM–5:30 PM daily; tour schedules and special events may vary.
Entry / ticket
Official Telangana Tourism listing shows adult day-tour entry from about ₹1,450; child and premium packages differ. Check the official booking page before visiting.
04
Kakatiya temple · Hanamkonda, Warangal

Thousand Pillar Temple

Built in 1163 under the Kakatiya ruler Rudra Deva, this star-shaped trikuta shrine honours Shiva, Vishnu and Surya. Its carved pillars, Nandi pavilion and stepped temple tank are key features.

Opening time
Commonly 5:30 AM–8:00 PM; ritual timings may affect access.
Entry / ticket
General entry and darshan are generally free; confirm any special charges locally.
05
UNESCO heritage · Palampet, Mulugu

Ramappa Temple

Also known as the Rudreshwara Temple, this 13th-century Kakatiya masterpiece is celebrated for detailed stone carvings, sculpted brackets and its distinctive lightweight porous bricks. It became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2021.

Opening time
Generally 6:00 AM–6:00 PM; check locally for changes.
Entry / ticket
General entry is currently listed as free by Telangana tourism information; confirm at the site.
06
Pilgrimage · Yadagirigutta, Yadadri Bhuvanagiri

Yadadri Lakshmi Narasimha Temple

The hilltop shrine dedicated to Lord Lakshmi Narasimha has been extensively rebuilt in carved stone, with impressive gopurams, courtyards and organised darshan facilities.

Opening time
Darshan schedules commonly begin around 4:00 AM and continue into the evening; special sevas have fixed slots. Check the temple’s daily schedule.
Entry / ticket
General darshan is free; paid special darshan and seva tickets are available.
07
Waterfall · Mulugu district

Bogatha Waterfall

A broad cascade surrounded by forest and rocky pools near Wazeedu, often called the “Niagara of Telangana”. It is especially impressive during and after the monsoon, when access and water levels should be checked carefully.

Opening time
Daytime visits are recommended; local access is often around 9:00 AM–5:00 PM but may change with weather.
Entry / ticket
Local entry, parking or eco-tourism charges may apply; confirm at the gate.
08
Waterfall · Nirmal district

Kuntala Waterfall

Set among the Sahyadri hills near Neradigonda, Kuntala is one of Telangana’s tallest waterfalls, dropping about 45 metres. A stepped path leads down through woodland to the base.

Opening time
Commonly around 9:30 AM–5:00 PM; daylight visits are safest and access can close during heavy rain.
Entry / ticket
Local forest, entry or parking fees may apply; verify at the entrance.
