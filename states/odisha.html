35 Top Tourist Places in Odisha

Discover Odisha's ancient temples, golden beaches, Buddhist heritage, mangrove forests, wildlife sanctuaries, waterfalls and hill destinations.

Odisha

From the sacred coast of Puri and the stone chariot of Konark to Chilika's wetlands and the green highlands of Koraput, explore Odisha's many landscapes.

Places to Explore

35 destinations · Visitor timings · Entry information

DESTINATION 01 · ODISHA

Shree Jagannath Temple

Puri

Located in the coastal holy city of Puri, the 12th-century Shree Jagannath Temple is one of India's four sacred Char Dham pilgrimage sites, dedicated to Lord Jagannath alongside Balabhadra and Subhadra. Built in classic Kalinga architecture with a towering 65-meter shikhara, it is globally famous for its daily Mahaprasad and the grand annual Rath Yatra chariot festival.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 5:00 AM – 11:00 PM (brief midday ritual breaks; entry restricted strictly to practicing Hindus).
Entry / ticketGeneral darshan is free; phones, cameras and leather items must be deposited outside.
DESTINATION 02 · ODISHA

Puri Beach / Golden Beach & Swargadwar

Puri

Stretching along the Bay of Bengal, Golden Beach is a clean, Blue Flag-certified coastal stretch known for golden sands, sunrise views, sand art and lifeguard-supervised swimming. Nearby Swargadwar Beach has a busy waterfront promenade, seafood stalls, local markets and evening pilgrims.

Opening timeGolden Beach: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM; Swargadwar: open 24 hours.
Entry / ticketSwargadwar is free; Golden Beach Blue Flag zone: around ₹20–₹30 per person.
DESTINATION 03 · ODISHA

Konark Sun Temple

Konark · Puri

Built in the 13th century by King Narasimhadeva I of the Eastern Ganga dynasty, this UNESCO World Heritage Site was conceived as a colossal stone chariot of the Sun God Surya. Its 24 carved stone wheels, seven horses and sculptural friezes are hallmarks of Kalinga architecture.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 8:00 PM; evening illumination after dusk.
Entry / ticketAround ₹40 for Indian citizens and ₹600 for foreign visitors; children under 15 are free (ASI ticket).
DESTINATION 04 · ODISHA

Chandrabhaga Beach & Ramachandi Temple

Konark · Puri

Chandrabhaga Beach lies about 3 km east of the Konark Sun Temple and is known for sunrise views, its Blue Flag beach zone and the annual Magha Saptami fair. A few kilometres south, Ramachandi Temple stands near the Kushabhadra River mouth amid casuarina groves and golden dunes.

Opening timeChandrabhaga: 6:00 AM – 6:30 PM; Ramachandi Temple: 6:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
Entry / ticketChandrabhaga Blue Flag beach: around ₹20; Ramachandi Temple is free.
DESTINATION 05 · ODISHA

Chilika Lake & Satapada / Mangalajodi

Puri / Khordha / Ganjam

Chilika Lake is Asia's largest brackish-water lagoon and a Ramsar Wetland. Boat trips from Satapada look for Irrawaddy dolphins and Sea Mouth Island, while Mangalajodi is known for winter migratory birdwatching, especially from November to February.

Opening timeBoating: 6:30 AM – 5:00 PM; best birding season November–February.
Entry / ticketShoreline viewing is free; Satapada boat trips around ₹1,200–₹2,500 per boat (up to 6–8 people); Mangalajodi boats around ₹800–₹1,200 per boat.
DESTINATION 06 · ODISHA

Kalijai Temple

Chilika Lake · Khordha / Ganjam

Perched on a small rocky island inside Chilika Lake near Balugaon, Kalijai Temple is dedicated to Goddess Kalijai, revered by local fishermen and boatmen as the lagoon's protective deity. The island shrine is reached by motorboat and hosts a major annual fair on Makar Sankranti.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM, aligned with daytime boat operations.
Entry / ticketTemple entry is free; shared boat transfers from Barkul/Balugaon around ₹150–₹250 per person.
DESTINATION 07 · ODISHA

Raghurajpur Heritage Crafts Village

Puri

About 14 km from Puri, Raghurajpur is a living heritage crafts village celebrated for Pattachitra cloth paintings and Gotipua dance. Homes function as open studios for palm-leaf engravings, tussar paintings, stone carvings and papier-mâché masks.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
Entry / ticketVillage entry and artist studio visits are free; artworks are available for purchase.
DESTINATION 08 · ODISHA

Lingaraj Temple

Bhubaneswar

The 11th-century Lingaraj Temple is the largest and most prominent Shiva temple in Bhubaneswar. Built in Kalinga style, its 55-meter tower, sculpted sandstone walls, Jagamohana, Natamandira and many subsidiary shrines form a major temple complex.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 5:00 AM – 9:00 PM, with a midday ritual break; inner entry restricted to Hindus. Non-Hindus can view from an elevated platform outside.
Entry / ticketGeneral darshan is free.
DESTINATION 09 · ODISHA

Mukteswara Temple & Rajarani Temple

Bhubaneswar

Mukteswara Temple, from the 10th century, is called the Gem of Odishan Architecture and is known for its sculpted arched Torana gateway. Nearby Rajarani Temple, from the 11th century, is built from warm red-and-gold sandstone and features graceful nayika carvings.

Opening timeBoth monuments: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
Entry / ticketMukteswara is free; Rajarani: ₹25 for Indian citizens and ₹300 for foreign visitors (ASI ticket).
DESTINATION 10 · ODISHA

Udayagiri and Khandagiri Caves

Bhubaneswar

These adjacent hills contain 1st-century BCE rock-cut Jain monastic caves associated with King Kharavela. Udayagiri has 18 caves, including the two-storeyed Rani Gumpha and Hathigumpha inscription; Khandagiri has 15 caves and hilltop city views.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
Entry / ticketAround ₹25 for Indian citizens and ₹300 for foreign visitors (ASI ticket).
DESTINATION 11 · ODISHA

Dhauli Shanti Stupa

Bhubaneswar

The white Peace Pagoda stands on Dhauli Hill above the Daya River, about 8 km south of Bhubaneswar. It marks the landscape associated with the Kalinga War of 261 BCE and Ashoka's embrace of Buddhism. Ancient Ashokan Rock Edicts are also preserved here.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM; light-and-sound show usually around 6:30 PM – 7:30 PM.
Entry / ticketStupa and rock edict entry is free; light-and-sound show around ₹25–₹50 per person.
DESTINATION 12 · ODISHA

Nandankanan Zoological Park

Bhubaneswar

Set in the Chandaka forest belt beside Kanjia Lake, Nandankanan Zoological Park is known as the world's first captive breeding centre for white tigers and Indian pangolins. The park offers forest enclosures, animal safaris, a ropeway and boating.

Opening timeTue–Sun: 7:30 AM – 5:30 PM (April–September) / 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (October–March); closed Mondays.
Entry / ticketAround ₹50 for Indian adults (₹10 children) and ₹100 for foreign visitors; safaris, battery cars and boating cost extra.
DESTINATION 13 · ODISHA

Odisha State Museum & Tribal Museum

Bhubaneswar

Odisha State Museum holds palm-leaf manuscripts, sculptures, coins and traditional arms. The Museum of Tribal Arts & Artifacts showcases dwellings, costumes, jewellery and musical traditions from Odisha's tribal communities.

Opening timeBoth museums: Tue–Sun: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM; closed Mondays and public holidays.
Entry / ticketOdisha State Museum: around ₹20 Indian / ₹100 foreign; Tribal Museum entry is free.
DESTINATION 14 · ODISHA

Chausathi Yogini Temple

Hirapur · Bhubaneswar

This rare 9th-century roofless circular temple is one of India's few surviving Chausathi Yogini shrines. Its inner circular wall contains 64 sculpted niches depicting Yoginis in dynamic postures.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
Entry / ticketFree entry (ASI-protected monument).
DESTINATION 15 · ODISHA

Barabati Fort & Netaji Birthplace Museum

Cuttack

Barabati Fort, built by the Eastern Ganga dynasty, preserves a stone gateway and moat beside the Mahanadi. Janakinath Bhawan, Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose's birthplace, displays letters and memorabilia from his life.

Opening timeBarabati Fort: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM; Netaji Museum: Tue–Sun: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM; closed Mondays.
Entry / ticketBarabati Fort is free; Netaji Birthplace Museum around ₹10–₹20 per person.
DESTINATION 16 · ODISHA

Dhabaleswar Temple

Athagarh · Cuttack

This Shiva shrine stands on a wooded island in the Mahanadi River about 27 km from Cuttack. Visitors reach it by boat or pedestrian footbridge; the temple draws large crowds during Bada Osha and Kartika Purnima.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 5:30 AM – 8:30 PM.
Entry / ticketTemple darshan is free; ferry or bridge toll charges are nominal, around ₹10–₹30.
DESTINATION 17 · ODISHA

Ratnagiri, Lalitgiri & Udayagiri Buddhist Complex

Jajpur / Cuttack

The Buddhist Diamond Triangle preserves 5th–12th-century monastic centres. Excavations revealed brick stupas, carved doorways, monastery courtyards, Buddha statues and sacred relic caskets.

Opening timeAll three sites and museums: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM; museums closed Fridays.
Entry / ticketRatnagiri and Lalitgiri: around ₹25 Indian / ₹300 foreign each; Udayagiri entry is free.
DESTINATION 18 · ODISHA

Biraja Temple

Jajpur

On the banks of the Baitarani River in Jajpur, Maa Biraja Temple is a revered Shakti Peetha. The present stone shrine dates to the 13th century and houses a two-armed idol of Goddess Durga slaying Mahishasura; Navratri is a major celebration.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 5:30 AM – 12:30 PM and 4:00 PM – 9:30 PM.
Entry / ticketGeneral darshan is free.
DESTINATION 19 · ODISHA

Bhitarkanika National Park

Kendrapara

Spread across the Brahmani–Baitarani delta, Bhitarkanika protects a major mangrove ecosystem and Ramsar Wetland. Creek boat safaris from Khola or Gupti gates explore habitats for saltwater crocodiles, king cobras, spotted deer, monitor lizards and waterbirds.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM; generally closed during monsoon/nesting season, roughly May 1–July 31.
Entry / ticketEntry permit around ₹40 Indian / ₹1,000 foreign, plus boat safari and guide/camera fees.
DESTINATION 20 · ODISHA

Gahirmatha Marine Sanctuary

Kendrapara

Gahirmatha's coast is a major mass-nesting site for Olive Ridley sea turtles. From December to March, turtles gather on beaches around Nasi Island to lay eggs, followed by hatchling emergence in spring.

Opening timeRegulated daytime eco-tourism; beach access is restricted during nesting windows and requires forest permission.
Entry / ticketCovered by Bhitarkanika forest permit rules; unauthorized tourist boats are not permitted near active nesting beaches.
DESTINATION 21 · ODISHA

Chandipur Beach

Balasore

About 16 km from Balasore, Chandipur is known for its vanishing-sea phenomenon. During low tide, the water can recede up to 5 km, exposing rippled seabed, shallow pools and red ghost crabs before the tide returns.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; check local tide timings before walking onto the exposed seabed.
Entry / ticketBeach access is free.
DESTINATION 22 · ODISHA

Panchalingeswar Temple & Kuldiha Wildlife Sanctuary

Balasore

Panchalingeswar is a hill shrine where five Swayambhu lingams lie beneath a perennial mountain stream. Nearby Kuldiha Wildlife Sanctuary protects forest habitats for elephants, gaur, leopards and giant squirrels.

Opening timePanchalingeswar: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM; Kuldiha: October–June, 6:00 AM – 4:00 PM.
Entry / ticketPanchalingeswar is free; Kuldiha entry around ₹40 per person, plus vehicle and guide fees.
DESTINATION 23 · ODISHA

Simlipal National Park & Barehipani / Joranda Falls

Mayurbhanj

Simlipal is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve and Tiger Reserve known for sal forests, elephants, melanistic tigers and waterfalls. Barehipani Falls drops 399 metres and Joranda Falls 150 metres within the reserve.

Opening timeSeasonal: November 1–June 15; closed during monsoon. Entry permits issued at Pithabata/Jashipur gates between 6:00 AM and 9:00 AM.
Entry / ticketAround ₹100 per Indian visitor / ₹1,000 foreign visitor, plus forest guide and 4x4 vehicle charges.
DESTINATION 24 · ODISHA

Khiching Temple / Kichakeswari Temple

Mayurbhanj

Near the Odisha–Jharkhand border, Kichakeswari Temple is a 10th-century shrine built from dark-blue chlorite stone by the Bhanja rulers and reconstructed in the 1930s. A nearby museum preserves medieval sculptures.

Opening timeTemple: 6:00 AM – 8:00 PM; museum: Tue–Sun: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM; closed Mondays.
Entry / ticketTemple entry is free; museum around ₹5–₹10 per person.
DESTINATION 25 · ODISHA

Daringbadi / Kashmir of Odisha

Kandhamal

At about 915 metres, Daringbadi is known for pine-covered hills, coffee and black-pepper plantations, misty valleys and occasional winter frost. Local highlights include Hill View Park, Doluri River, Midubanda Waterfall and Lovers' Paradise.

Opening timeTown open 24 hours; parks and waterfalls typically 7:00 AM – 5:30 PM.
Entry / ticketTown viewpoints and waterfalls are free; Hill View Park and Nature Park may charge around ₹10–₹20.
DESTINATION 26 · ODISHA

Gopalpur-on-Sea & Tara Tarini Temple

Ganjam

Gopalpur is a coastal town known for golden sands, colonial port ruins and a historic lighthouse. About 30 km north, Tara Tarini Temple sits atop Purnagiri Hill beside the Rushikulya River and can be reached by steps, road or ropeway.

Opening timeGopalpur Beach open 24 hours; lighthouse: 3:30 PM – 5:30 PM; Tara Tarini Temple: 5:30 AM – 8:30 PM.
Entry / ticketBeach and temple are free; lighthouse and ropeway/bus have separate charges.
DESTINATION 27 · ODISHA

Taptapani Hot Springs & Jirang Monastery

Ganjam / Gajapati

Taptapani is known for its natural sulfur hot-water spring. Further south at Chandragiri, Padmasambhava Mahavihara (Jirang Monastery) is a major Tibetan Buddhist monastery with a 70-foot stupa and colourful murals.

Opening timeTaptapani: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM; Jirang Monastery: 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
Entry / ticketEntry to both the spring tank and monastery is free.
DESTINATION 28 · ODISHA

Hirakud Dam & Debrigarh Wildlife Sanctuary

Sambalpur

Hirakud Dam spans the Mahanadi River about 15 km from Sambalpur and creates a vast reservoir viewed from Gandhi Minar and Jawahar Minar. Nearby Debrigarh Wildlife Sanctuary is known for gaur, leopards, chousingha and its links to freedom fighter Veer Surendra Sai.

Opening timeHirakud viewpoints: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM; Debrigarh: October–June, 6:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
Entry / ticketHirakud viewpoints around ₹10–₹20; Debrigarh safari permits and vehicle charges vary.
DESTINATION 29 · ODISHA

Samaleswari Temple & Leaning Temple of Huma

Sambalpur

Maa Samaleswari Temple is a revered 16th-century shrine and focal point of the Nuakhai harvest festival. About 23 km south, the 17th-century Leaning Temple of Huma is a rare Shiva temple that tilts noticeably to one side.

Opening timeSamaleswari: 5:30 AM – 12:30 PM and 4:00 PM – 9:30 PM; Huma: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM.
Entry / ticketGeneral darshan at both temples is free.
DESTINATION 30 · ODISHA

Nrusinghanath Temple & Harishankar Temple

Bargarh / Balangir

These pilgrimage shrines stand on opposite slopes of the Gandhamardan Hills, beside mountain streams and medicinal forests. Pilgrims traditionally trek across the ridge between the temples during Nrusinghanath Mela.

Opening timeBoth temples: 6:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
Entry / ticketEntry and darshan at both temples are free.
DESTINATION 31 · ODISHA

Gupteswar Cave Temple & Deomali Peak

Koraput

Gupteswar Cave Temple is a limestone hill shrine above the Kolab River with a naturally formed Shiva Lingam. Deomali Peak, at about 1,672 metres, is Odisha's highest mountain and is known for meadows, cloud views and nearby tribal villages.

Opening timeGupteswar: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM; Deomali: open 24 hours, daylight visits recommended.
Entry / ticketEntry to both Gupteswar Cave Temple and Deomali Peak is free.
DESTINATION 32 · ODISHA

Duduma Waterfalls & Kolab Dam

Koraput

Duduma Waterfalls plunges along the Machkund River on the Odisha–Andhra Pradesh border, framed by rugged gorges and forested hills. Kolab Dam and its botanical garden near Jeypore offer reservoir views, boating and landscaped gardens.

Opening timeBoth sites: 7:00 AM – 5:30 PM.
Entry / ticketDuduma viewing is free; Kolab Botanical Garden around ₹10–₹20, boating extra.
DESTINATION 33 · ODISHA

Satkosia Gorge & Tikarpada Wildlife Sanctuary

Angul / Nayagarh

The Mahanadi cuts through the Eastern Ghats to form the forested Satkosia Gorge, a 22-km river canyon within Satkosia Tiger Reserve. Tikarpada is a base for riverside eco-camps, boat trips and wildlife viewing.

Opening timeSeasonal: October–June, 6:00 AM – 5:00 PM; closed during monsoon.
Entry / ticketAround ₹40 per Indian visitor / ₹1,000 foreign visitor, plus boat safari and eco-camp charges.
DESTINATION 34 · ODISHA

Bhimkund & Sanaghagara / Badaghagara Waterfalls

Keonjhar

Bhimkund is a deep natural pool carved into rocky gorges by the Baitarani River and linked in local legend to Bhima. Near Keonjhar town, Sanaghagara and Badaghagara are twin waterfalls surrounded by forested picnic areas.

Opening timeAll three sites: 7:00 AM – 5:30 PM.
Entry / ticketBhimkund is free; Sanaghagara and Badaghagara park entry around ₹20–₹30 per person.
DESTINATION 35 · ODISHA

Khandadhar Waterfalls & Vedavyas Temple

Sundargarh / Rourkela

Khandadhar Waterfalls plunges about 244 metres in a single horse-tail drop amid forests in Bonaigarh subdivision. Near Rourkela, Vedavyas Temple stands at the confluence of the Sankh, Koel and Saraswati rivers and is linked to Sage Vyasa.

Opening timeKhandadhar: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM; Vedavyas Temple: 6:00 AM – 8:30 PM.
Entry / ticketKhandadhar around ₹20–₹30 per person; Vedavyas Temple is free.
