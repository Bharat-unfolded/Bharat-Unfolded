01 · SECTOR 1

Sukhna Lake

Set at the foothills of the Shivalik range in Sector 1, Sukhna Lake is a large man-made reservoir and Chandigarh's signature waterfront hangout. Its scenic promenade is popular for morning walks and jogging, pedal boats, and winter migratory bird sightings.

LocationSector 1, ChandigarhKnown forPromenade, boating and migratory birds
Opening timeExact daily hours are not listed on Maps; commonly visited during early morning and sunset/evening hours.
Ticket priceWalking along the lakefront promenade is free. Pedal boat rides and lakeside activities are paid separately.

02 · SECTOR 1

Rock Garden of Chandigarh

Located in Sector 1, the 24-acre Rock Garden was conceived by Nek Chand using industrial and urban waste such as broken ceramics, bangles, tiles and rock formations. Its winding courtyards, waterfalls, and whimsical human and animal figures make it a must-see Chandigarh landmark.

LocationSector 1, ChandigarhKnown forRecycled-art sculptures and waterfalls
Opening timeExact daily hours are not listed on Maps; commonly visited during daytime hours.
Ticket pricePaid entry applies; typically around ₹30 for Indian adults and ₹15 for children. Confirm current gate tariff on arrival.

03 · SECTOR 16B

Zakir Hussain Rose Garden

Spread across 30 acres in Sector 16B, Zakir Hussain Rose Garden opened in 1967 and features numerous rose varieties, shaded walkways and fountains. The nearby Rose Garden area offers open green spaces for morning strollers.

LocationSector 16B, ChandigarhKnown forRose varieties, fountains and shaded walks
Opening timeZakir Hussain Rose Garden: Mon–Sat 6:00 AM–10:00 PM; Sun 9:00 AM–10:00 PM. Nearby Rose Garden: daily 5:00 AM–9:00 PM. Verify current schedule.
Ticket priceGeneral garden entry is typically free or nominal. Confirm any special event or festival pass charges at the gate.

04 · SECTOR 1

Capitol Complex

Situated in Sector 1, the Capitol Complex is the modernist government precinct designed as part of Chandigarh's master plan. It features monumental concrete architecture and open plazas admired for their geometric scale, open spaces and civic design heritage.

LocationSector 1, ChandigarhKnown forModernist architecture and civic landmarks
Opening timeExact daily hours are not listed on Maps; entry into specific government buildings or tours is subject to security and scheduled access rules.
Ticket priceExterior plaza access is generally free. Guided entry or passes, when scheduled, follow official security rules.

05 · INDUSTRIAL AREA PHASE I

Elante Mall

Located in Industrial Area Phase I, Elante Mall is a shopping and entertainment hub with retail stores, dining outlets, a cinema multiplex and family play areas. Its wide layout and dining variety make it a convenient indoor break.

LocationIndustrial Area Phase I, ChandigarhKnown forShopping, dining and cinema
Opening timeExact daily hours are not listed on Maps; retail stores, dining outlets and the cinema typically operate from morning through late evening.
Ticket priceMall entry is free. Shopping, dining, cinema tickets and parking/play zones are paid separately.

06 · SECTOR 1

Chandigarh Bird Park

Located near Rock Garden and Lake Sports Club entry in Sector 1, Chandigarh Bird Park features landscaped walk-through aviaries and bird habitats. Visitors can see colorful exotic and native birds in managed enclosures.

LocationNear Rock Garden, Sector 1Known forWalk-through aviaries and bird habitats
Opening timeWed–Sun 10:00 AM–4:30 PM; closed Mondays and Tuesdays. Verify current schedule.
Ticket pricePaid entry applies; typically around ₹50 for adults and ₹30 for children. Confirm current rate at the counter.

09 · SHIVALIK HILLS

Sukhna Wildlife Sanctuary

Lying northeast of Sukhna Lake toward the Shivalik hills, Sukhna Wildlife Sanctuary is an eco-tourism forest reserve featuring eight nature trails, birds and forest fauna such as deer and leopards. Its trails offer quiet hiking and forest scenery away from city traffic.

LocationNortheast of Sukhna Lake, Shivalik foothillsKnown forNature trails, birds and forest wildlife
Opening timeMon–Sun 10:00 AM–4:00 PM. Verify current entry and trail access with the forest office.
Ticket pricePaid forest entry or permit and parking charges apply; confirm current rates at the forest range office.

10 · SECTOR 10C

Government Museum and Art Gallery

Located in Sector 10C, the Government Museum and Art Gallery houses collections spanning ancient sculptures, miniature paintings, modern art, architecture and natural history. Its calm galleries offer curated cultural displays.

LocationSector 10C, ChandigarhKnown forSculpture, miniature paintings and modern art
Opening timeTue–Sun 10:00 AM–4:30 PM; closed Mondays. Verify current schedule before visiting.
