Lakshadweep

Discover coral islands, clear lagoons, white-sand beaches and the living culture of India’s smallest Union Territory — a chain of tropical islands in the Arabian Sea.

TRAVEL NOTES BEFORE YOU GO
Lakshadweep has 30 island and coastal experiences listed here, but not every island is open to tourists. Entry permits, authorised tour packages, accommodation and boat services are controlled and can change. Some islets and bird habitats are protected or uninhabited; do not assume landing is allowed. Check the Lakshadweep Administration’s latest permit and travel rules before booking. Protect coral reefs: never touch, stand on or collect coral.
