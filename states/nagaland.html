25 Top Tourist Places in Nagaland

DESTINATION 01 · NAGALAND

Dzükou Valley

Kohima · Nagaland–Manipur border

Straddling the Nagaland–Manipur border at about 2,452 metres and roughly 25 km south of Kohima, Dzükou Valley is known for rolling emerald bamboo-grass hills, winding streams and seasonal pink-and-white Dzükou lilies, usually blooming in June–July. Treks begin from Viswema or Zakhama villages; cloud-filled views and simple overnight rest-house stays make it a favourite trekking destination.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 4:00 PM recommended for daylight trekking; flowers are usually best in June–July, while October–April often brings clearer views.Entry / ticketAround ₹100 for Indian visitors; foreign visitor rates, camera charges, guides and rest-house fees may vary. Nagaland Inner Line Permit rules apply.
DESTINATION 02 · NAGALAND

Kisama Heritage Village / Hornbill Festival Venue

Kohima · about 12 km south of Kohima

Naga Heritage Village at Kisama is a cultural complex designed like a traditional Naga village, with tribal morungs and architecture representing the communities of Nagaland. It becomes the main venue for the Hornbill Festival, held every year from 1–10 December, with folk performances, indigenous games, crafts and food.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM; festival programming may extend into the evening during 1–10 December.Entry / ticketOff-season entry and Hornbill Festival ticket prices can change; check the Nagaland Tourism notice before visiting.
DESTINATION 03 · NAGALAND

Kohima War Cemetery

Garrison Hill · Kohima

Maintained by the Commonwealth War Graves Commission, Kohima War Cemetery honours the Allied soldiers who died in the 1944 Battle of Kohima. Its terraced lawns, stone memorials and the well-known epitaph make it one of the city’s most significant Second World War memorials.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM; confirm local access before visiting.Entry / ticketFree entry.
DESTINATION 04 · NAGALAND

Khonoma Green Village

About 20 km west of Kohima

Khonoma is an Angami Naga village known for its forested hills, stone lanes, traditional gates and terraced alder-based farming. The community’s conservation movement led to a village-wide decision in 1998 to protect forests and wildlife, including the Blyth’s tragopan.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM for day visits; homestays may arrange overnight stays.Entry / ticketA village or conservation contribution may be collected; confirm current fees and local guide charges with the village council.
DESTINATION 05 · NAGALAND

Japfu Peak

Kigwema · south of Kohima

Rising to about 3,048 metres, Japfu Peak is one of Nagaland’s best-known high points. The forest trek from the Kigwema side passes through bamboo and rhododendron habitat and rewards hikers with wide views over the Naga hills and Dzükou region. The area is also associated with a record-holding giant rhododendron tree.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 4:00 PM recommended; October–April is generally preferred for trekking conditions.Entry / ticketA nominal village or trail contribution may apply; arrange a local guide and confirm current charges before setting out.
DESTINATION 06 · NAGALAND

Nagaland State Museum

Bayavü Hill · Kohima

The Nagaland State Museum introduces the history and cultural diversity of the state’s communities through traditional attire, ornaments, wood carvings, household objects, musical instruments, tools and displays of village life. It is a useful starting point for understanding Naga material culture before visiting the villages.

Opening timeTue–Sun: 9:30 AM – 3:30 PM; closed Mondays and state holidays.Entry / ticketNominal entry charges may apply; confirm the current ticket rate at the museum.
DESTINATION 07 · NAGALAND

Mary Help of Christians Cathedral

Aradura Hill · Kohima

Standing on Aradura Hill, Mary Help of Christians Cathedral is the principal Catholic cathedral of the Diocese of Kohima. Its modern design incorporates Naga architectural motifs, and the hilltop setting offers broad views across the city.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 6:30 PM; visitors should avoid entering during services unless attending respectfully.Entry / ticketFree entry; donations are optional.
DESTINATION 08 · NAGALAND

Pulie Badze Wildlife Sanctuary

Jotsoma village · Kohima

Located near Jotsoma village outside Kohima, this 9.23 sq. km sanctuary protects subtropical evergreen and cloud forest reaching about 2,296 metres. Its forest trails and birdlife include the rare Blyth’s tragopan, Nagaland’s state bird, as well as the dark-rumped swift and barking deer.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 4:30 PM; October–April is generally best.Entry / ticketApprox. ₹20–₹50 per person; confirm current forest or village conservation charges.
DESTINATION 09 · NAGALAND

Touphema Tourist Village

Touphema · about 41 km north of Kohima

This community-run Angami heritage village offers traditional-style Naga cottages, cultural displays, folk performances and local cuisine. It is a good place to experience village hospitality and arrange a cultural stay.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours for staying guests; day visitors: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketApprox. ₹20–₹50 for day visits; cottages and meals cost extra.
DESTINATION 10 · NAGALAND

Kachari Ruins / Rajbari Ruins

Dimapur

The Kachari Ruins are a group of historic monoliths and pillars associated with the medieval Dimasa Kachari kingdom. Their distinctive mushroom-shaped and V-shaped forms make this an important heritage stop in Dimapur.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM; check local access.Entry / ticketFree entry; verify current site rules.
DESTINATION 11 · NAGALAND

Triple Falls

Seithekema · Chümoukedima district

A forest trail leads to three streams that cascade down a steep green hillside into a natural pool. Triple Falls is a popular short outing for nature walks, photography and picnics near Dimapur.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM; visit in daylight.Entry / ticketApprox. ₹20–₹50; village, parking and eco-fees may vary.
DESTINATION 12 · NAGALAND

Nagaland Zoological Park

Rangapahar · Chümoukedima district

Spread across forested terrain at Rangapahar, the state zoological park supports wildlife conservation and education. Its animal collection includes regional birds and mammals such as hornbills, Blyth’s tragopan, bears, leopards and deer.

Opening timeTue–Sun: 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM; closed Mondays.Entry / ticketIndicative: ₹20 adults and ₹10 children; extra charges may apply for cameras, parking and battery vehicles.
DESTINATION 13 · NAGALAND

Diezephe Craft Village

Diezephe · about 13 km from Dimapur

Diezephe is known for traditional Naga handloom and handicrafts. Visitors can discover woodcarving, bamboo and cane work, weaving and locally made shawls, and may be able to arrange demonstrations with artisans.

Opening timeMon–Sat: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM; closed Sundays.Entry / ticketVillage entry is generally free; confirm workshop charges with artisans.
DESTINATION 14 · NAGALAND

Green Park & Noune Resort

Dimapur–Chümoukedima area

Green Park offers landscaped gardens and picnic spaces, while nearby Noune Resort is a lakeside retreat with wooded surroundings and activities such as boating and kayaking. Check each venue separately before travelling between them.

Opening timeGreen Park: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM; Noune Resort: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM.Entry / ticketIndicative: Green Park ₹20–₹30; Noune Resort ₹50–₹100; activities extra.
DESTINATION 15 · NAGALAND

Mokokchung Town & Ungma Village

Mokokchung district

Mokokchung is a lively Ao Naga town set among green hills. A short drive away, Ungma is one of the best-known Ao villages, with traditional community spaces, log drums, heritage structures and views across the surrounding hills.

Opening timeOpen daily; village visits are best planned between 7:00 AM and 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketNo general entry fee; ask locally about guided visits or museum charges.
DESTINATION 16 · NAGALAND

Longkhum Village

About 17 km southwest of Mokokchung

Longkhum is an Ao Naga hill village known for misty viewpoints, rhododendrons in spring, local weaving and dramatic rocky outcrops including Mongzu Ki. Its folklore and landscape make it a memorable stop around Mokokchung.

Opening timeOpen daily; visit cliff viewpoints in daylight.Entry / ticketFree entry; local guide or homestay costs may apply.
DESTINATION 17 · NAGALAND

Mopungchuket Village

About 16 km from Mokokchung

Mopungchuket is an Ao Naga heritage village associated with the Jina and Etiben folk story. Visitors can explore a morung museum, watchtowers, monoliths, village gardens and a small lake; activities may depend on local arrangements.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:30 PM.Entry / ticketGenerally free; small museum or boating fees may apply.
DESTINATION 18 · NAGALAND

Chuchuyimlang Village

About 30 km from Mokokchung

Known as the “Village of Festivals,” Chuchuyimlang celebrates Ao traditions through festivals such as Moatsü and Tsüngremmong. The village is surrounded by green hills and community spaces, with local cultural and craft experiences best arranged ahead.

Opening timeOpen daily; daytime visits recommended.Entry / ticketFree entry; confirm any event or activity charges locally.
DESTINATION 19 · NAGALAND

Mon Town & Longwa Village

Mon district · Konyak Naga region

Mon is a gateway to Konyak Naga culture. Longwa, near the India–Myanmar border, is known for the Angh’s house and its unusual location across the international boundary. Arrange a local guide and check current border-area rules before visiting.

Opening timeDaytime visits recommended, around 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketVillage entry is generally free; local guide charges may apply. Confirm current permit requirements.
DESTINATION 20 · NAGALAND

Shangnyu Village & Veda Peak

Mon district

Shangnyu is known for its Konyak heritage and a large carved wooden monument traditionally associated with the village chief’s house. Veda Peak is a nearby high viewpoint; weather and road conditions affect visibility and access.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM; check local access.Entry / ticketGenerally free; guide and transport costs may apply.
DESTINATION 21 · NAGALAND

Mount Tiyi & Doyang Reservoir

Wokha district

Mount Tiyi is a forested peak linked to Lotha Naga folklore. Doyang Reservoir is a major birding destination, especially during the Amur falcon migration and roosting season, usually around October–November.

Opening timeMount Tiyi: 6:00 AM – 5:00 PM; Doyang: daylight hours. Peak falcon season is usually October–November.Entry / ticketViewpoints are generally free; boating and local birding guides cost extra.
DESTINATION 22 · NAGALAND

Ntangki (Intanki) National Park

Peren district

Ntangki National Park protects a large area of forest, bamboo groves and river habitats. Wildlife reported in the wider landscape includes hoolock gibbons, elephants, hornbills, deer and other mammals. Entry and access should be arranged with the forest department.

Opening timeAccess is seasonal and weather-dependent; confirm current visiting dates and hours with the forest department.Entry / ticketForest permits, guide and vehicle charges apply; verify current rates before travel.
DESTINATION 23 · NAGALAND

Benreu Tourist Village & Mount Pauna

Peren district

Benreu is a Zeliang Naga community tourism village set among high hills and terraced farms. Nearby Mount Pauna offers forest trekking and wide views across the surrounding region; use a local guide and check trail conditions.

Opening timeDay visits and treks are best planned between 6:00 AM and 5:00 PM; homestays by arrangement.Entry / ticketVillage entry is generally free; guide and homestay charges apply.
DESTINATION 24 · NAGALAND

Mount Saramati & Thanamir Village

Kiphire district · India–Myanmar border region

Mount Saramati, at about 3,841 metres, is Nagaland’s highest peak. Thanamir, known as the Apple Village, is a common base for the trek. The high-altitude route needs advance planning, local guides and suitable weather.

Opening timeTrekking is generally planned in the October–April season; start early and check conditions locally.Entry / ticketVillage entry is generally free; registration, guides and porters may be chargeable.
DESTINATION 25 · NAGALAND

Shilloi Lake

Near Lutsam village · Phek district

Shilloi is a scenic natural lake in a bowl-shaped valley near the Myanmar border. Surrounded by hills and meadows, it is associated with local Pochury folklore and is best enjoyed during a calm daylight visit.

Opening timeOpen daily; daylight visits, around 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM, are recommended.Entry / ticketGenerally free; check locally for any village or parking charge.
