India travel – common questions

Which are the most famous tourist places in India?

Popular choices include the Taj Mahal in Agra, Jaipur's forts and palaces, Varanasi's ghats, Goa's beaches, Kerala's backwaters, Kashmir's valleys, the Golden Temple in Amritsar, Hampi, Khajuraho and the Himalayan hill stations.

Which destinations are good for a first trip to India?

The Golden Triangle (Delhi, Agra and Jaipur), Kerala, Goa, Rajasthan and selected Himalayan destinations are common starting points. Choose based on season, trip length, budget and interests.

How can I find places state-wise?

Use the search box or browse the state and union territory sections below. Open a regional guide for more information about that part of India.

Are entry fees and opening times included?

Not in this directory. Fees, opening hours, permits and transport schedules can change, so verify them with the official destination authority before travelling.
