35 Top Tourist Places & Waterfalls in Meghalaya

Explore Meghalaya’s living root bridges, dramatic waterfalls, limestone caves, crystal-clear rivers, Khasi heritage, sacred forests and the green hills of the Garo and Jaintia regions.

35 destinations · Visitor timings · Entry information

DESTINATION 01 · MEGHALAYA

Double Decker Living Root Bridge / Umshiang

Nongriat, Sohra / Cherrapunji

Hidden deep inside a lush tropical rainforest valley at Nongriat village near Sohra, the Umshiang Double Decker Living Root Bridge is an extraordinary bio-engineering marvel hand-woven by the Khasi people from the aerial roots of Ficus elastica (rubber fig) trees over centuries. Reached via a steep 3,500-step descent from Tyrna village across suspension bridges, the two-tiered living bridge spans a clear emerald forest stream.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 5:00 PM (start the trek from Tyrna before 10:00 AM to return before dark).Entry / ticketNominal village conservation entry fee of around ₹30–₹50 per person (plus camera fee ~₹20–₹50 and optional guide/homestay costs).
DESTINATION 02 · MEGHALAYA

Nohkalikai Falls

Sohra / Cherrapunji

Plunging an astonishing 340 meters (1,115 feet) down a sheer, forested cliff into an emerald-blue plunge pool near Sohra, Nohkalikai Falls is India's tallest plunge waterfall. Named after the tragic Khasi legend of Likai, the cascade thunders dramatically during the monsoon while winter offers clear views of the turquoise pool from cliffside viewing decks.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketEntry fee is around ₹20–₹30 per person (plus nominal vehicle parking and camera fees).
DESTINATION 03 · MEGHALAYA

Seven Sisters Falls / Nohsngithiang Falls

Mawsmai, Sohra / Cherrapunji

Located just south of Mawsmai village near Sohra, Nohsngithiang Falls—famously known as Seven Sisters Falls—consists of seven distinct parallel streams plunging roughly 315 meters down sheer limestone cliffs overlooking the plains of Bangladesh. Fed predominantly by rainwater, the waterfall is at its most spectacular during and immediately after the monsoon (June to October).

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:30 PM.Entry / ticketRoadside viewpoint is Free; viewing from inside Eco Park carries a nominal park entry ticket.
DESTINATION 04 · MEGHALAYA

Mawsmai Cave

Sohra / Cherrapunji

Situated about 6 km from Sohra town near Mawsmai village, Mawsmai Cave is Meghalaya's most accessible illuminated limestone cave system, winding roughly 150 meters through dramatic karst formations. Visitors squeeze through narrow natural rock passages, wade ankle-deep across clear spring pools, and admire stalactites and stalagmites sculpted by underground water.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 9:30 AM – 5:30 PM.Entry / ticketAround ₹20–₹30 for Indian adults (₹10 children), plus camera fees (~₹20–₹50).
DESTINATION 05 · MEGHALAYA

Arwah Cave

Sohra / Cherrapunji

Nestled inside the Lawshynna hill slopes near Sohra, Arwah Cave is a vast, well-lit limestone cavern famous for preserving million-year-old marine fossils—including ancient shells, crustaceans, and fish bones—embedded directly in its rocky walls. A scenic cliffside forest walkway overlooks waterfalls and gorges before reaching spacious chambers and dramatic karst pillars.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 9:30 AM – 5:30 PM.Entry / ticketAround ₹20–₹30 per person (plus nominal parking and camera fees).
DESTINATION 06 · MEGHALAYA

Wei Sawdong Falls

Sohra / Cherrapunji

Tucked inside a dense wooded gorge near Dainthlen in Sohra, Wei Sawdong is a stunning three-tiered waterfall renowned for its crystalline turquoise plunge pools framed by dark rock ledges. A steep, rustic bamboo-railed downhill trail leads to its layered cascades and clear emerald waters.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 4:30 PM (daylight hours recommended due to steep bamboo stairs).Entry / ticketNominal village/eco-tourism entry fee of around ₹30–₹50 per person.
DESTINATION 07 · MEGHALAYA

Dainthlen Falls

Sohra / Cherrapunji

Located about 5 km from Sohra on a broad rocky plateau, Dainthlen Falls plunges roughly 90 meters into a deep forested ravine and is steeped in Khasi folklore about the slaying of a giant mythical serpent (Thlen). Visitors can walk across flat rock slabs near the upper stream and observe natural rock carvings tied to the legend.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:30 PM.Entry / ticketNominal entry/parking fee of around ₹10–₹20 per person.
DESTINATION 08 · MEGHALAYA

Kynrem Falls & Thangkharang Park

Sohra / Cherrapunji

Set inside Thangkharang Park about 12 km from Sohra, Kynrem Falls is a towering three-tiered waterfall cascading 305 meters (1,000 feet) down a lush green mountainside. The hilltop park offers gardens, rare orchids, a greenhouse, panoramic views of Bangladesh's plains and the Khoh Ramhah rock formation.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketPark entry around ₹20–₹30 per adult (₹10 children), plus nominal parking/camera fees.
DESTINATION 09 · MEGHALAYA

Eco Park

Sohra / Cherrapunji

Developed by the Meghalaya government on a scenic plateau near Mawsmai in Sohra, Eco Park features manicured lawns, an orchid conservatory showcasing native Meghalayan flora, pedestrian bridges, and walking paths. Its cliffside viewpoints sit at the upper rim of the Seven Sisters Falls gorge, with views of the waterfalls and Bangladesh plains.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketNominal entry ticket of around ₹20–₹30 per person.
DESTINATION 10 · MEGHALAYA

Umngot River / Dawki

West Jaintia Hills

Flowing through the border town of Dawki along the India–Bangladesh frontier, the Umngot River is famous for its crystal-clear emerald-green waters. During autumn and winter (October to April), wooden country boats can appear to float above the riverbed pebbles.

Opening timeBoating Mon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM (water clarity is best October to April; muddy during monsoon).Entry / ticketRiverbank viewing is Free; traditional wooden boat rides cost around ₹600–₹800 per boat (up to 3–4 passengers) for 45–60 minutes.
DESTINATION 11 · MEGHALAYA

Shnongpdeng

Dawki, West Jaintia Hills

Located about 7 km upstream from Dawki along the Umngot River, Shnongpdeng is a tranquil riverside adventure village famed for clear, quiet stretches of turquoise water and a suspension footbridge. Visitors camp on pebble beaches and enjoy kayaking, snorkeling, cliff jumping, scuba diving, and zip-lining.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours for campers (adventure activities Mon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM).Entry / ticketVillage/riverbank access is Free (boating ~₹500–₹700; kayaking, cliff jumping, and camping packages ₹300–₹1,500+).
DESTINATION 12 · MEGHALAYA

Mawlynnong Village / Cleanest Village in Asia

East Khasi Hills

Nestled near the Bangladesh border about 78 km south of Shillong, Mawlynnong was famously crowned 'Asia's Cleanest Village' by Discover India. The Khasi village features bamboo dustbins on every lane, flower gardens, traditional stilted houses, and a bamboo Sky Walk overlooking Bangladesh.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM (day visitors).Entry / ticketVillage entry/parking around ₹50–₹100 per vehicle (Sky Walk viewpoint ~₹20 per person).
DESTINATION 13 · MEGHALAYA

Riwai Living Root Bridge & Balancing Rock

Mawlynnong, East Khasi Hills

Located in Riwai village just 2 km before Mawlynnong, the Riwai Living Root Bridge is an easily accessible single-tier bridge grown across a rocky forest stream, reached by a gentle 10-minute stone-step walk. Nearby stands the Balancing Rock, a massive boulder perched at an angle atop a smaller rock.

Opening timeBoth sites Mon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:30 PM.Entry / ticketRiwai Living Root Bridge around ₹20 per person; Balancing Rock around ₹10–₹20 per person.
DESTINATION 14 · MEGHALAYA

Umiam Lake / Barapani

Ri-Bhoi

Stretching across a vast area amidst pine-covered hills about 15 km north of Shillong, Umiam Lake (Barapani) is a scenic reservoir created in the 1960s by damming the Umiam River. Emerald islands and misty ridges surround this popular stop for speedboating, kayaking, water scooters, and sunset photography.

Opening timeWater Sports Complex & viewpoints Mon–Sun: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketRoadside viewpoints are Free; Nehru Park/Water Sports Complex entry around ₹20–₹50 per person (boating ₹200–₹1,000+ extra).
DESTINATION 15 · MEGHALAYA

Elephant Falls

Upper Shillong, East Khasi Hills

Located about 12 km from central Shillong in Upper Shillong, Elephant Falls is a picturesque three-tiered waterfall cascading down fern-draped rocks amidst dense pine woods. It was named by the British after a nearby elephant-shaped rock, later destroyed in the 1897 earthquake. Stone steps allow visitors to view all three tiers.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketAround ₹20–₹30 per person (plus nominal camera/parking fees).
DESTINATION 16 · MEGHALAYA

Shillong Peak

Upper Shillong, East Khasi Hills

Rising to 1,965 meters (6,449 feet) in Upper Shillong, Shillong Peak is the highest point in the East Khasi Hills and offers panoramic views over Shillong city, pine valleys, and distant ranges on clear days. Located inside an Indian Air Force radar base, visitors undergo security verification at the gate.

Opening timeTue–Sun: 9:00 AM – 3:30 PM (Closed Mondays; valid government ID required and cameras/phones face restrictions).Entry / ticketNominal entry fee of around ₹30–₹50 per person/vehicle.
DESTINATION 17 · MEGHALAYA

Ward's Lake

Shillong, East Khasi Hills

Nestled in central Shillong near Police Bazar, Ward's Lake (Nan Polok) is a horseshoe-shaped artificial lake created during the colonial era. Cobblestone walking trails, flowerbeds, pine trees, and an arched wooden bridge over koi-filled waters make it popular for paddle boating and afternoon strolls.

Opening timeWed–Mon: 8:30 AM – 5:30 PM (Closed Tuesdays).Entry / ticketAround ₹20 for adults and ₹10 for children (paddle boating around ₹100–₹200 extra).
DESTINATION 18 · MEGHALAYA

Lady Hydari Park

Laban, Shillong, East Khasi Hills

Located in Laban, Shillong, Lady Hydari Park is a Japanese-style landscaped botanical park named after the first lady of Assam, Lady Hydari. Its grounds feature rose beds, rhododendrons, willow trees, small ponds, and a mini zoo with Himalayan black bears, leopards, deer, and native birds.

Opening timeTue–Sun: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Closed Mondays).Entry / ticketAround ₹20 for adults and ₹10 for children.
DESTINATION 19 · MEGHALAYA

Don Bosco Museum

Mawlai, Shillong

Situated in Mawlai, Shillong, the Don Bosco Centre for Indigenous Cultures is a seven-story hexagonal museum dedicated to Northeast India. Its galleries showcase traditional costumes, weapons, musical instruments, handicrafts, and a rooftop skywalk with panoramic views of Shillong.

Opening timeMon–Sat: 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM (Closed Sundays).Entry / ticketAround ₹100 for Indian adults (₹50 for students) and ₹200 for foreign visitors.
DESTINATION 20 · MEGHALAYA

Cathedral of Mary Help of Christians

Laitumkhrah, Shillong

Crowning a hillock in Laitumkhrah, Shillong, the Cathedral of Mary Help of Christians is the principal church of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Shillong, built in 1936 on an earthquake-resistant foundation. It is admired for blue-and-white Gothic-style spires, stained-glass windows, and a serene prayer hall overlooking the city.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 6:30 PM.Entry / ticketEntry is Free.
DESTINATION 21 · MEGHALAYA

Police Bazar & Golf Links

Shillong, East Khasi Hills

Police Bazar (Khyndailad) is Shillong's lively commercial hub, with local Khasi street food, handicrafts, winter wear, and cafes. A short drive east lies Shillong Golf Links, one of Asia's oldest and largest natural 18-hole golf courses, set amidst rolling pine-lined meadows.

Opening timePolice Bazar: Mon–Sun: 9:00 AM – 9:30 PM; Golf Links: Mon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM.Entry / ticketWalking through Police Bazar and the outer meadow paths of Golf Links is Free.
DESTINATION 22 · MEGHALAYA

Laitlum Canyons

Smit, East Khasi Hills

Perched above Smit village about 25 km southeast of Shillong, Laitlum Canyons ('End of Hills') is a dramatic mountain ridge overlooking deep green gorges, winding valley trails, and remote hamlets like Rasong below. Drifting clouds often blanket the grassy cliff edges, making sunrise and sunset especially atmospheric.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM (best early morning or late afternoon before thick fog).Entry / ticketEntry is Free (nominal village parking around ₹20–₹50).
DESTINATION 23 · MEGHALAYA

Mawphlang Sacred Forest / Law Lyngdoh

Mawphlang, East Khasi Hills

Located about 25 km southwest of Shillong, Mawphlang Sacred Forest (Law Lyngdoh) is an ancient forest preserved for over 800 years under indigenous Khasi spiritual traditions. It contains moss-draped oaks, medicinal plants, orchids, and monolith altars. Local tradition requires that nothing—not even a leaf or twig—be taken out.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM.Entry / ticketAround ₹20–₹50 per person plus a mandatory local Khasi guide fee (~₹300–₹500 per group).
DESTINATION 24 · MEGHALAYA

Krang Suri Waterfalls

Amlarem, West Jaintia Hills

Nestled near Amlarem about 30 km from Dawki, Krang Suri is celebrated for its vivid sapphire-blue plunge pool framed by golden cliffs and lush forest. Visitors descend a scenic stone pathway to swim with life jackets or walk behind the curtain of falling water.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketEntry around ₹50 per person (life jacket rental ~₹50–₹100 extra).
DESTINATION 25 · MEGHALAYA

Phe Phe Falls

Shkentalang, West Jaintia Hills

Located near Shkentalang village not far from Krang Suri, Phe Phe Falls ('Paradise Falls') is a secluded two-tiered turquoise waterfall cascading down stepped boulders into an emerald basin. A picturesque 20-to-30-minute downhill trail crosses streams and bamboo bridges.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:30 AM – 4:30 PM (daylight hours recommended).Entry / ticketNominal village eco-tourism entry fee of around ₹50 per person.
DESTINATION 26 · MEGHALAYA

Nartiang Monoliths & Nartiang Durga Temple

West Jaintia Hills

Situated in Nartiang village about 65 km from Shillong, Nartiang Monoliths is Meghalaya's largest collection of megalithic menhirs and dolmens, including the 27-foot Moo Shynrang, erected between the 16th and 18th centuries by Jaintia kings. Nearby stands the 500-year-old Nartiang Durga Temple, one of the 51 sacred Shakti Peethas blending Hindu and Jaintia traditions.

Opening timeNartiang Monoliths: Mon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM; Durga Temple: Mon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM.Entry / ticketEntry to both the monoliths (ASI site) and Durga Temple is Free.
DESTINATION 27 · MEGHALAYA

Mawsynram & Mawjymbuin Cave

East Khasi Hills

Located about 60 km southwest of Shillong, Mawsynram is famed for exceptional annual monsoon rainfall amidst rolling green hills and waterfalls. Just outside town, Mawjymbuin Cave is a natural cavern known for a massive stalagmite naturally shaped like a Shiva Lingam beneath a dripping rock dome.

Opening timeMawjymbuin Cave: Mon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM.Entry / ticketEntry to Mawsynram and Mawjymbuin Cave is Free (optional donations at the shrine).
DESTINATION 28 · MEGHALAYA

Krem Liat Prah & Krem Chympe

East Jaintia Hills

Hidden inside the limestone-rich Shnongrim ridge, Krem Liat Prah is India's longest known natural cave system, with over 34 km mapped and a colossal 'Aircraft Hangar' chamber. Nearby Krem Chympe features an underground river navigated by bamboo rafts and internal waterfalls.

Opening timeSeasonal access November to March (closed during monsoon flooding); guided daytime caving hours apply.Entry / ticketKrem Liat Prah requires special caving permissions and specialist guides; Krem Chympe village eco-tourism guide and bamboo raft tours around ₹500–₹1,500+ per group.
DESTINATION 29 · MEGHALAYA

Nongkhnum River Island

West Khasi Hills

Located about 14 km from Nongstoin where the Kynshi River divides into the Phanliang and Namliang rivers, Nongkhnum is Meghalaya's largest river island and the second largest in Asia. It features a sandy beach, grassy picnic meadows, and nearby Weinia and Langshiang waterfalls.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours daily (daylight visits between 7:00 AM and 5:30 PM recommended).Entry / ticketNominal village/eco-tourism entry and parking fee around ₹20–₹50.
DESTINATION 30 · MEGHALAYA

Kyllang Rock

Mairang, Eastern West Khasi Hills

Rising to 5,400 feet near Mairang, Kyllang Rock (Lum Kyllang) is a colossal dome-shaped red granite monolith over 1,000 feet wide. Khasi mythology regards it as a mighty spirit; visitors hike up stone-cut steps and forest trails to its windswept summit for panoramic views.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketNominal entry/parking fee around ₹20–₹30 per person.
DESTINATION 31 · MEGHALAYA

Wari Chora

South Garo Hills

Tucked deep within the forested hills near Emangre, Wari Chora is a river canyon where emerald-green waters wind between towering moss-covered vertical rock walls. Visitors reach it by rugged off-road drive and short forest hike, then glide through the narrow gorge aboard bamboo rafts.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 4:30 PM (best visited November to April).Entry / ticketVillage entry and bamboo rafting packages around ₹300–₹600 per person.
DESTINATION 32 · MEGHALAYA

Nokrek National Park & Nokrek Peak

West Garo Hills

Located in the Tura Range, Nokrek National Park is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve and the highest peak in the Garo Hills at 1,412 meters. Known as a genetic sanctuary of wild citrus (Memang Narang), its evergreen rainforest protects hoolock gibbons, elephants, clouded leopards, and diverse hornbills.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 4:00 PM (best visited October to April).Entry / ticketForest entry permit around ₹50–₹100 per Indian visitor plus mandatory local guide charges (~₹300–₹500).
DESTINATION 33 · MEGHALAYA

Siju Cave & Siju Bird Sanctuary

South Garo Hills

Situated along the Simsang River, Siju Cave (Dobakkol) is a limestone cave system stretching nearly 4.7 km underground, with dramatic chambers, river passages, and thousands of roosting bats. Adjacent Siju Bird Sanctuary is a forest reserve attracting migratory waterbirds and hornbills along the river.

Opening timeBoth sites Mon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM (closed during heavy monsoon flooding).Entry / ticketAround ₹30–₹50 per person plus mandatory forest guide and torch/helmet rental fees.
DESTINATION 34 · MEGHALAYA

Balpakram National Park

South Garo Hills

Spanning 220 sq. km along the Garo Hills near the Bangladesh border, Balpakram National Park ('Land of Perpetual Winds') is famed for a dramatic deep canyon often called the 'Grand Canyon of Meghalaya.' Revered in Garo mythology as the abode of departed spirits, its plateau and forested gorges shelter wildlife and rare pitcher plants.

Opening timeSeasonal: October to April, 7:00 AM – 4:00 PM (closed during monsoon).Entry / ticketForest entry permit around ₹50–₹100 for Indian visitors (₹300+ for foreigners), plus vehicle and guide fees.
DESTINATION 35 · MEGHALAYA

Pelga Falls & Tura Peak

Tura, West Garo Hills

About 7 km from Tura, Pelga Falls cascades over rocky boulders amidst bamboo groves and is crossed by a traditional bamboo footbridge. Tura Peak rises 872 meters behind the town and is reached by a steep 5-km forest trek offering views of the Brahmaputra Valley and Bangladesh plains.

Opening timePelga Falls: Mon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:30 PM; Tura Peak trek: Mon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 4:30 PM.Entry / ticketEntry to both Pelga Falls and Tura Peak is Free (nominal parking around ₹20 may apply at Pelga Falls).
