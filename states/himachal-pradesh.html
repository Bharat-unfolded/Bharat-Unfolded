40 Best Places to Visit in Himachal Pradesh

DESTINATION 01

Shimla

The Ridge, Christ Church, Mall Road and colonial-era buildings make Shimla one of North India’s best-known hill destinations. Visitors come for mountain views, cafés, heritage walks and seasonal snowfall. The central promenade is lively through the day, while nearby viewpoints offer quieter scenery. Allow time for steep lanes and changing mountain weather.
