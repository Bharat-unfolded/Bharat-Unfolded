DESTINATION 01 · MANIPUR

Loktak Lake & Sendra Island

Moirang, Bishnupur

Located near Moirang in Bishnupur district about 45 km south of Imphal, Loktak Lake is the largest freshwater lake in Northeast India, famous for its circular floating biomass islands called phumdis. Rising from the lake is Sendra Island, a scenic hillock offering panoramic views of traditional fishing huts, wooden canoes, and tranquil sunset horizons.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours daily (boating and Sendra viewpoint visits best between 7:00 AM and 5:30 PM).Entry / ticketLakeside and Sendra viewpoint access is Free (boating rides cost around ₹200–₹800 per boat depending on duration).
DESTINATION 02 · MANIPUR

Keibul Lamjao National Park

Bishnupur

Situated on the southeastern fringe of Loktak Lake in Bishnupur district, Keibul Lamjao National Park is the world's only floating national park, sustained by thick mats of floating phumdi vegetation. It serves as the last natural habitat of the endangered brow-antlered deer (Sangai), Manipur's state animal, alongside hog deer, otters, and migratory waterbirds.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM – 4:30 PM (early morning between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM is best for Sangai sightings).Entry / ticketAround ₹30 for Indian visitors and ₹200 for foreign visitors, plus separate camera and boat fees.
DESTINATION 03 · MANIPUR

Shree Govindajee Temple

Imphal East

Standing adjacent to the historic Royal Palace in Palace Compound, Imphal East, Shree Govindajee Temple is Manipur's premier Vaishnavite shrine, originally built in 1846 during the reign of Maharaja Nara Singh. Featuring twin gold-plated domes, a spacious paved courtyard, and sacred shrines dedicated to Radha-Govinda, Balaram, and Krishna, it hosts vibrant Ras Leela dances.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 4:30 AM – 12:30 PM and 4:00 PM – 8:30 PM (morning and evening aarti rituals are major highlights).Entry / ticketGeneral darshan entry is Free.
DESTINATION 04 · MANIPUR

Kangla Fort & Kangla Sha

Imphal West

Situated on the banks of the Imphal River in the heart of Imphal West, Kangla Fort served as the ancient seat of Manipur's Meitei rulers until 1891. Guarded at its gate by the iconic twin dragon-lion statues (Kangla Sha), the fortified complex encompasses royal palace ruins, Shri Govindajee's original site, the Kangla Museum, moat gardens, and sacred shrines.

Opening timeTue–Sun: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Closed on Mondays).Entry / ticketNominal entry fee of around ₹10–₹20 for Indian visitors and ₹100 for foreign visitors (e-rickshaw/bicycle tours inside cost extra).
DESTINATION 05 · MANIPUR

Ima Keithel / Mother's Market

Imphal West

Located in central Imphal (Khwairamband Bazaar), Ima Keithel ("Mother's Market") is the world's largest all-women-run traditional market, dating back over 500 years. Operated exclusively by thousands of Manipuri women (Imas), its covered market complexes burst with handwoven phaneks, bamboo crafts, fresh produce, dried fish, spices, and regional delicacies.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM.Entry / ticketMarket entry is Free.
DESTINATION 06 · MANIPUR

INA Memorial Complex

Moirang, Bishnupur

Located in Moirang town of Bishnupur district, the Indian National Army (INA) Memorial Complex marks the historic spot where Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose's INA hoisted the Indian Tricolour on mainland Indian soil for the first time on April 14, 1944. The complex features a bronze statue of Netaji, a replica of Singapore's INA Martyr's Memorial, and a World War II museum.

Opening timeTue–Sun: 9:30 AM – 4:30 PM (Closed on Mondays).Entry / ticketNominal entry fee of around ₹10–₹20 per person.
DESTINATION 07 · MANIPUR

Manipur State Museum

Imphal West

Situated near the Polo Ground in Imphal West, the Manipur State Museum showcases the rich cultural, royal, and tribal heritage of the state across well-curated galleries. Exhibits include traditional Meitei and tribal costumes, ancient manuscripts, royal Hiyang Hiren ceremonial war boats, weaponry, portraits of Manipur's rulers, and natural history collections.

Opening timeTue–Sun: 10:00 AM – 4:30 PM (Closed on Mondays and state holidays).Entry / ticketAround ₹10–₹20 for Indian adults (₹5 for children) and ₹50–₹100 for foreign visitors.
DESTINATION 08 · MANIPUR

Shaheed Minar & Bir Tikendrajit Park

Imphal West

Standing prominently in the heart of Imphal West at Bir Tikendrajit Park (opposite Kangla Fort), the tall Shaheed Minar commemorates the indomitable Manipuri patriots—including Bir Tikendrajit and Thangal General—who laid down their lives fighting British forces during the Anglo-Manipur War of 1891. Surrounded by manicured lawns and fountains, it is a serene civic landmark.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM.Entry / ticketEntry to the park and monument is Free.
DESTINATION 09 · MANIPUR

Imphal War Cemetery

Imphal West

Maintained impeccably by the Commonwealth War Graves Commission in Dewlahland, Imphal West, the Imphal War Cemetery honors over 1,600 Commonwealth soldiers who died during the fierce 1944 Battle of Imphal in World War II. Set amidst peaceful sloped lawns, flowering trees, and stone Cross of Sacrifice memorials, each bronze plaque records the soldier's name and regiment.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketEntry is Free.
DESTINATION 10 · MANIPUR

Red Hill / Maibam Lokpa Ching & India Peace Memorial

Bishnupur

Located about 17 km southwest of Imphal along the Tiddim Road in Bishnupur district, Red Hill (Maibam Lokpa Ching) was the site of a decisive 1944 World War II battle between Allied and Japanese forces. At the foot of the hill stands the India Peace Memorial, erected by Japanese veterans in 1994 as a solemn tribute to fallen soldiers and lasting peace.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketEntry to the hill and peace memorial is Free.
DESTINATION 11 · MANIPUR

Sanamahi Temple / Lainingthou Sanamahi

Imphal West

Situated within the 1st Manipur Rifles campus in Imphal West, Lainingthou Sanamahi Temple is one of the oldest and most sacred shrines of the indigenous Sanamahism faith of the Meitei community. Built in distinctive octagonal and curved Bengal-hut (do-chala) architectural style during King Kulachandra's reign, it honors Lainingthou Sanamahi, the guardian deity of households.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 5:00 AM – 7:00 PM.Entry / ticketGeneral darshan entry is Free.
DESTINATION 12 · MANIPUR

Mahabali Temple

Imphal East

Nestled inside a sacred bamboo and banyan forest grove along the Imphal River in Palace Compound, Imphal East, Shri Mahabali Temple is an ancient 18th-century shrine dedicated to Lord Hanuman. Built in traditional curved-roof brick architecture during the reign of Maharaja Garibniwaz, the tranquil woodland sanctuary is home to troops of friendly macaques.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 5:30 AM – 7:30 PM.Entry / ticketGeneral darshan entry is Free.
DESTINATION 13 · MANIPUR

Hiyangthang Lairembi Temple

Imphal West

Crowning a lush wooded hillock about 11 km south of Imphal in Imphal West district, Hiyangthang Lairembi Temple is a revered Shakti shrine dedicated to Goddess Hiyangthang Lairembi (venerated as Goddess Durga). Surrounded by giant ancient trees and reached via stepped pathways, it becomes the vibrant epicenter of Manipur's grand Bor festival during Durga Puja.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM.Entry / ticketGeneral darshan entry is Free.
DESTINATION 14 · MANIPUR

Andro Cultural Heritage Village & Santhei Natural Park

Imphal East

Located about 25 km east of Imphal at the foothills of the Nongmaiching range, Andro is an ancient Loi scheduled-caste village renowned for traditional coil-built black pottery, a sacred eternal fire (Mei) burning for centuries, and a doll museum showcasing Northeast tribes. Nearby lies Santhei Natural Park, a scenic lakeside garden built around a reservoir.

Opening timeBoth sites: Mon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketSanthei Natural Park & Heritage Complex charge nominal fees of around ₹20–₹50 per person.
DESTINATION 15 · MANIPUR

Kaina Hill & Temple

Thoubal

Rising amidst forested hills about 29 km from Imphal in Thoubal district, Kaina is a sacred Vaishnavite hillock where, according to Manipuri tradition, Lord Govindajee appeared in a dream to Maharaja Jai Singh (Bhagyachandra), instructing him to carve his sacred idol from a jackfruit tree growing on this very hill. Today, a serene hilltop temple and Ras Leela pavilion mark the site.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM.Entry / ticketEntry and darshan are Free.
DESTINATION 16 · MANIPUR

Dzüko Valley / Manipur Side

Senapati

Straddling the Manipur–Nagaland border at an altitude of 2,452 meters, Dzüko Valley is famous for its rolling emerald bamboo-grass hills, winding streams, and seasonal Dzüko Lilies blooming in June–July. Approached via the scenic Mt. Iso / Mao trailhead in Manipur's Senapati district, the trek delivers dramatic ridge-top panoramas without heavy crowds.

Opening timeDaytime trekking hours (start between 6:00 AM and 10:00 AM; best visited June–September for flowers or October–April for clear views).Entry / ticketVillage/eco-tourism entry fee at Mao trailhead is around ₹100–₹200 per person (local guide and camping charges extra).
DESTINATION 17 · MANIPUR

Shirui Kashong Peak

Ukhrul

Rising to 2,835 meters in Ukhrul district, Shirui Kashong Peak is a lush mountain summit renowned globally as the only natural habitat of the rare Shirui Lily (Lilium mackliniae), Manipur's state flower, which blooms in pale bluish-pink bell shapes during May and June. A scenic 3-to-4-km ridge trek rewards hikers with sweeping valley views.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 5:00 PM (peak lily bloom occurs from mid-May to mid-June).Entry / ticketNominal village/eco-tourism conservation fee of around ₹50–₹100 per person applies during festival/peak season.
DESTINATION 18 · MANIPUR

Khangkhui Mangsor Cave

Ukhrul

Located about 15 km from Ukhrul town amidst forested limestone hills, Khangkhui Mangsor Cave is a fascinating Paleolithic archaeological site featuring vast natural limestone chambers, stalactites, and stalagmites. Excavations here uncovered Stone Age tools and animal remains, while local Tangkhul Naga folklore associates the caverns with ancient king Mangsor.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM (carry a flashlight/headlamp and visit during daylight).Entry / ticketNominal village/eco-tourism entry fee of around ₹30–₹50 per person applies.
DESTINATION 19 · MANIPUR

Moreh Border Town

Tengnoupal

Situated about 110 km southeast of Imphal in Tengnoupal district right along the India–Myanmar border, Moreh is a bustling international trade town linked to Tamu in Myanmar via the Indo-Myanmar Friendship Gate. Visitors come to explore vibrant cross-border markets selling Southeast Asian goods, electronics, textiles, and local cuisine.

Opening timeMarket hours: Mon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (cross-border movement into Tamu depends on active bilateral border regulations).Entry / ticketVisiting Moreh town and markets is Free (check current travel advisories and ID requirements before traveling).
DESTINATION 20 · MANIPUR

Barak Waterfalls

Tamenglong

Hidden deep inside pristine tropical forests along the Barak River in Tamenglong district, Barak Waterfalls is a series of seven cascading waterfalls plunging through rugged rocky gorges and lush emerald greenery. Celebrated as one of Manipur's most scenic natural wonders, it attracts eco-travelers, photographers, and adventure seekers.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM (daylight visits recommended due to forest trail access).Entry / ticketEntry is Free (local guide or vehicle parking fees if any are nominal).
DESTINATION 21 · MANIPUR

Tharon Cave

Tamenglong

Located about 27 km from Tamenglong town amidst dense bamboo and evergreen forests, Tharon Cave is a remarkable 655-meter-long subterranean limestone cave system mapped with five exits and 34 interconnected chambers. Archaeological findings indicate ancient Hoabinhian human habitation, while locals guide visitors through its winding rocky passages.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM (daylight hours with a local guide and torch required).Entry / ticketCave entry is Free (hiring a local village guide costs ~₹200–₹400 per group).
DESTINATION 22 · MANIPUR

Zeilad Lake Wildlife Sanctuary

Tamenglong

Nestled near Makoi village in Tamenglong district, Zeilad Lake Wildlife Sanctuary encompasses a cluster of seven scenic natural lakes—centered on Zeilad Lake—surrounded by dense subtropical hills. Steeped in Zeliangrong Naga folklore, the sanctuary protects pythons, turtles, barking deer, leopards, and diverse migratory waterbirds.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 4:30 PM (best visited between October and April).Entry / ticketNominal forest/village conservation entry fee of around ₹30–₹50 per person applies.
DESTINATION 23 · MANIPUR

Khongjom War Memorial Complex

Thoubal

Situated at Kheba Ching about 36 km south of Imphal along the Indo-Myanmar highway in Thoubal district, Khongjom War Memorial honors Major Paona Brajabashi and the brave Manipuri warriors who fought to the death against the British army in the final battle of the Anglo-Manipur War on April 23, 1891. The hilltop complex features tall sword-shaped monuments and a statue of Paona Brajabashi.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM.Entry / ticketEntry to the memorial complex is Free.
DESTINATION 24 · MANIPUR

Koubru Peak & Mount Koubru Temple

Kangpokpi

Rising to 2,562 meters in Kangpokpi district northwest of Imphal, Mount Koubru is one of Manipur's highest and most sacred peaks, revered in Meitei tradition as the abode of guardian deity Lainingthou Koubru. Pilgrims and trekkers undertake a scenic, steep uphill climb through mist-wrapped forests to reach the hilltop temple and natural cave shrines.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours (daytime trekking between 6:00 AM and 4:00 PM recommended; check local trail access before visiting).Entry / ticketEntry and trekking on Mount Koubru are Free.
DESTINATION 25 · MANIPUR

Singda Dam

Kangpokpi / Imphal West

Perched at an elevation of 921 meters about 16 km northwest of Imphal along the Kangpokpi–Imphal West border, Singda Dam is recognized as the world's highest mud dam. Surrounded by verdant, terraced hills and quiet village landscapes, the reservoir supplies water to Imphal while offering a peaceful picnic spot with panoramic mountain reflections.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:30 PM.Entry / ticketEntry to the dam viewpoint area is Free.
