Basilica of Bom Jesus

Constructed between 1594 and 1605 in Old Goa, the Basilica of Bom Jesus is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Christendom's most revered churches, housing the mortal remains of St. Francis Xavier in a silver casket inside a richly gilded Baroque altar. Set in the Mandovi River about 10 km upstream from Panaji and reached via short free vehicle-and-passenger ferries from Old Goa or Ribandar, Divar Island is a serene time capsule of rural Goa. Dotted with lush paddy fields, wooded hills, Portuguese-era heritage homes, and the hilltop **Our Lady of Compassion Church**, it offers peaceful cycling and village exploration.

LocationTiswadi, Goa, IndiaKnown forDivar Island
Opening hoursFerries operate roughly 6:00 AM – 10:30 PM daily.
Ticket / entryIsland access and passenger ferry crossings are **Free** (nominal fee applies for four-wheelers on the ferry).

39 · TISWADI, GOA

Chorao Island & Dr. Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary

Located across the Mandovi River from Ribandar via a short ferry ride, Chorao Island is Goa's largest river island, featuring sleepy village lanes, whitewashed chapels, and dense estuarine mangroves. On its western tip lies **Dr. Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary**, a protected mangrove habitat where visitors take guided canoe rides through narrow tidal creeks to spot kingfishers, herons, egrets, mudskippers, and flying foxes.

LocationTiswadi, Goa, IndiaKnown forChorao Island & Dr. Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary
Opening hoursBird Sanctuary: Mon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM (early morning 6:30 AM – 9:30 AM is best for birdwatching).
Ticket / entrySanctuary entry is around ₹10 for Indian visitors and ₹100 for foreign visitors, plus boat/canoe hire charges (around ₹500–₹800 per boat).

40 · OFF VASCO DA GAMA / SOUTH GOA, GOA

Grande Island

Situated in the Arabian Sea a few kilometers off the coast of Vasco da Gama / Bogmalo in South Goa, Grande Island (Ilha Grande) is Goa's premier destination for scuba diving, snorkeling, and boat excursions. Surrounded by clear waters, coral patches, and historic shipwrecks like Suzy's Wreck, day trips departing from Sinquerim, Candolim, or Bogmalo typically combine dolphin spotting, snorkeling, and island beach barbecues.

Locationoff Vasco da Gama / South Goa, Goa, IndiaKnown forGrande Island
Opening hoursBoat excursions operate seasonally (**October to May**), departing around 8:00 AM – 9:00 AM and returning by 3:00 PM – 4:00 PM.
Ticket / entryIsland access is via organized boat/diving tours costing around ₹1,500–₹2,200 for snorkeling/boat packages and ₹3,500–₹5,000 for scuba diving packages.
