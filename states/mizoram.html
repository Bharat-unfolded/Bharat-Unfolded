25 Top Tourist Places in Mizoram

Explore Mizoram’s misty mountain ridges, Mizo heritage villages, waterfalls, sacred lakes, limestone caves, border valleys and rich wildlife reserves.

From Reiek’s panoramic ridges to the blue haze of Phawngpui, discover the hills, lakes and living culture of Mizoram.

Places to Explore

25 destinations · Visitor timings · Entry information

DESTINATION 01 · MIZORAM

Reiek Tlang & Reiek Heritage Village

Mamit / Aizawl belt

Rising to 1,465 meters about 29 km west of Aizawl, Reiek Tlang is a grassy mountain peak with sweeping views of valleys, Aizawl and, on clear days, the plains of Bangladesh. At its foot, Reiek Heritage Village showcases traditional Mizo huts, community halls and cultural life.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM; daylight trekking recommended.Entry / ticketAround ₹20–₹50 per person; parking/camera fees may apply. Indian visitors need a Mizoram Inner Line Permit.
DESTINATION 02 · MIZORAM

Solomon's Temple / Kohhran Thianghlim

Chawlhhmun, Aizawl

A striking white religious complex on Aizawl’s western outskirts, built by Kohhran Thianghlim. Its large prayer hall, four corner spires and landscaped courtyards make it a distinctive modern landmark.

Opening timeMon–Sat: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM; Sunday services at scheduled times.Entry / ticketFree entry; donations are optional.
DESTINATION 03 · MIZORAM

Mizoram State Museum

Zarkawt, Aizawl

Located on McDonald Hill, the museum introduces Mizo history, anthropology and natural heritage through galleries of puan textiles, bamboo and cane crafts, ceremonial objects, weapons, musical instruments and natural-history displays.

Opening timeTue–Fri: 9:30 AM – 5:00 PM; Mon: 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM; closed weekends and state holidays.Entry / ticketApprox. ₹20 Indian adults; ₹10 students/children; foreign visitor rates may differ.
DESTINATION 04 · MIZORAM

Durtlang Hills

Northern Aizawl

A rocky ridge overlooking Aizawl’s hillside neighbourhoods and deep green valleys. The viewpoints are popular at sunrise and sunset, and after dark when the city lights spread across the hills.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; daylight and early evening recommended.Entry / ticketFree access to viewpoints.
DESTINATION 05 · MIZORAM

Bara Bazar / Ritz Market & Chanmari

Central Aizawl

A lively market district around Dawrpui and Chanmari, with Mizo handwoven puans, bamboo baskets, spices, hill produce, winter clothing and local eateries. It offers a close look at everyday city life.

Opening timeMon–Sat: approximately 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM; many shops close Sunday.Entry / ticketFree market access; shopping costs vary.
DESTINATION 06 · MIZORAM

KV Paradise & Falkawn Cultural Village

Durtlang & Falkawn, Aizawl

KV Paradise is a multi-storey marble memorial built by Khawlhring in memory of his wife. Falkawn, about 18 km south of Aizawl, preserves traditional Mizo dwellings, a chief’s house and folk artefacts.

Opening timeBoth generally Mon–Sat: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM; confirm locally before visiting.Entry / ticketKV Paradise approx. ₹20–₹30; Falkawn approx. ₹20.
DESTINATION 07 · MIZORAM

Tam Dil Lake

Near Saitual

A peaceful lake surrounded by forested hills and bamboo groves. Visitors come for paddle boating, lakeside walks, picnics and birdwatching.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketApprox. ₹20–₹30 entry; boating extra, where available.
DESTINATION 08 · MIZORAM

Vantawng Khawhthla / Vantawng Falls

Near Thenzawl, Serchhip

Mizoram’s highest waterfall drops about 229 metres through dense forest on the Vanva River. A viewing tower gives visitors a safe view of the cascade and steep wooded cliffs.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 7:30 AM – 5:00 PM.Entry / ticketApprox. ₹20 adult / ₹10 child; parking or camera charges may apply.
DESTINATION 09 · MIZORAM

Thenzawl Deer Park & Khawnglung Wildlife Sanctuary

Serchhip

Thenzawl Deer Park has woodland enclosures for deer near the town’s golf greens. Khawnglung Wildlife Sanctuary protects rugged forest habitat for wildlife including serow, gibbons and barking deer.

Opening timeDeer Park: generally 9:00 AM – 4:30 PM; sanctuary visits by daylight, subject to forest permission.Entry / ticketDeer Park approx. ₹10–₹20; sanctuary permit and guide charges may apply.

DESTINATION 10 · MIZORAM

Chawngtlai & Hmuifang Tlang

Aizawl / Serchhip hill belt

Hmuifang Tlang rises to about 1,619 metres and is known for green meadows, forested slopes and broad ridge views. Chawngtlai is associated with local Mizo heritage, memorials and village traditions.

Opening timeDaylight visits recommended; approximately 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM.Entry / ticketHmuifang may have a nominal fee; village heritage sites generally free.
DESTINATION 11 · MIZORAM

Phawngpui / Blue Mountain National Park

Lawngtlai

At about 2,157 metres, Phawngpui is Mizoram’s highest peak and a revered mountain near the Myanmar border. Its forests, rhododendrons, orchids and cliff viewpoints support rich bird and wildlife diversity.

Opening timeSeasonal access; commonly October/November–April, subject to forest advisories. Trek during daylight.Entry / ticketForest permit, local guide and transport charges apply; verify current rates before travel.
DESTINATION 12 · MIZORAM

Dampa Tiger Reserve

Mamit

Mizoram’s largest protected forest landscape spans tropical evergreen and semi-evergreen forest, bamboo and steep river valleys. Wildlife includes clouded leopard, elephant, gaur, hoolock gibbon and hornbills.

Opening timeAccess is seasonal and subject to forest department permission; plan daytime visits.Entry / ticketEntry permit, guide and vehicle charges apply; confirm with the reserve.
DESTINATION 13 · MIZORAM

Murlen National Park

Champhai

A misty evergreen forest near the Chin Hills, known for dense canopy, orchids and wildlife such as Himalayan black bear, sambar, serow, hoolock gibbon and pheasants.

Opening timeUsually visited in the drier months, subject to park rules; daytime only.Entry / ticketForest entry permit and guide charges apply.
DESTINATION 14 · MIZORAM

Lengteng Wildlife Sanctuary

Champhai

A forest sanctuary with high ridges rising above 2,100 metres. It is an important bird area and habitat for pheasants, hornbills, clouded leopard and barking deer.

Opening timeSeasonal forest access; daylight visits with local guidance.Entry / ticketForest permit and guide charges apply.
DESTINATION 15 · MIZORAM

Rih Dil & Champhai Valley / Zokhawthar

Champhai and Myanmar border

Champhai Valley is known for terraced paddy fields, vineyards and views of the Chin Hills. Rih Dil lies across the border in Myanmar and is culturally significant to Mizo communities; cross-border access depends on current regulations.

Opening timeChamphai Valley accessible by day; border crossing hours and permissions vary.Entry / ticketValley viewing is free; Rih Dil requires valid cross-border permissions and applicable fees.
DESTINATION 16 · MIZORAM

Khuangchera Puk Cave

Ailawng / Khawbung heritage circuit

A limestone cave associated with the Mizo warrior Khuangchera. Its underground passages are explored with a torch and local guidance, combining natural features with regional history.

Opening timeDaylight visits; check local access and guide availability.Entry / ticketGenerally no formal entry ticket; local guide may cost extra.
DESTINATION 17 · MIZORAM

Lamsial Puk Cave

Farkawn, Champhai

A historic cave associated with local oral traditions and skeletal remains preserved inside the cavern. The site is part of the area’s cultural memory and should be visited respectfully with local guidance.

Opening timeDaylight visits; confirm access with the village.Entry / ticketNo standard ticket reported; local guide may be advisable.
DESTINATION 18 · MIZORAM

Bungtlang & Tlawng River

Kolasib / Aizawl belt

The Tlawng is one of Mizoram’s major rivers, flowing through green valleys and forested riverbanks. The Bungtlang area offers quiet riverside scenery, picnic spots and opportunities to observe local life.

Opening timeOpen access; daytime visits recommended.Entry / ticketFree riverside access.
DESTINATION 19 · MIZORAM

Ngengpui Wildlife Sanctuary

Lawngtlai

A low-altitude forest sanctuary in southwestern Mizoram, with tropical evergreen and riverine habitat. Its wildlife includes elephant, gaur, clouded leopard, barking deer and diverse primates.

Opening timeSeasonal access subject to forest permission; daylight only.Entry / ticketForest permit and guide charges apply.
DESTINATION 20 · MIZORAM

Thorangtlang Wildlife Sanctuary

Lunglei

A forested biodiversity corridor in western Lunglei district, known as an elephant movement route between Mizoram and the Chittagong Hill Tracts. The sanctuary also supports other mammals and primates.

Opening timeSeasonal access subject to forest permission; daylight only.Entry / ticketForest permit and guide charges apply.
DESTINATION 21 · MIZORAM

Khawnglung & Sairep

Lunglei

Sairep is a quiet highland village above misty valleys. Nearby Khawnglung’s forested ridges are linked with the tragic Mizo legend of Khawnglung Run and local heritage sites.

Opening timeOpen village access; daytime sightseeing recommended.Entry / ticketFree access; local guide may be useful.
DESTINATION 22 · MIZORAM

Tuipui D

Kolasib

A peaceful riverside village in northern Mizoram, framed by bamboo groves, betel-nut plantations and gentle hills. Visitors come for quiet river views, fishing spots and rural hospitality.

Opening timeOpen access; daytime visits recommended.Entry / ticketFree access.
DESTINATION 23 · MIZORAM

Sialsuk & Chhingchhip

Aizawl / Serchhip

Two scenic Mizo villages in the central hill belt, known for ridge-top views, rolling green slopes, terraced farms and traditional village life.

Opening timeOpen access; daylight recommended for viewpoints.Entry / ticketFree access to villages and viewpoints.
DESTINATION 24 · MIZORAM

Vaitin & Serlui B Reservoir

Kolasib

A large reservoir framed by forested hills near Bilkhawthlir, with scenic inlets and calm water views. It is a popular setting for lakeside picnics and birdwatching; boating depends on local operations.

Opening timeGenerally 7:00 AM – 5:30 PM for visitor facilities; check locally.Entry / ticketViewing is generally free; boating, if operating, costs extra.
DESTINATION 25 · MIZORAM

Zotlang & Kawlbem

Champhai

Zotlang is associated with Champhai’s cultural landscape, memorial stones and valley views. Kawlbem is a remote highland village surrounded by forest and misty trails near the eastern frontier.

Opening timeOpen village access; daylight recommended.Entry / ticketFree access.
