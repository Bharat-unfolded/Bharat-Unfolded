Discover Andhra Pradesh

From Tirumala's sacred hills and the ancient Buddhist sites of Amaravati to the Eastern Ghats, Godavari river country and the Bay of Bengal coast.

Andhra Pradesh brings together celebrated pilgrimage centres, historic forts, Buddhist heritage, long beaches, forest waterfalls and hill landscapes. Tirumala Venkateswara Temple

⌖ Tirupati

Perched on Venkatadri, the seventh peak of the Seshachalam Hills, this major Vaishnava shrine is dedicated to Lord Venkateswara. Its Dravidian complex is crowned by the gold-plated Ananda Nilayam vimana; the famous Tirupati laddu is offered as prasadam.

Opening hoursDaily, darshan batches run around the clock; timings vary with sevas and rituals.Entry / ticketFree Sarva Darshan; Special Entry Darshan around ₹300.
