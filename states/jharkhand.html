21 Top Tourist Places & Temples in Jharkhand

Discover sacred shrines, forest waterfalls, wildlife and scenic escapes across the Land of Forests.

BHARAT UNFOLDED · JHARKHAND

Explore Jharkhand

From ancient pilgrimage routes to waterfalls, forests and hill-country views.
Jharkhand is known for its sacred pilgrimage sites, forested plateaus, dramatic waterfalls, reservoirs and wildlife landscapes. This guide brings together 21 destinations across Deoghar, Dumka, Giridih, Ranchi, Ramgarh, Latehar, Jamshedpur and Dhanbad. Visitor hours and charges are indicative; always confirm local conditions before travel.

01 · DEOGHAR, JHARKHAND

Baba Baidyanath Jyotirlinga Temple श्री बाबा बैद्यनाथ ज्योतिर्लिंग मंदिर

One of the twelve revered Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, set within a major temple complex of 22 shrines. The annual Shravani Mela brings Kanwariya pilgrims who carry holy water from Sultanganj.

LocationDEOGHAR, Jharkhand, IndiaKnown forJyotirlinga shrine, 22-shrine complex and Shravani Mela

Timings & Entry

Opening hours4:00 AM – 9:00 PM; festival hours may be extended
Entry / ticketGeneral darshan free; optional quick-darshan passes and special puja are charged separately.

Timings and fees are indicative and can change by season, festival, weather or local administration. Confirm current access, safety conditions and charges with the site before travelling.

03 · MADHUBAN, GIRIDIH, JHARKHAND

Parasnath Hill (Shikharji) पारसनाथ पर्वत / श्री सम्मेद शिखरजी

Jharkhand’s highest peak, around 4,480 ft, and one of Jainism’s holiest pilgrimage sites. Jain tradition holds that 20 of the 24 Tirthankaras attained moksha here. Pilgrims usually begin the long ascent from Madhuban.

LocationMADHUBAN, GIRIDIH, Jharkhand, IndiaKnown forJain pilgrimage, sacred mountain trek and panoramic views

Timings & Entry

Opening hoursDaylight yatra; start early. No fixed all-day visitor schedule
Entry / ticketGeneral access free; doli carriers, guides and any available transfers cost extra.
