01 · DIU

Diu Fort

Constructed by the Portuguese in 1535 along the Arabian Sea coast of Diu Island, Diu Fort is an imposing seaside fortress encircled by three-sided ocean ramparts and a deep moat. Visitors explore historic iron cannons, arched passageways, ancient chapels, and a tall lighthouse offering sweeping views of the sea and Panikota Fort.

02 · DIU

Naida Caves

Located just outside the walls of Diu Fort, Naida Caves is a labyrinthine network of interconnected rock-cut tunnels, square-hewn steps, and open-ceiling caverns formed originally by Portuguese stone quarrying. Dramatic shafts of sunlight pour through natural rock openings onto warm orange-brown sandstone walls, making it a favorite photography stop.

03 · DIU

Nagoa Beach

Situated in Bucharwada village about 7 km from Diu town, Nagoa Beach is a picturesque horseshoe-shaped cove fringed by swaying Hoka palm trees and soft golden sand. Known for calm, shallow waters safe for swimming, it is Diu's hub for parasailing, jet skiing, banana boat rides, and camel rides.

04 · DIU

Ghoghla Beach

Stretching along the mainland side of Diu across the creek from Diu town, Ghoghla Beach is Diu's longest shoreline and a proud Blue Flag-certified eco-beach. Celebrated for its clean golden sands, tranquil atmosphere, solar-powered amenities, and dramatic sunsets, it offers safe swimming zones and seasonal water activities.

Tucked inside a rocky coastal cavern at Fudam village about 3 km from Diu town, Gangeshwar Mahadev Temple is an ancient shrine linked to the five Pandava brothers of the Mahabharata. 06 · DIU

INS Khukri Memorial & Chakratirth Beach

Perched on a scenic coastal cliff overlooking Chakratirth Beach and Sunset Point in Diu, the INS Khukri Memorial honors Captain Mahendra Nath Mulla and 194 Indian Navy personnel who went down with the frigate INS Khukri during the 1971 Indo-Pak War. It features a large scale model of the warship inside a glass enclosure.

07 · DIU

St. Paul's Church & Diu Museum / St. Thomas Church

Completed in 1610 in central Diu, St. Paul's Church is a striking white Baroque church renowned for its ornate shell-motif facade and intricately carved wooden altar. Nearby stands the 1598 St. Thomas Church, now housing the Diu Museum, which displays antique stone sculptures, wooden carvings, and colonial-era inscriptions.

08 · DIU

Sea Shell Museum & Zampa Gateway

Located near Nagoa Beach, the Sea Shell Museum exhibits over 2,500–3,000 rare marine shells collected globally by merchant navy captain Devjibhai Vira Fulbaria. In central Diu stands Zampa Gateway, a bright red-and-ochre medieval entrance arch adorned with carved lions, angels, and religious reliefs.

11 · MOTI DAMAN

Moti Daman Fort & Bom Jesus Church

Built by the Portuguese in 1559 along the southern bank of the Daman Ganga River, Moti Daman Fort encloses ten bastions, two grand gateways, and colonial administrative buildings within massive stone walls. Inside stands the 1603 Church of Bom Jesus, famed for its gilded wooden altar and richly carved Baroque portals.

12 · NANI DAMAN

Nani Daman Fort / St. Jerome Fort

Standing on the northern bank of the Daman Ganga River opposite Moti Daman, St. Jerome Fort (Nani Daman Fort) was built between 1614 and 1627 and is distinguished by a giant statue of St. Jerome flanking its river-facing gateway. 14 · DABHEL, DAMAN

Somnath Mahadev Temple

Located in Dabhel village on the outskirts of Nani Daman, Somnath Mahadev Temple is a revered 19th-century Shiva shrine built around a self-emerged (Swayambhu) lingam discovered in the area. 15 · SILVASSA

Dudhni Lake & Madhuban Dam

Created by the massive Madhuban Dam across the Daman Ganga River about 40 km from Silvassa, Dudhni Lake is a sprawling emerald reservoir ringed by green Sahyadri foothills. Often called the Kashmir of West India, it offers Kashmir-style shikara boat rides, speedboating, kayaking, and scenic lakeside sunset viewpoints.

Satmaliya Deer Park

Our Lady of Piety Church

Built in 1889 in central Silvassa opposite the Tribal Cultural Museum, Our Lady of Piety Church is one of the oldest surviving Portuguese-era stone churches in Dadra and Nagar Haveli. 23 · SILVASSA

Nakshatra Garden & Butterfly Park

Developed along the Daman Ganga riverfront in Silvassa, Nakshatra Garden is uniquely landscaped according to the 27 Nakshatras (lunar constellations) and 12 zodiac signs, planting specific medicinal and sacred trees associated with each star. Adjacent lies the Butterfly Park, featuring nectar-rich flowering gardens and paved walking trails.
