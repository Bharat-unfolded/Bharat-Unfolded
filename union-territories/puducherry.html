Puducherry

French-colonial streets, peaceful ashrams, ancient ports, colourful temples and long beaches — explore all four regions of this coastal Union Territory.

ABOUT THIS GUIDE
This list includes places in all four regions of the Union Territory: Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe and Yanam. Some places have limited access or specific opening hours. Confirm current timings, boat operations and visitor rules before travelling. Auroville’s Matrimandir viewing and meditation visits require advance arrangements.
