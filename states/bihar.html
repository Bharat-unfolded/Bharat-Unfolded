Nalanda🏛️
05 / BIHAR

Archaeological Site of Nalanda Mahavihara

Founded under Emperor Kumaragupta I in the 5th century CE, Nalanda Mahavihara became a celebrated centre of learning from the 5th to the 12th century. Students travelled here from China, Mongolia, Tibet, Korea and other parts of Asia. Admission to advanced study was selective, with teachers testing prospective students. Mahayana Buddhism was a major focus, alongside other subjects. In the late 12th century, the monastery was attacked by forces led by Bakhtiyar Khalji; its monastic community and celebrated library were devastated. The UNESCO-listed ruins remain a symbol of shared Asian learning. Nalanda University has also been re-established nearby.
