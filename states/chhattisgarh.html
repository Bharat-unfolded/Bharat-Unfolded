01 · BASTAR, CHHATTISGARH

Chitrakote Waterfalls

Often called the “Niagara Falls of India,” Chitrakote Waterfalls is on the Indravati River about 38 km west of Jagdalpur. During the monsoon, it stretches nearly 300 metres wide in a horseshoe shape and drops roughly 95 feet into a forested gorge. Boating is available near the base in drier months.

LocationBastar, Chhattisgarh, IndiaKnown forChitrakote Waterfalls
Opening hoursMon–Sun: 6:00 AM–7:00 PM; daylight views recommended.
Ticket / entryFree viewpoint entry; parking around ₹20–₹50; boat rides around ₹100–₹200 per person.

02 · BASTAR, CHHATTISGARH

Tirathgarh Waterfalls

Inside Kanger Valley National Park, about 35 km southwest of Jagdalpur, Tirathgarh is a multi-tiered cascade where the Mugabahar River drops nearly 300 feet over rocky ledges. Stone steps lead through greenery to viewing platforms and a small Shiva-Parvati shrine.

03 · BASTAR, CHHATTISGARH

Kanger Valley National Park & Kutumsar Caves

This forest reserve near Jagdalpur covers roughly 200 sq. km and is known for sal and teak forests, wildlife and karst formations. Kutumsar Caves descend about 35 metres and extend over 300 metres, with stalactites, stalagmites and blind cave fish.
