8 Top Tourist Places in Tripura

Explore Tripura

INDIA · TRIPURA · 8 DESTINATIONS

Discover the soul of Tripura.

From royal palaces and sacred temples to ancient rock carvings, forest hills and tranquil lakes.

THE LAND OF PALACES, TEMPLES AND GREEN HILLS
Tripura brings together the heritage of the Manikya dynasty, revered pilgrimage sites, remarkable rock carvings, forest sanctuaries and peaceful lake landscapes. This guide covers eight destinations across the state. Opening times, permits and fees can change; confirm locally before travel.

01 · AGARTALA, WEST TRIPURA

Ujjayanta Palace

A former royal residence of Tripura’s Manikya dynasty, now home to the Tripura State Museum. Its grand white façade, landscaped gardens and fountains make it one of Agartala’s best-known landmarks.

LocationAGARTALA, WEST TRIPURA, Tripura, IndiaKnown forA former royal residence of Tripura’s Manikya dynasty, now home to the Tripura State Museum
Opening hoursTue–Sun: approximately 10:00 AM–5:15 PM; closed Monday. Confirm museum hours and special evening programmes locally.
Entry / ticketMuseum entry ticket applies; often around ₹20–₹50 for Indian visitors. Special shows, if operating, may cost extra.

Hours, entry fees and facilities can change. Confirm current access with the site authority or local operator before travelling.

Photo source: Wikimedia Commons
02 · UDAIPUR, GOMATI

Tripura Sundari Temple (Matabari)

A revered Shakti Peetha built in 1501, known for its distinctive temple architecture and the Kalyan Sagar pond beside the shrine.

LocationUDAIPUR, GOMATI, Tripura, IndiaKnown forA revered Shakti Peetha built in 1501, known for its distinctive temple architecture and the Kalyan Sagar pond beside the shrine
Opening hoursDaily: approximately 4:00 AM–10:00 PM; festival schedules may differ.
Entry / ticketGeneral darshan is free. Puja offerings and donations are optional.

Hours, entry fees and facilities can change. Confirm current access with the site authority or local operator before travelling.

Photo source: Wikimedia Commons
03 · UNAKOTI DISTRICT, NEAR KAILASHAHAR

Unakoti Rock Carvings

An extraordinary forested archaeological site in the Raghunandan Hills, famous for monumental Shaivite rock reliefs and ancient stone carvings.

LocationUNAKOTI DISTRICT, NEAR KAILASHAHAR, Tripura, IndiaKnown forAn extraordinary forested archaeological site in the Raghunandan Hills, famous for monumental Shaivite rock reliefs and ancient stone carvings
Opening hoursDaily: approximately 8:00 AM–5:00 PM; confirm seasonal access.
Entry / ticketMonument entry fee applies; indicative rates are around ₹25 for Indian visitors and ₹300 for foreign visitors. Confirm at the gate.

Hours, entry fees and facilities can change. Confirm current access with the site authority or local operator before travelling.

Photo source: Wikimedia Commons
04 · MELAGHAR, SEPAHIJALA

Neermahal

A water palace completed in 1938 in the middle of Rudrasagar Lake, blending Hindu and Islamic architectural details. Visitors usually reach it by boat from the ghat.

LocationMELAGHAR, SEPAHIJALA, Tripura, IndiaKnown forA water palace completed in 1938 in the middle of Rudrasagar Lake, blending Hindu and Islamic architectural details
Opening hoursDaily: approximately 9:30 AM–5:00 PM. Boat services depend on daylight, weather and lake conditions.
Entry / ticketPalace entry and boat/ferry charges apply; indicative shared-boat charges are around ₹30–₹50 per person, subject to change.

Hours, entry fees and facilities can change. Confirm current access with the site authority or local operator before travelling.

Sepahijala Wildlife Sanctuary & Zoo

A green sanctuary combining woodland trails, zoo enclosures, botanical sections and a lake where boating may be available.

LocationBISHALGARH, SEPAHIJALA, Tripura, IndiaKnown forA green sanctuary combining woodland trails, zoo enclosures, botanical sections and a lake where boating may be available
Opening hoursIndicative schedule: Tue–Thu and Sat 9:00 AM–4:00 PM; Sun 9:00 AM–5:30 PM; closed Friday. Verify before travel.
Entry / ticketEntry ticket applies; boating, camera and parking charges may be separate.

Hours, entry fees and facilities can change. Confirm current access with the site authority or local operator before travelling.

06 · VANGHMUN, NORTH TRIPURA

Jampui Hills

A scenic highland ridge known for cooler weather, orange orchards, terraced slopes and broad views across the surrounding valleys.

LocationVANGHMUN, NORTH TRIPURA, Tripura, IndiaKnown forA scenic highland ridge known for cooler weather, orange orchards, terraced slopes and broad views across the surrounding valleys
Opening hoursOpen hill area; daytime visits are recommended, especially because roads are winding.
Entry / ticketGeneral access is free. Transport, accommodation and local guide charges are separate.

Hours, entry fees and facilities can change. Confirm current access with the site authority or local operator before travelling.

07 · GOMATI–DHALAI REGION

Dumboor Lake & Gonda Twisa

A large reservoir formed by the Gumti River, dotted with small islands and surrounded by green hills. Gonda Twisa is a nearby base for exploring the region’s rural and hilly landscapes.

LocationGOMATI–DHALAI REGION, Tripura, IndiaKnown forA large reservoir formed by the Gumti River, dotted with small islands and surrounded by green hills
Opening hoursOpen area; visit during daylight and check boat operations and road conditions locally.
Entry / ticketGeneral access is usually free; boat rides, transport and eco-stays cost extra.

Hours, entry fees and facilities can change. Confirm current access with the site authority or local operator before travelling.

Photo source: Wikimedia Commons
08 · GOMATI DISTRICT, GUMTI RIVER

Chabimura Rock Carvings

Ancient rock-cut images, including a striking relief of Goddess Durga, rise above the steep banks of the Gumti River near Chakwrakma. The river gorge is best explored by boat in daylight.

LocationGOMATI DISTRICT, GUMTI RIVER, Tripura, IndiaKnown forAncient rock-cut images, including a striking relief of Goddess Durga, rise above the steep banks of the Gumti River near Chakwrakma
Opening hoursDaytime visit recommended; boat trips depend on weather, river conditions and local operators.
Entry / ticketSite access is generally free or nominal; boat and guide charges may apply.

Hours, entry fees and facilities can change. Confirm current access with the site authority or local operator before travelling.
