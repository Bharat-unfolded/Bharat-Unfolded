India — a story shaped over thousands of years
A journey through time

India: many eras, one living civilisation.

India's story has grown across river valleys, ancient cities, kingdoms, trade routes, art, ideas and movements for freedom. Its past is not just preserved in monuments; it continues in languages, traditions, food, music and everyday life.

Ancient roots

The Indus Valley Civilisation developed some of South Asia's earliest cities, including Harappa and Mohenjo-daro. Over centuries, new traditions of learning, philosophy, mathematics, literature and architecture took shape across the subcontinent.

Kingdoms, ideas and architecture

Empires and regional kingdoms shaped different parts of India. The Maurya and Gupta periods, the Chola kingdoms, the Delhi Sultanate and the Mughal Empire each left marks on administration, art, literature and architecture. Buddhist, Hindu, Jain, Sikh and many other traditions grew and interacted over time.

The freedom movement and modern India

Many generations took part in India's struggle against British colonial rule. India became independent on 15 August 1947. The Constitution came into effect on 26 January 1950, and India became a republic.
