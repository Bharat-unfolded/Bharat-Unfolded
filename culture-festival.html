Culture & Festival

India's traditions change from region to region. These are a few well-known examples, not the whole story.

🪔 Diwali

Festival of lights celebrated in many parts of India with lamps, family gatherings, sweets and local traditions.

🎨 Holi

Spring festival known for colours, music and community celebrations. Follow local guidance and use skin-safe colours.

🌾 Pongal & Makar Sankranti

Harvest-season celebrations observed in different forms across states, with food, gratitude and seasonal rituals.

🐘 Onam

Kerala's harvest festival, associated with pookalam floral designs, Onam sadya and cultural performances.

🥁 Durga Puja

Especially prominent in West Bengal, with artistic pandals, worship, music and community gatherings.

🌙 Eid

Celebrated by Muslim communities with prayers, charity, family visits and festive meals; dates follow the lunar calendar.

🎶 Baisakhi & Bihu

Seasonal and harvest-linked celebrations with regional music, dance, food and community events in Punjab and Assam.

🧵 Living heritage

Explore handloom weaving, folk music, classical dance, pottery, crafts and regional languages wherever you travel.

Festival dates can change each year and celebrations differ by community and region. Check the local calendar before planning a visit.
