DESTINATION 01 · MAHARASHTRA

Gateway of India & Elephanta Caves

Mumbai

Built in 1924 along the Apollo Bunder waterfront in South Mumbai to commemorate the 1911 visit of King George V and Queen Mary, the Gateway of India is a grand basalt triumphal arch overlooking the Arabian Sea. From its jetty, ferries depart for Gharapuri Island to explore the UNESCO-listed Elephanta Caves, 5th–7th century rock-cut Shaivite shrines famous for the monumental three-headed Trimurti sculpture.

Opening timeGateway of India: Open 24 hours; Elephanta Caves: Tue–Sun: 9:00 AM–5:30 PM (Closed Mondays; ferries run ~9:00 AM–3:30 PM).Entry / ticketGateway: Free; Elephanta Caves: ₹40 Indian / ₹600 foreign, plus round-trip ferry ~₹260 per adult.
DESTINATION 02 · MAHARASHTRA

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus & Marine Drive

Mumbai

Constructed in 1887 in South Mumbai, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT) is a UNESCO World Heritage railway station celebrated for its ornate Victorian Gothic Revival domes, turrets, and Indian architectural motifs. A short distance west curves Marine Drive, a 3.6-km seaside boulevard famed as the “Queen's Necklace” when its streetlights glow after dusk.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours daily (heritage guided tours of CSMT run in afternoon slots).Entry / ticketCSMT exterior and Marine Drive walk: Free; guided CSMT heritage tours ~₹200 per person.
DESTINATION 03 · MAHARASHTRA

Siddhivinayak Temple & Mahalaxmi Temple

Mumbai

Located in Prabhadevi, Shree Siddhivinayak Ganapati Mandir is Mumbai's most iconic Ganesha shrine, built in 1801 and housing a black-stone idol with a rare right-tilted trunk. Along the Bhulabhai Desai seafront stands the 18th-century Mahalaxmi Temple, dedicated to Goddesses Mahalaxmi, Mahakali, and Mahasaraswati overlooking the Arabian Sea.

Opening timeSiddhivinayak: Mon–Sun 5:30 AM–9:50 PM (extended Tuesdays); Mahalaxmi: Mon–Sun 6:00 AM–10:00 PM.Entry / ticketGeneral darshan at both temples: Free; optional paid Mukh Darshan / VIP passes at Siddhivinayak.
DESTINATION 04 · MAHARASHTRA

Haji Ali Dargah & Bandra-Worli Sea Link

Mumbai

Situated on a tiny tidal islet off Worli in South Mumbai and connected by a 500-meter narrow causeway, Haji Ali Dargah is a 15th-century Indo-Islamic marble shrine housing the tomb of Sufi saint Pir Haji Ali Shah Bukhari. Rising across Mahim Bay just north is the iconic 5.6-km Bandra-Worli Sea Link.

Opening timeHaji Ali: Mon–Sun 5:30 AM–10:00 PM (causeway access depends on sea tide); Sea Link: Open 24 hours for four-wheelers.Entry / ticketHaji Ali: Free; Sea Link toll ~₹100 one-way / ₹150 return for cars (no pedestrians/two-wheelers).
DESTINATION 05 · MAHARASHTRA

Sanjay Gandhi National Park & Kanheri Caves

Mumbai

Spanning 87 sq. km within northern Mumbai's Borivali belt, Sanjay Gandhi National Park is a rare urban tropical forest sheltering leopards, deer, macaques, and over 270 bird species. Deep inside the park sit the Kanheri Caves, a complex of 109 basalt rock-cut Buddhist caves dating from the 1st century BCE to the 10th century CE.

Opening timeNational Park: Tue–Sun 7:30 AM–6:30 PM (Closed Mondays); Kanheri Caves: Tue–Sun 8:00 AM–5:30 PM.Entry / ticketPark ~₹95 adults / ₹50 children; Kanheri Caves ₹25 Indian / ₹300 foreign.
DESTINATION 06 · MAHARASHTRA

Ajanta Caves

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar / Aurangabad

Carved into a horseshoe-shaped cliff along the Waghora River gorge about 100 km from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, the Ajanta Caves are 30 rock-cut Buddhist cave monuments dating from the 2nd century BCE to around 480 CE. A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Ajanta is celebrated for its fresco paintings depicting the Jataka tales and serene Buddha sculptures.

Opening timeTue–Sun 9:00 AM–5:00 PM (Closed Mondays).Entry / ticket~₹40 Indian / ₹600 foreign (ASI ticket; mandatory shuttle from parking ~₹30–₹50 extra).
DESTINATION 07 · MAHARASHTRA

Ellora Caves & Kailasa Temple

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar / Aurangabad

Located about 30 km northwest of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar in the Charanandri hills, the UNESCO-listed Ellora Caves comprise 34 monumental rock-cut temples and monasteries representing Buddhism, Hinduism, and Jainism carved between the 6th and 10th centuries CE. Its centerpiece is Cave 16 (Kailasa Temple), the world's largest monolithic rock excavation carved top-down from a single basalt cliff.

Opening timeWed–Mon 6:00 AM–6:00 PM (Closed Tuesdays).Entry / ticket~₹40 Indian / ₹600 foreign (ASI ticket).
DESTINATION 08 · MAHARASHTRA

Bibi Ka Maqbara & Daulatabad Fort

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar / Aurangabad

Built in 1660 by Prince Azam Shah in memory of his mother Dilras Banu Begum, Bibi Ka Maqbara is a Mughal mausoleum famed as the “Taj of the Deccan”. Nearby rises Daulatabad (Devagiri) Fort, a hill fortress featuring dry moats, dark spiked passageways, and Chand Minar.

Opening timeBibi Ka Maqbara: Mon–Sun 8:00 AM–8:00 PM; Daulatabad Fort: Mon–Sun 8:00 AM–6:00 PM.Entry / ticketBoth monuments ~₹25–₹30 Indian / ₹300 foreign (ASI tickets).
DESTINATION 09 · MAHARASHTRA

Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga Temple

Verul, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar

Situated in Verul village less than a kilometer from Ellora Caves, Grishneshwar Temple is revered as the twelfth Jyotirlinga of Lord Shiva. Rebuilt in its present form in the 18th century by Queen Ahilyabai Holkar using red volcanic basalt, it showcases intricate Maratha-style carvings and Dashavatara sculptures.

Opening timeMon–Sun 5:30 AM–9:30 PM (extended during Shravan and Maha Shivratri).Entry / ticketGeneral darshan: Free (men must remove upper-body shirts before entering the inner sanctum).
DESTINATION 10 · MAHARASHTRA

Shirdi Sai Baba Temple

Shirdi, Ahilyanagar / Ahmednagar

Shri Sai Baba Samadhi Mandir is one of India's most visited spiritual shrines, built over the sacred resting place of saint Sai Baba. The sprawling complex encompasses the main Samadhi Mandir, Dwarkamai mosque, Chavadi, Gurusthan, and Lendi Baug.

Opening timeMon–Sun 4:00 AM–11:15 PM (darshan runs between four daily aartis).Entry / ticketGeneral darshan: Free; online time-slot booking recommended; paid Mukha/VIP passes ~₹200 per person.
DESTINATION 11 · MAHARASHTRA

Shani Shingnapur Temple

Ahilyanagar / Ahmednagar

Situated about 35 km from Ahilyanagar and 70 km from Shirdi, Shani Shingnapur is a famed open-air shrine dedicated to Lord Shani, represented by a five-and-a-half-foot self-emerged black stone slab on an uncovered marble platform. The village is traditionally known for houses and shops without locks or doors.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours daily (devotees perform oil abhishekam around the clock).Entry / ticketGeneral darshan: Free.
DESTINATION 12 · MAHARASHTRA

Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga Temple

Nashik

Nestled at the foothills of the Brahmagiri mountains about 28 km from Nashik city near the sacred origin of the Godavari River, Trimbakeshwar is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas. Built in the 18th century by Peshwa Balaji Baji Rao from black stone, its sanctum houses a rare lingam with three faces symbolizing Brahma, Vishnu, and Mahesh.

Opening timeMon–Sun 5:30 AM–9:00 PM.Entry / ticketGeneral darshan: Free; paid VIP darshan pass ~₹200 per person.
DESTINATION 13 · MAHARASHTRA

Panchavati, Sita Gufa & Sula Vineyards

Nashik

Panchavati along the Godavari River, including Kalaram Temple, Ramkund, and Sita Gufa, is deeply tied to Lord Rama's exile and the Kumbh Mela. On the city's rolling hills stands Sula Vineyards, offering guided vineyard walks, cellar tours, and tastings.

Opening timePanchavati & Sita Gufa: Mon–Sun 6:00 AM–9:00 PM; Sula Vineyards: Mon–Sun 11:00 AM–11:00 PM.Entry / ticketPanchavati & Sita Gufa: Free; Sula entry cover ~₹600–₹1,000 per person (redeemable on food/wine; tasting tours extra).
DESTINATION 14 · MAHARASHTRA

Bhimashankar Jyotirlinga Temple & Wildlife Sanctuary

Pune

Set high in the Sahyadri hills at about 3,250 feet in Pune district, Bhimashankar Temple is an ancient Nagara-style black-stone shrine housing one of the twelve Jyotirlingas at the source of the Bhima River. The surrounding wildlife sanctuary protects the endangered Indian Giant Squirrel (Shekru).

Opening timeTemple: Mon–Sun 4:30 AM–9:30 PM; sanctuary trails: Mon–Sun 6:00 AM–6:00 PM.Entry / ticketTemple darshan: Free; sanctuary entry ~₹50 per person plus vehicle fees.
DESTINATION 15 · MAHARASHTRA

Shaniwar Wada, Dagdusheth Halwai Ganpati & Aga Khan Palace

Pune

In central Pune, the 1732 Shaniwar Wada served as the fortified seat of the Maratha Peshwas, alongside the ornate Dagdusheth Halwai Ganpati Temple. Across town stands the 1892 Aga Khan Palace, where Mahatma Gandhi and Kasturba Gandhi were imprisoned during the Quit India Movement.

Opening timeShaniwar Wada: 9:30 AM–5:00 PM; Dagdusheth Ganpati: 6:00 AM–11:00 PM; Aga Khan Palace: 9:00 AM–5:30 PM.Entry / ticketDagdusheth Ganpati: Free; Shaniwar Wada ₹25 Indian / ₹300 foreign; Aga Khan Palace ₹25 Indian / ₹300 foreign.
DESTINATION 16 · MAHARASHTRA

Sinhagad Fort & Karla / Bhaja Caves

Pune

Perched atop a Sahyadri cliff 35 km southwest of Pune, Sinhagad Fort is legendary for Tanaji Malusare's 1670 battle. Along the old Mumbai–Pune highway near Lonavala lie the 2nd-century BCE Karla and Bhaja Caves, renowned for Buddhist rock-cut chaitya prayer halls.

Opening timeSinhagad Fort: 6:00 AM–6:00 PM; Karla & Bhaja Caves: 9:00 AM–5:30 PM.Entry / ticketSinhagad ₹20 per person plus ₹50–₹100 vehicle toll; Karla & Bhaja Caves ₹25 Indian / ₹300 foreign each.
DESTINATION 17 · MAHARASHTRA

Lonavala & Khandala / Tiger's Leap & Bhushi Dam

Pune

The twin hill stations of Lonavala and Khandala burst into greenery and waterfalls during monsoon. Highlights include Bhushi Dam, where water cascades over stone steps, and Tiger's Leap, a dramatic cliff edge offering sweeping valley views.

Opening timeTiger's Leap: Open 24 hours; Bhushi Dam: Mon–Sun 9:00 AM–5:00 PM (best July to October).Entry / ticketEntry to Tiger's Leap and Bhushi Dam: Free (local parking ~₹50–₹100).
DESTINATION 18 · MAHARASHTRA

Rajmachi Fort & Lohagad / Visapur Forts

Lonavala / Pune

Framing the Sahyadri ridges near Lonavala, Lohagad and Visapur offer stone-step climbs, misty ramparts, and the Vinchu Kata bastion at Lohagad overlooking Pawna Lake. Further north sits Rajmachi Fort, a premier trekking destination celebrated for firefly sightings before monsoon.

Opening timeMon–Sun 6:00 AM–6:00 PM (daylight trekking recommended).Entry / ticketLohagad & Visapur ₹25 Indian / ₹300 foreign; Rajmachi trekking free (nominal forest/parking fees if any).
DESTINATION 19 · MAHARASHTRA

Matheran Hill Station

Raigad

Perched at 800 meters in the Western Ghats, Matheran is Asia's only automobile-free hill station, preserving red laterite forest trails, colonial cottages, and clean air. Visitors explore 38 viewpoints on foot, horseback, or aboard the historic Neral–Matheran toy train.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours daily (Dasturi Naka parking gate round the clock; toy train on fixed schedules).Entry / ticketMunicipal entry/eco-tax at Dasturi Naka ₹50 adults / ₹25 children (toy train and horse rides extra).
DESTINATION 20 · MAHARASHTRA

Alibaug Beach, Kolaba Fort & Kashid Beach

Raigad

Alibaug Beach is a coastal getaway featuring black-sand shores and low-tide access to the 17th-century sea fortress Kolaba Fort. About 30 km south lies Kashid Beach, famed for white sands, blue waters, and casuarina groves.

Opening timeBeaches: Open 24 hours; Kolaba Fort: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM (accessible on foot only during low tide).Entry / ticketBeaches: Free; Kolaba Fort ₹25 Indian / ₹300 foreign, plus optional transfers ~₹50–₹150.
DESTINATION 21 · MAHARASHTRA

Murud-Janjira Fort

Raigad

Rising out of the Arabian Sea on an oval rock island off Murud, Murud-Janjira is a formidable 15th–17th century marine fortress built by the Siddis that famously remained unconquered. Traditional sailboats from Rajapuri jetty reach the fort, which has freshwater lakes, massive cannons, and towering bastions.

Opening timeMon–Sun 7:00 AM–6:00 PM (closed during monsoon from late May to September due to rough seas).Entry / ticketFort entry free; round-trip sailboat ferry from Rajapuri jetty ~₹80–₹100 per person.
DESTINATION 22 · MAHARASHTRA

Raigad Fort

Raigad

Crowning a 2,700-foot Sahyadri peak, Raigad Fort served as the capital of the Maratha Empire where Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was crowned in 1674. Accessible via 1,737 steps or ropeway, the plateau preserves Raj Bhavan ruins, Hirakani Buruj, Jagadishwar Temple, and Shivaji Maharaj's Samadhi.

Opening timeFort: Mon–Sun 8:00 AM–6:00 PM; ropeway: 8:00 AM–5:30 PM.Entry / ticketFort ₹25 Indian / ₹300 foreign; ropeway round trip ~₹300–₹350 per adult.
DESTINATION 23 · MAHARASHTRA

Mahabaleshwar & Pratapgad Fort

Satara

At 1,353 meters in the Western Ghats, Mahabaleshwar is known for strawberry farms, Venna Lake boating, Arthur's Seat, and Elephant's Head Point. About 22 km west stands Pratapgad Fort, where Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj defeated Afzal Khan in 1659.

Opening timeViewpoints: Open 24 hours; Pratapgad Fort: Mon–Sun 6:00 AM–6:00 PM.Entry / ticketPratapgad Fort free; Mahabaleshwar municipal green tax ₹20–₹50 per person/vehicle (boating extra).
DESTINATION 24 · MAHARASHTRA

Panchgani & Table Land

Satara

Nestled amidst five Sahyadri hills at 1,293 meters, Panchgani is famed for its pleasant climate and Parsi Point overlooking Dhom Dam. Its centerpiece is Table Land, a vast flat laterite plateau offering panoramic valley views and horse rides.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours daily (horse rides and viewpoints best 7:00 AM–7:00 PM).Entry / ticketPanchgani and Table Land entry free (parking ~₹30–₹50; horse/buggy rides extra).
DESTINATION 25 · MAHARASHTRA

Kaas Plateau & Thoseghar Waterfalls

Satara

About 25 km west of Satara, Kaas Plateau is a UNESCO World Natural Heritage Site known as Maharashtra's Valley of Flowers, with over 850 species of wildflowers during late August to October. Nearby Thoseghar Waterfalls cascade into a wooded gorge.

Opening timeKaas Plateau: seasonal (late August–October), 7:00 AM–6:00 PM; Thoseghar Waterfalls: 8:00 AM–5:00 PM.Entry / ticketKaas online slot ~₹150 per person; Thoseghar entry ~₹20–₹30 per person.
DESTINATION 26 · MAHARASHTRA

Mahalaxmi Temple / Ambabai & Rankala Lake

Kolhapur

Shri Mahalaxmi (Ambabai) Temple is an ancient 7th-century Chalukyan Shakti Peetha housing a four-armed gemstone idol, famous for Kiranotsav when sunrays fall directly on the deity. Nearby stretches Rankala Lake, a historic stone-quarry lake with an evening promenade and Shalini Palace.

Opening timeMahalaxmi Temple: 4:30 AM–10:00 PM; Rankala Lake: Open 24 hours.Entry / ticketBoth temple darshan and lake promenade free (boating extra).
DESTINATION 27 · MAHARASHTRA

Panhala Fort & Jyotiba Temple

Kolhapur

Rising to 3,177 feet about 20 km northwest of Kolhapur, Panhala Fort is famed for Baji Prabhu Deshpande's stand at Pavan Khind and Sajja Kothi. Nearby Jyotiba Temple is a hilltop shrine where Chaitra Purnima draws crowds showering the hill in pink gulal.

Opening timePanhala Fort: 6:00 AM–6:00 PM; Jyotiba Temple: 5:30 AM–9:30 PM.Entry / ticketEntry to both Panhala Fort and Jyotiba Temple free.
DESTINATION 28 · MAHARASHTRA

Tarkarli Beach & Sindhudurg Fort

Malvan, Sindhudurg

Tarkarli Beach is famed for white sands, clear turquoise waters, scuba diving, and snorkeling over coral reefs. Just off the Malvan coast rises Sindhudurg Fort, a 17th-century sea fortress built by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj on Kurte Island.

Opening timeTarkarli Beach: Open 24 hours; Sindhudurg Fort: 8:00 AM–6:00 PM (closed during monsoon).Entry / ticketTarkarli Beach free (scuba ~₹1,000–₹2,500); Sindhudurg Fort ₹25 Indian / ₹300 foreign plus ferry ~₹80–₹100.
DESTINATION 29 · MAHARASHTRA

Ganpatipule Temple & Beach

Ratnagiri

About 25 km from Ratnagiri, Ganpatipule is a pilgrimage and beach town centered around a 400-year-old temple housing a Swayambhu white-sand idol of Lord Ganesha facing west. In front of the temple stretches palm-lined Ganpatipule Beach.

Opening timeTemple: 5:00 AM–9:00 PM; beach: Open 24 hours.Entry / ticketEntry to temple and beach free (water sports and parking extra).
DESTINATION 30 · MAHARASHTRA

Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve

Chandrapur

Spanning 1,727 sq. km of dry deciduous teak and bamboo forests, Tadoba-Andhari is Maharashtra's oldest and largest national park, famed as the Jewel of Vidarbha. It is known for Bengal tigers, leopards, sloth bears, dholes, and marsh crocodiles around Tadoba Lake.

Opening timeBuffer zones open year-round; core zones October 1–June 30 (core closed Tuesdays); safaris ~6:00–10:00 AM and 2:30–6:30 PM.Entry / ticketOnline gypsy safari (permit + vehicle + guide for up to 6 Indians) ~₹4,500–₹7,500+ depending on gate and day.
DESTINATION 31 · MAHARASHTRA

Pench Tiger Reserve

Nagpur

About 70 km north of Nagpur along the Maharashtra–Madhya Pradesh border, Pench preserves rugged Satpura hills, teak forests, and Pench River backwaters. It is home to Bengal tigers, leopards, gaur, wild dogs, and over 250 bird species.

Opening timeSeasonal October 1–June 30 (closed Wednesdays); morning ~6:00–10:00 AM; afternoon ~2:30–6:30 PM.Entry / ticketSafari permit + gypsy + guide ~₹4,000–₹5,500 per vehicle (up to 6 Indian visitors).
DESTINATION 32 · MAHARASHTRA

Deekshabhoomi & Futala Lake

Nagpur

Deekshabhoomi is a sacred Buddhist monument and the world's largest hollow stupa, marking the site where Dr. B.R. Ambedkar embraced Buddhism with hundreds of thousands of followers on October 14, 1956. Nearby Futala Lake is a 200-year-old lake with gardens, fountains, and evening food stalls.

Opening timeDeekshabhoomi: 6:00 AM–9:00 PM; Futala Lake: Open 24 hours.Entry / ticketEntry to both Deekshabhoomi and Futala Lake free.
DESTINATION 33 · MAHARASHTRA

Ramtek Fort Temple

Nagpur

Crowning a wooded hill about 50 km northeast of Nagpur, Ramtek Fort Temple is a historic shrine complex dedicated to Lord Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana, believed to be where Rama rested during exile and where poet Kalidasa composed Meghaduta. It features whitewashed temples and reservoir views.

Opening timeMon–Sun 6:00 AM–8:00 PM.Entry / ticketEntry and darshan free.
DESTINATION 34 · MAHARASHTRA

Lonar Crater Lake & Temple

Buldhana

Lonar Lake is a hyper-velocity meteor impact crater formed roughly 50,000 years ago in Deccan basalt, creating a 1.8-km-wide saline and alkaline lake. Ringed by forest and ancient stone temples such as Daitya Sudan and Kamalja Devi, it is a geological and archaeological wonder.

Opening timeMon–Sun 6:00 AM–6:00 PM.Entry / ticketGeneral crater rim and temple viewing free (nominal forest/guide fees may apply for lake shore access).
DESTINATION 35 · MAHARASHTRA

Tulja Bhavani Temple

Tuljapur, Dharashiv / Osmanabad

Shri Tulja Bhavani Mandir is one of Maharashtra's major Shakti Peethas and the revered Kuldaivat of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, who is believed to have received the Bhavani Sword from the Goddess here. Built in Hemadpanti style, it draws millions during Navratri.

Opening timeMon–Sun 4:00 AM–10:00 PM.Entry / ticketGeneral darshan free (optional Mukh Darshan / VIP passes ~₹200–₹500).
DESTINATION 36 · MAHARASHTRA

Vithoba Temple / Vitthal Rukmini Mandir

Pandharpur, Solapur

On the Chandrabhaga (Bhima) River, Shri Vitthal Rukmini Mandir is the spiritual heart of Maharashtra's Varkari Bhakti movement, housing black-stone idols of Lord Vitthal standing with hands akimbo on a brick, alongside Goddess Rukmini. Millions of Varkaris walk here during Ashadhi Ekadashi.

Opening timeMon–Sun 4:00 AM–11:00 PM (continuous darshan during major Ekadashi festivals).Entry / ticketGeneral darshan free (online time-slot booking; Mukh Darshan passes ~₹100–₹200).
DESTINATION 37 · MAHARASHTRA

Swami Samarth Mandir

Akkalkot, Solapur

Shri Swami Samarth Maharaj Mandir (Vatavruksha Swami Mandir) is built around the sacred banyan tree beneath which the 19th-century saint Swami Samarth meditated and blessed devotees. The temple complex offers free Mahaprasad meals and peaceful prayer halls.

Opening timeMon–Sun 5:00 AM–10:00 PM.Entry / ticketGeneral darshan and Mahaprasad free.
DESTINATION 38 · MAHARASHTRA

Aundha Nagnath Jyotirlinga Temple

Hingoli

This ancient 13th-century Hemadpanti-style temple is revered as one of the twelve Jyotirlingas. Built from intricately carved stone, its distinctive subterranean garbhagriha is reached by descending narrow steps; the temple also holds deep ties to saint Namdev.

Opening timeMon–Sun 5:00 AM–9:00 PM.Entry / ticketGeneral darshan free (special darshan passes around ₹100).
DESTINATION 39 · MAHARASHTRA

Parli Vaijnath Jyotirlinga Temple

Beed

Situated on the slopes of Meru Parvat in Parli, Shri Vaijnath Temple is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas, renovated in the 18th century by Queen Ahilyabai Holkar. Built with massive stone fortifications and wide steps, it is revered as a shrine where devotees may touch the Jyotirlinga during darshan.

Opening timeMon–Sun 5:00 AM–9:30 PM.Entry / ticketGeneral darshan free (VIP/quick-darshan passes ~₹100–₹200).
DESTINATION 40 · MAHARASHTRA

Chikhaldara Hill Station & Melghat Tiger Reserve

Amravati

Perched at 1,118 meters in the Satpura Range, Chikhaldara is Vidarbha's hill station and coffee-growing region, famed for Gavilgad Fort, Bhimkund waterfall, and Hurricane Point. Melghat Tiger Reserve shelters Bengal tigers, leopards, sloth bears, flying squirrels, and forest owlets.

Opening timeChikhaldara: Open 24 hours; Melghat safaris seasonal October 1–June 30, ~6:00–10:00 AM and 2:30–6:30 PM.Entry / ticketChikhaldara viewpoints free; Melghat safari permits + gypsy + guide ~₹3,500–₹4,500 per vehicle (up to 6 Indians).
