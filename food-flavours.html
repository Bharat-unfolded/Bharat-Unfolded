03 · TASTE THE COUNTRY

Food & Flavours of India

There is no single “best” Indian dish—every region has its own favourites. Start with these much-loved classics.

🍛 NORTH INDIA

Chole Bhature · Dal Makhani · Amritsari Kulcha

Rich gravies, breads, tandoori flavours and hearty Punjabi favourites.

🥘 SOUTH INDIA

Masala Dosa · Idli-Sambar · Hyderabadi Biryani

Rice-based dishes, lentils, coconut, spices and distinctive regional biryanis.

🍚 EAST INDIA

Machher Jhol · Litti Chokha · Rasgulla

Bengali fish curry, Bihar's roasted litti with chokha, and famous sweets.

🥙 WEST INDIA

Dhokla · Pav Bhaji · Goan Fish Curry

Gujarati snacks, Mumbai street-food favourites and Konkan coastal flavours.

🌶️ CENTRAL INDIA

Poha-Jalebi · Dal Bafla

Popular breakfast and traditional wheat-and-lentil dishes from Madhya Pradesh and nearby regions.

🥬 NORTHEAST INDIA

Assamese Thali · Momos · Smoked Pork

Distinct local ingredients, fermented flavours, rice dishes and mountain-region specialities.

Food varies by household and region. Ask about ingredients, spice levels, allergens and food-handling practices; choose busy, clean stalls and freshly prepared food.
