Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain language, how the current Bharat Unfolded website handles information.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Information you provide

The current website is a static information site. It does not provide account registration, login, comments or a built-in contact form. If you email us, we receive the email address and message details you choose to send.

Search on this website

The homepage search works in your browser to find state, Union Territory and featured sections. The search text is not submitted to a Bharat Unfolded account or database by this page.

Cookies and analytics

We have not intentionally added a Bharat Unfolded account system, advertising tracker or analytics dashboard to the current static pages. Your browser, hosting provider or third-party services may process technical data as part of delivering web pages.

Third-party services and links

The site may load fonts and images from external providers and link to third-party websites. Those services have their own privacy practices, which we do not control. Please review their policies before using them.

Children

This website is intended as a general information resource and does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when website features change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
