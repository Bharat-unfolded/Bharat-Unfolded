40 Top Tourist Places & Temples in Uttarakhand

Bharat Unfolded · Uttarakhand

Explore Uttarakhand

From the Char Dham and ancient Himalayan shrines to river ghats, alpine meadows, lakes, forests and quiet hill towns — discover the Land of the Gods.

Uttarakhand brings together the four sacred Char Dham shrines, the Panch Kedar temples, Sikh pilgrimage routes, historic Kumaoni temple towns, the Ganga riverfronts of Haridwar and Rishikesh, Himalayan lakes, forests, waterfalls and high-altitude valleys. This guide covers all 40 destinations below. Opening hours, seasonal access, permits and fees are indicative; mountain weather, road conditions, forest rules and temple calendars can change quickly. Check official notices before travelling.

01 · RUDRAPRAYAG, UTTARAKHAND

Kedarnath Temple

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, set at 3,583 metres beside the Mandakini River. Reach it from Gaurikund by a demanding 16–18 km trek; seasonal helicopter, pony and palki services may operate.

LocationRudraprayag, Uttarakhand, IndiaDiscoverHimalayan heritage, nature and local culture

Timings & Entry

Opening hoursSeasonal, usually late April/May–October/November. Common darshan: 4:00 AM–3:00 PM and 5:00 PM–9:00 PM.
Entry / ticketDarshan is free. Char Dham registration is required; transport and special pujas cost extra.

Hours, permits and prices are indicative and may change with season, weather, festivals and local administration. Check official notices and route conditions before travelling.
