Virat Ramayan Mandir: 33-Foot Shivling Installed in East Champaran

At Kaithwalia (Kaithawalia/Bahuara), near the Kesariya–Chakia road, the Mahavir Mandir Trust is developing the Virat Ramayan Mandir complex along the Ram Janaki Marg. In January 2026, a 33-foot monolithic black-granite Shivling, weighing around 200–210 tonnes and carved near Mahabalipuram, Tamil Nadu, was installed at the site. Devotees are already visiting to see the Shivling while construction of the wider temple complex continues. Reports from April–May 2026 put completion at around 30% of the contracted earth and civil work.
