7 Top Tourist Places in Punjab

Explore Punjab's sacred Sikh heritage, freedom-movement memorials, historic forts and the unforgettable energy of the India–Pakistan border.

7 destinations · Visitor timings · Entry information

DESTINATION 01 · PUNJAB

Sri Harmandir Sahib / Golden Temple

Amritsar

Sri Harmandir Sahib, popularly known as the Golden Temple, is the spiritual center of Sikhism. Set beside the sacred Amrit Sarovar, the shrine features white marble walls and gold-leaf cladding. Pilgrims of all backgrounds visit for kirtan, peaceful reflections around the sarovar and community meals served through the langar tradition.

Opening timeMon–Sun: Open 24 hours.
Entry / ticketEntry is free. Head covering and modest conduct are required; langar meals are also free.
DESTINATION 02 · PUNJAB

Jallianwala Bagh

Amritsar

Located right next to Sri Harmandir Sahib, Jallianwala Bagh is a historic memorial garden and flame monument honoring the victims of the April 13, 1919 massacre. Visitors walk through the preserved courtyard, bullet-marked walls, martyr well and exhibition galleries.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:30 AM – 7:30 PM.
Entry / ticketEntry is free.
DESTINATION 03 · PUNJAB

Attari–Wagah Border

Near Amritsar

Situated along the Grand Trunk Road on the India–Pakistan frontier near Amritsar, Attari–Wagah Border is famous for its ceremonial retreat parade and flag-lowering event in the late afternoon. Spectators gather at the viewing stands to watch patriotic chants and border guard drills. Ceremony timings vary seasonally.

Opening timeListing hours: Mon–Sat 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM; Sunday listed as closed. Retreat ceremony timings vary seasonally—arrive early with valid ID.
Entry / ticketEntry to the border viewing stands is free.
DESTINATION 04 · PUNJAB

Shri Durgiana Mandir

Amritsar · Hathi Gate

Located near Hathi Gate in Amritsar, Shri Durgiana Mandir is a prominent Hindu temple set within a sacred water tank, featuring a gilded dome, silver-plated doors and shrines dedicated to Goddess Durga, Lord Vishnu and Lord Hanuman. Its lakeside layout and carved halls draw steady pilgrims daily.

Opening timeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM.
Entry / ticketGeneral darshan entry is free.
DESTINATION 05 · PUNJAB

Partition Museum

Town Hall · Amritsar

Situated near Town Hall in Amritsar, the Partition Museum documents the 1947 Partition of India and Pakistan through archival photographs, personal belongings, oral histories, newspapers and themed galleries. It offers a moving look at human displacement, resilience and shared cultural roots.

Opening timeTue–Sun: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM; closed Mondays.
Entry / ticketPaid admission applies (around ₹10–₹20 for Indian citizens and ₹250 for foreign visitors; confirm current counter rate on arrival).
DESTINATION 06 · PUNJAB

Qila Mubarak

Bathinda

Standing in the old city of Bathinda, Qila Mubarak is a historic brick-and-mortar fort with thick ramparts, bastions and courtyards tied to centuries of Punjab and northern Indian history, including traditions linked to Razia Sultan and Guru Gobind Singh Ji.

Opening timeExact daily hours are not listed; commonly visited during daytime hours.
Entry / ticketEntry is generally free or nominal (verify locally at the monument entrance).
DESTINATION 07 · PUNJAB

Takhat Sri Kesgarh Sahib

Anandpur Sahib

Located in Anandpur Sahib, Takhat Sri Kesgarh Sahib is one of the five Takhts (seats of temporal authority) of Sikhism and the historic birthplace of the Khalsa founded by Guru Gobind Singh Ji in 1699. Set on a hilltop amidst the Shivalik foothills, it houses sacred weapons and hosts major Hola Mohalla gatherings.

Opening timeMon–Sun: Open 24 hours.
Entry / ticketEntry and langar are free.
