35 Top Tourist Places & Temples in Himachal Pradesh

Discover mountain valleys, ancient temples, high-altitude lakes, monasteries and scenic treks across Himachal Pradesh.

DESTINATION 01 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Hadimba Devi Temple

Manali, Kullu

A distinctive 16th-century pagoda-style wooden shrine built in 1553 by Maharaja Bahadur Singh, dedicated to Hidimba Devi of the Mahabharata. Its carved wooden doorway, four-tiered roof and sacred natural rock sit among cedar trees in Dhungri Forest.

Opening time8:00 AM–6:00 PMEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 02 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Solang Valley

Manali, Kullu

A Himalayan adventure valley about 14 km northwest of Manali. Winter brings skiing, snowboarding and snowmobiling; warmer months offer paragliding, zorbing, ATV rides and gondola trips towards Mount Phatru.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; activities usually 9:00 AM–5:00 PMEntry / ticketValley free; gondola about ₹600–₹750 round-trip; activities extra
DESTINATION 03 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Rohtang Pass

Kullu / Lahaul & Spiti

At 3,978 metres on the Pir Panjal Range, Rohtang Pass links Kullu with Lahaul and Spiti. It is known for glaciers, snow walls and wide Himalayan views; seasonal access requires an official permit.

Opening timeSeasonal, usually May–November; daytime permit hoursEntry / ticketPermit ₹500 + ₹50 congestion fee per vehicle; transport extra
DESTINATION 04 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Atal Tunnel & Sissu Valley

Kullu / Lahaul

The 9.02-km Atal Tunnel runs beneath Rohtang and provides all-weather road access to Lahaul when conditions permit. Beyond its north portal, Sissu is known for its lake, waterfall and snow-framed peaks.

Opening timeTunnel open 24 hours subject to weather; Sissu best by dayEntry / ticketFree; optional lake activities extra
DESTINATION 05 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Kullu Valley & Bijli Mahadev Temple

Kullu

Kullu Valley follows the Beas River and is known for orchards, rafting and Dussehra. Bijli Mahadev Temple stands on a ridge above the valley and is reached by a scenic trek; its tall wooden staff is associated with lightning.

Opening timeTemple 6:00 AM–7:00 PM; trek by dayEntry / ticketTemple and trek free; rafting about ₹500–₹1,200+
DESTINATION 06 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Manikaran Sahib & Kasol

Parvati Valley, Kullu

Kasol is a riverside village on the Parvati, known for pine trails, cafés and routes towards Kheerganga and Tosh. Nearby Manikaran Sahib is a Sikh and Hindu pilgrimage centre with geothermal hot springs and community langar.

Opening timeGurudwara open 24 hours; Kasol village open 24 hoursEntry / ticketFree entry, baths and langar
DESTINATION 07 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Great Himalayan National Park

Kullu

This UNESCO World Heritage Site protects Western Himalayan forests, alpine meadows and wildlife including blue sheep, Himalayan tahr and snow leopards. Visitors explore it through guided and multi-day eco-treks.

Opening timeHigh-altitude treks generally April–November; 6:00 AM–5:00 PMEntry / ticketPermit about ₹100/day Indian visitors; ₹400/day foreign visitors; extras apply
DESTINATION 08 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Jibhi & Jalori Pass / Serolsar Lake

Tirthan Valley, Kullu

Jibhi is a quiet hamlet of traditional wooden cottages, cedar forests and a waterfall. Jalori Pass rises above the valley and leads to a forest trail to Serolsar Lake and Buddhi Nagin Temple.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; pass may close in heavy winter snowEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 09 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

The Ridge & Mall Road

Shimla

The Ridge is Shimla’s open promenade, with Himalayan views and colonial landmarks including Christ Church and Gaiety Theatre. Mall Road below is a pedestrian shopping and dining street with Victorian-era architecture.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; shops usually 10:00 AM–9:30 PMEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 10 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Jakhoo Temple

Shimla

An ancient Hanuman shrine crowns Jakhoo Hill, Shimla’s highest point at 2,455 metres. A 108-foot Hanuman statue overlooks the town; visitors can walk up, take a taxi or use the ropeway.

Opening timeTemple 5:00 AM–12:00 PM, 4:00–9:00 PM; ropeway 9:30 AM–6:30 PMEntry / ticketTemple free; ropeway about ₹450–₹550 round-trip
DESTINATION 11 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Kufri & Mashobra

Shimla

Kufri, around 15 km from Shimla, is known for winter snow, Mahasu Peak and the Himalayan Nature Park. Nearby Mashobra offers a quieter setting of apple orchards, cedar trails and colonial estates.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; Nature Park Tue–Sun 10:00 AM–4:00 PM, closed MondayEntry / ticketViewpoints free; park about ₹20–₹30 for adults; activities extra
DESTINATION 12 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Narkanda & Hatu Peak

Shimla

Narkanda is known for apple orchards, skiing slopes and deodar forests. A mountain road leads to Hatu Peak at about 3,400 metres, where Hatu Mata Temple offers wide views of the surrounding ranges.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; daytime travel to Hatu Peak recommendedEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 13 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Chail Palace & Kali Ka Tibba

Solan

Chail Palace is a 19th-century summer retreat built by Maharaja Bhupinder Singh of Patiala, set among deodar and pine forests. Kali Ka Tibba is a hilltop temple with broad views towards Choor Chandni and the Shivalik hills.

Opening timePalace grounds 9:00 AM–6:00 PM; Kali Ka Tibba 6:00 AM–7:00 PMEntry / ticketPalace about ₹100; Kali Ka Tibba free
DESTINATION 14 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Kasauli & Manki Point

Solan

Founded as a British cantonment in 1842, Kasauli has pine-lined paths, Victorian cottages and Christ Church. Manki Point, inside an Air Force station, has a Hanuman temple and views towards the Sutlej and Chandigarh plains.

Opening timeTown open 24 hours; Manki Point 9:00 AM–5:00 PMEntry / ticketTown walks free; Manki Point about ₹20–₹30; valid ID required
DESTINATION 15 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

McLeod Ganj & Dalai Lama Temple

Dharamshala, Kangra

Known as Little Lhasa, McLeod Ganj is home to the Tsuglagkhang Complex, the Dalai Lama’s temple complex. It includes Buddha images, prayer wheels, Namgyal Monastery and the Tibet Museum.

Opening timeTemple complex 5:00 AM–8:00 PM; Tibet Museum Tue–Sun 9:00 AM–5:00 PM, closed MondayEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 16 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Bhagsunag Temple & Bhagsu Waterfall

McLeod Ganj, Kangra

Bhagsunag is an old stone Shiva temple with sacred pools. A short uphill trail behind it reaches Bhagsu Waterfall, a cascade framed by rocky cliffs and hillside cafés.

Opening timeTemple 5:00 AM–9:00 PM; waterfall 7:00 AM–6:00 PMEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 17 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Triund Trek

McLeod Ganj / Dharamshala, Kangra

This popular ridge trek climbs through oak and rhododendron forest to a grassy meadow at about 2,850 metres, with close views of the Dhauladhar range and Kangra Valley. The route is roughly 9 km from the Dharamkot/Gallu Devi side.

Opening timeUsually March–December; checkpost ascents roughly 7:00 AM–1:00 PMEntry / ticketForest registration about ₹100–₹200
DESTINATION 18 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Kangra Fort & Masroor Rock-Cut Temples

Kangra

Kangra Fort stands on a steep ridge above the Banganga and Manjhi rivers. Around 35 km away, the 8th-century Masroor complex contains monolithic rock-cut shrines carved from a sandstone ridge.

Opening timeBoth generally 9:00 AM–5:30 PMEntry / ticketKangra Fort about ₹150 Indian / ₹300 foreign; Masroor about ₹25 Indian / ₹300 foreign
DESTINATION 19 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Jwala Ji & Kangra Devi Temples

Kangra

Jwala Ji Temple is famed for natural flames emerging from rock crevices. In Kangra town, Brajeshwari Devi Temple is another major Shakti shrine, known for its dome, silver-plated doors and Navratri celebrations.

Opening timeGenerally 5:00 AM–10:00 PM; festival hours may extendEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 20 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Bir Billing

Kangra

Bir Billing is a major paragliding destination. Flights launch from Billing meadow at about 2,400 metres and land at Bir, passing over tea gardens and Tibetan monasteries; Bir also has cafés and Chokling Monastery.

Opening timeParagliding usually October–June, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM; monasteries 7:00 AM–6:00 PMEntry / ticketVillage and monasteries free; tandem flight about ₹2,500–₹3,500
DESTINATION 21 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Khajjiar

Chamba

At 1,920 metres, Khajjiar is an emerald meadow ringed by deodar forest, with a small lake and floating island. The area also has the 12th-century Khajji Nag Temple and is often called Mini Switzerland of India.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; activities usually 9:00 AM–6:00 PMEntry / ticketMeadow and temple free; activities/parking extra
DESTINATION 22 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Dalhousie, Panchpula & Dainkund Peak

Chamba

Dalhousie spreads across five pine-covered hills and retains colonial-era churches and promenades. Nearby Panchpula has a waterfall and memorial, while Dainkund Peak is the area’s highest point and has Pholani Devi Temple.

Opening timeDalhousie and Dainkund open 24 hours; Panchpula 8:00 AM–6:00 PMEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 23 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Chamba Town & Laxmi Narayan Temple

Chamba

Historic Chamba sits beside the Ravi River and is known for Chaugan meadow, Bhuri Singh Museum and Pahari miniature paintings. Its 10th-century Laxmi Narayan complex contains six carved stone temples.

Opening timeTemple 6:00 AM–12:30 PM, 2:30–8:30 PM; museum Tue–Sun 10:00 AM–5:00 PMEntry / ticketTemple free; museum about ₹20–₹50 Indian visitors
DESTINATION 24 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Key Monastery & Kibber Village

Spiti Valley, Lahaul & Spiti

Key Monastery is a historic Tibetan Buddhist monastery on a hill above the Spiti River, known for whitewashed prayer rooms and murals. Kibber is a high-altitude village near Chicham Bridge and winter wildlife-spotting routes.

Opening timeMonastery 6:00 AM–6:00 PM; Kibber open 24 hours, access mainly May–OctoberEntry / ticketFree; donations optional
DESTINATION 25 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Chandratal Lake

Spiti Valley, Lahaul & Spiti

At about 4,300 metres on the Samudra Tapu plateau, Chandratal is a crescent-shaped glacial lake whose water shifts from emerald to turquoise. A rough mountain track and short walk lead to the lake.

Opening timeSeasonal, usually June–early October; visit by dayEntry / ticketLake entry free; designated camping fees apply
DESTINATION 26 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Tabo & Dhankar Monasteries

Spiti Valley, Lahaul & Spiti

Founded in 996 CE, Tabo is famed for ancient murals and sculptures and is often called the Ajanta of the Himalayas. Dhankar Monastery stands dramatically on a cliff above the meeting of the Spiti and Pin rivers.

Opening timeGenerally 6:00 AM–6:00 PM; access usually May–NovemberEntry / ticketFree; donations optional
DESTINATION 27 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Kalpa, Sangla Valley & Chitkul

Kinnaur

Kalpa overlooks the Kinner Kailash massif and has apple orchards and mountain viewpoints. Sangla follows the Baspa River towards Chitkul, a high-altitude village known for wooden temples and Himalayan scenery.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; best access generally April–NovemberEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 28 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Nako Lake & Monastery

Kinnaur

Nako is a high-altitude village near the Spiti border, centred on a lake edged by willow and poplar trees. Its old monastery contains mud-brick prayer halls with Indo-Tibetan paintings and sculptures.

Opening timeLake open 24 hours; monastery 7:00 AM–6:00 PMEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 29 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Chintpurni Temple

Una

Mata Chintpurni Devi Temple is a major Shakti shrine in the Sola Singhi Dhar range. The goddess is worshipped as Chhinnamastika Devi, and the temple draws large crowds during Shravan Ashtami and Navratri fairs.

Opening timeGenerally 4:00 AM–11:00 PM; mela hours varyEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 30 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Naina Devi Temple

Bilaspur

This hilltop Shakti shrine overlooks Gobind Sagar Lake from about 1,100 metres. Pilgrims reach the temple by steps or aerial ropeway, with views across the Sutlej reservoir and Anandpur Sahib plains.

Opening timeGenerally 4:00 AM–10:00 PM; ropeway roughly 7:30 AM–6:30 PMEntry / ticketDarshan and steps free; ropeway about ₹120–₹150 round-trip
DESTINATION 31 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Prashar Lake

Mandi

At 2,730 metres, Prashar Lake is known for a small floating island that drifts across its surface. A 13th-century three-tiered pagoda-style temple dedicated to Sage Prashar stands beside the lake, with views of Himalayan ranges.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; best access April–December; visit by dayEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 32 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Rewalsar Lake

Mandi

Also called Tso Pema, this sacred mountain lake is revered by Buddhists, Hindus and Sikhs. Its shores hold Tibetan monasteries, temples, a historic gurudwara and a 123-foot statue of Padmasambhava.

Opening timeLake promenade 24 hours; shrines generally 6:00 AM–7:00 PMEntry / ticketFree
DESTINATION 33 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Barot Valley

Mandi

Barot is a quiet valley along the Uhl River, developed in connection with the Shanan Hydel Project. Cedar forests, terraced fields, trout farms and Nargu Wildlife Sanctuary make it suited to nature walks and riverside stays.

Opening timeOpen 24 hours; daytime sightseeing recommendedEntry / ticketFree; fishing permits and farm visits may cost extra
DESTINATION 34 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Palampur Tea Gardens & Baijnath Temple

Kangra

Palampur is known for Kangra tea gardens, the Neugal Khad stream and nearby Andretta art village. Baijnath Temple, about 16 km away, is a 13th-century Nagara-style stone shrine dedicated to Shiva.

Opening timeTea gardens by day; Baijnath Temple 6:00 AM–9:00 PMEntry / ticketTemple and public viewpoints free; guided tea tours extra
DESTINATION 35 · HIMACHAL PRADESH

Renuka Lake & Churdhar Peak

Sirmaur

Renuka Lake is Himachal’s largest natural lake and is revered as the embodiment of Goddess Renuka. Churdhar Peak rises to 3,647 metres and has a shrine and statue associated with Shirgul Maharaj.

Opening timeLake open 24 hours; zoo/boating 9:00 AM–5:00 PM; trek seasonal May–NovemberEntry / ticketLake and trek free; zoo, safari and boating nominal fees
