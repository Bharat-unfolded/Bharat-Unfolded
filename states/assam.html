THE LAND OF THE RED RIVER AND BLUE HILLS
Assam brings together the Brahmaputra’s river landscapes, the Ahom kingdom’s heritage, Vaishnavite satras, silk weaving, tea gardens and some of India’s richest wildlife habitats.
