Statue of Unity & Sardar Sarovar Dam (Ekta Nagar, Narmada)

Standing 182 meters tall on Sadhu Bet island facing the massive Sardar Sarovar Dam across the Narmada River in Ekta Nagar (Kevadia), the Statue of Unity is the world's tallest statue, honoring Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. The expansive complex includes a high-altitude viewing gallery inside the statue's chest, a museum, Valley of Flowers, Jungle Safari, and an evening laser light-and-sound show.

Somnath Temple (Prabhas Patan, Gir Somnath)

Situated on the shores of the Arabian Sea at Prabhas Patan near Veraval, Shree Somnath Temple is revered as the first among the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. Reconstructed multiple times through history and rebuilt in its present Chalukya-style stone architecture in 1951, the temple features a towering shikhara, oceanfront promenade, and an evening sound-and-light show.

Dwarkadhish Temple (Dwarka)

Located at the confluence of the Gomti River and the Arabian Sea in the sacred coastal city of Dwarka, Dwarkadhish Temple (Jagat Mandir) is a five-storied limestone shrine supported by 72 pillars, dedicated to Lord Krishna as the King of Dwarka. Forming one of India's four major Char Dham pilgrimage sites, it is famous for its towering 78-meter spire and ceremonial flag-changing rituals (Dhwaja Arohan).

Timings & Tickets

Opening TimeMon–Sun: 6:30 AM – 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 9:30 PM.

06 · GUJARAT

Rann of Kutch & White Rann (Dhordo, Kutch)

Stretching across thousands of square kilometers in Kutch district, the Great Rann of Kutch is one of the world's largest salt deserts, transforming into a surreal expanse of blinding white salt crystals during winter after monsoon waters recede. Centered around Dhordo village during the annual Rann Utsav (November to February), visitors enjoy moonlit salt-flat walks, cultural performances, and camel cart rides.

LocationDhordo, Kutch, Gujarat, IndiaCategoryNature

Timings & Tickets

Opening TimeMon–Sun: 6:00 AM – 8:00 PM (daytime, sunset, and full-moon night permit hours).

Kutch Museum & Aina Mahal / Prag Mahal (Bhuj, Kutch)

Situated in the historic walled city of Bhuj, Kutch Museum (established in 1877) is Gujarat's oldest museum, showcasing Kshatrapa inscriptions, Kutchi embroidery, silverwork, and tribal artifacts. Nearby stand the 18th-century Aina Mahal ("Palace of Mirrors") built by Rao Lakhpatji with Venetian glasswork and the 19th-century Italian-Gothic Prag Mahal featuring a tall clock tower.

Kalo Dungar / Black Hill (Kutch)

Dholavira Harappan Archaeological Site (Kutch)

Located on Khadir Bet island in the Great Rann of Kutch, Dholavira is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the five largest excavated cities of the ancient Indus Valley (Harappan) Civilization (c. 3000–1500 BCE). Celebrated for its sophisticated stone fortifications, geometric town planning, signboard inscription, and world-famous water conservation reservoirs, it includes an informative on-site archaeological museum.

Gir National Park & Sasan Gir (Junagadh / Gir Somnath)

Spanning dry deciduous forests and rugged hills across Junagadh and Gir Somnath districts, Gir National Park and Wildlife Sanctuary is the only natural habitat in the world for the Asiatic Lion. Alongside over 600 lions, the park supports leopards, sambar, chital, marsh crocodiles at Kamleshwar Dam, and over 300 bird species explored via regulated jeep safaris from Sasan Gir.

Girnar Hill & Girnar Temples (Junagadh)

Lothal Archaeological Site (Ahmedabad)

Located about 80 km southwest of Ahmedabad near Saragwala village in Bhal region, Lothal is one of the southernmost and best-preserved port cities of the ancient Indus Valley Civilization (c. 2400 BCE). Nal Sarovar Bird Sanctuary (Ahmedabad / Surendranagar)

Located about 60 km southwest of Ahmedabad across Ahmedabad and Surendranagar districts, Nal Sarovar is Gujarat's largest wetland bird sanctuary, covering a 120 sq. km shallow lake dotted with over 300 small islands. Between November and February, the marshlands host over 200 species of migratory and resident birds, including flamingos, pelicans, spoonbills, cranes, and brahminy ducks, explored via early-morning rowboats.

Polo Forest & Vijaynagar (Sabarkantha)

Nestled at the foothills of the Aravalli Range along the Harnav River in Sabarkantha district (about 150 km from Ahmedabad), Polo Forest is a lush, 400-sq.-km dry mixed deciduous woodland hiding ruins of 10th–15th century Hindu and Jain temples built by Parihar and Idar rulers. Surrounded by hills, streams, and ancient carved shrines like Sharneshwar Temple, it is especially vibrant during and after the monsoon.

Koteshwar Mahadev Temple (Kutch)

Perched on a rocky coastal headland at the westernmost tip of India near Narayan Sarovar in Kutch district, Koteshwar Mahadev Temple overlooks the Arabian Sea where the Kori Creek meets the open ocean. Linked in legend to Ravana receiving a Shiva Lingam from Lord Shiva, the ancient stone shrine offers dramatic views of the sea and lights across the border at night.

Dandi Heritage Memorial (Navsari)

Located along the Arabian Sea coast in Dandi village of Navsari district, the National Salt Satyagraha Memorial commemorates the historic culmination of Mahatma Gandhi's 24-day, 385-km Dandi Salt March on April 6, 1930. Set across a 15-acre landscaped campus, the memorial features life-size bronze sculptures of Gandhi and 80 fellow marchers, a crystal pyramid symbolizing salt, and narrative murals.

LocationNavsari, Gujarat, IndiaCategoryHeritage
